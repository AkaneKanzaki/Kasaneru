## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can run your second full simulation in Real mode: 140 minutes with no breaks, no explanations, with answer-sheet discipline like the real exam. You also know how to apply section thresholds (floors) to your results and how to handle unexpected problems mid-simulation.

## Explanation

**Real mode** = a full N3 simulation under real exam conditions: 140 minutes running with no breaks between sections, opening explanations is forbidden, opening a dictionary or notes is forbidden. One purpose: measuring your ability under conditions like test day.

**140-minute allocation:** もじ・ごい 30 minutes → ぶんぽう + どっかい 70 minutes (30 + 40) → ちょうかい 40 minutes. No rest in between; prepare water and your mindset for the full 140 minutes.

**Floors applied (as estimates).** Practice passing criteria: total ≥95/180 AND each section above the estimated ±19/60 threshold. That means a high total but one failed section = not safe yet. Record your score in the format: total | げんごちしき | どっかい | ちょうかい, then mark which section is below the threshold.

**Emergency protocol.** If you hit a problem mid-simulation — for example going totally blank on one reading text, or missing a listening audio — don't panic and don't replay. Mark it, move to the next question, and save your remaining time.

## Practice

1. What three things are forbidden in Real mode?
2. What is each section's allocation in the 140 minutes? When must you switch from grammar to reading?
3. Your score: total 102/180, げんごちしき 34/60, どっかい 40/60, ちょうかい 28/60. Does it meet the practice criteria? Explain.
4. In the middle of listening you missed one dialog. What should you do?
5. Why is "one failed section" dangerous even when the total is high?

### Answer key

1. Forbidden: breaks between sections, opening explanations, opening a dictionary or notes.
2. もじ・ごい 30', ぶんぽう + どっかい 70' (30+40), ちょうかい 40'. Switch from grammar to reading when the 30 minutes are up, no bargaining.
3. Yes — total ≥95 and each section above the estimated ±19/60 threshold. But the ちょうかい margin is thin (28), so the next priority is listening.
4. Mark it, move to the next dialog, don't replay your memory. One lost dialog must not ruin the rest.
5. Because the passing criteria use per-section thresholds: one section below the threshold means not passing, even with a high total.

## Tips / key patterns

- Do Real mode at the same hour as your real exam schedule so your body rhythm gets used to it.
- Prepare your もちもの like it's test day: pencils, eraser, watch, drinking water. Small habits reduce surprises.
- Don't judge yourself while working — judge after the 140 minutes are done.
