## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use four concession and regret patterns: 〜とはいえ, 〜とはいうものの, 〜ものの, and 〜ものを. Main target: don't confuse ものの ("even though") with ものを (regret, "if only ...").

## Explanation

Four patterns — memorize this contrast table:

| Pattern | Meaning | Nuance |
| 〜とはいえ | even though ... | light concession: "they say ..., but" |
| 〜とはいうものの | even though it's said ... | more formal than とはいえ |
| 〜ものの | even though ... (but) | fact A is true, but there's a contrasting B |
| 〜ものを | if only ... (what a shame) | REGRET: if only ... |

**WATCH OUT:** ものの (even though) vs ものを (regret). They differ by just one kana (の vs を), but the meanings point in opposite directions — a JLPT favorite trap.

Examples: はるとはいえ; 安いとはいうものの; 安いものの; もっと 勉強しておけばよかったものを (regret).

〜ものを always carries a complaining tone, often following the 〜ばよかった form.

The seven kanji in this lesson: 漆 (うるし = lacquer), 藍 (あい = indigo), 藤 (ふじ = wisteria), 桃 (もも = peach), 棟 (むね = roof ridge), 軒 (のき = eaves), 塀 (へい = wall).

## Example sentences

1. 4月とはいえ、朝晩は まだ 寒いです。(Even though it's April, mornings and evenings are still cold.)
2. 子どもとはいえ、約束は 約束です。(Even though they're children, a promise is a promise.)
3. 安いとはいうものの、ひんしつは わるくありません。(Even though they say it's cheap, the quality isn't bad.)
4. この カメラは 安いものの、使いにくいです。(This camera is cheap, but it's hard to use.)
5. もっと 早く じゅんびしておけばよかったものを。(If only I'd prepared earlier.)
6. 気をつけろと いったものを、かれは 聞きませんでした。(I told him "be careful", but he wouldn't listen.)
7. はるとはいえ、山の うえは まだ 雪が 残っています。(Even though it's spring, there's still snow on the mountain.)
8. りゅうがくすると きめたものの、やはり ふあんでした。(Even though I'd decided to study abroad, I was still anxious.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Main trap: ものの = concession, ものを = regret. One kana changes the whole meaning.
- 〜ものを carries a complaining tone; the sentence often trails off.
- 〜とはいえ and 〜とはいうものの can't be used for regret — that's 〜ものを's territory.
- All four patterns are more formal than けど; good for essays and JLPT texts.

## Summary

- 〜とはいえ = light concession: はるとはいえ, 子どもとはいえ.
- 〜とはいうものの = the formal version of とはいえ.
- 〜ものの = "even though ..., but ...": 安いものの.
- 〜ものを = regret, "if only ...": 〜ばよかったものを.
- Main trap: ものの (concession) vs ものを (regret).
- Kanji: 漆 (うるし), 藍 (あいいろ), 藤 (ふじ), 桃 (もも), 棟 (むね), 軒 (のきさき), 塀 (へい).
