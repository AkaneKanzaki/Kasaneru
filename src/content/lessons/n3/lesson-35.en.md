## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜とおす and 〜かける without misreading them in N3 questions.

## Explanation

### 1. 〜とおす — carrying through to the end / piercing through

Pattern: どうしますけい + とおす. Nuances: (a) staying consistent to the end, (b) penetrating through.

- (a) つらぬきとおす (to hold firm to the end), はしりとおす (to keep running to the end).
- (b) みとおす (to see through), つきとおす (to pierce to the end).

### 2. 〜かける — starting & unfinished / directing an action

Pattern: どうしますけい + かける. Meanings: (a) started but not finished, (b) an action directed at someone.

- (a) かきかける (half-written), たべかける (half-eaten), 降りかける (starting to fall).
- (b) 話しかける (to address / talk to).

Don't confuse with 〜かけ: かきかけの手紙 is a noun, かきかける is a verb.

The nine kanji in this lesson are themed around weather and geography: 露 (つゆ = dew), 霜 (しも = frost), 彗 (すい = comet, as in 彗星すいせい), 瀬 (せ = rapids), 洲 (す = sandbar), 沖 (おき = open sea), 崎 (さき = cape, as in ながさき), 嵐 (あらし = storm), 颯 (さつ = gentle breeze, as in さっそう).

## Example sentences

1. かれはさいごまでじぶんのいけんをつらぬきとおした。(He held his opinion firm to the end.)
2. 霧のむこうをみとおすことはできなかった。(I couldn't see through the fog.)
3. しらない人に話しかけられた。(A stranger talked to me.)
4. かきかけの手紙がつくえのうえにあった。(A half-written letter was on the desk.)
5. 雨が降りかけてきたので、いそいで帰った。(Rain started falling, so I hurried home.)
6. かれはしんねんをつらぬきとおしてけんきゅうをつづけた。(He kept researching while holding his conviction firm.)
7. たべかけのなしをおいていった。(The half-eaten pear was left behind.)
8. かれは嵐の中をはしりとおした。(He kept running through the storm.)

## Practice

1. Change to the 〜とおす pattern: みる → ______
2. Change to the 〜かける pattern: はなす → ______
3. Choose the correct meaning of 「話しかける」: a. to start speaking (unfinished) b. to address / talk to c. to speak to the end
4. Choose the correct meaning of 「降りかける」: a. to start falling (rain) b. to fall completely c. to pierce through the rain
5. What's the difference between 「かきかけの手紙」 and 「手紙をかきかける」? Answer in English.
6. Translate into Japanese: "He held his opinion firm to the end."
7. Fill in with the correct pattern: しらない人に____。(はなす)
8. Which use of 〜とおす is WRONG?
a. 霧をみとおす b. いけんをつらぬきとおす c. ごはんをつうす

### Answer key

1. みとおす
2. はなしかける
3. b
4. a
5. かきかけの手紙 = a noun ("a half-written letter"); 手紙をかきかける = a verb ("to start writing a letter").
6. かれはいけんをつらぬきとおした。
7. 話しかけられた / 話しかけた
8. c（つうす is not a valid 〜とおす pattern）

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: mixing up 〜かけ and 〜かける. 〜かけ forms a noun (よみかけの本); 〜かける stays a verb (本をよみかける).
- The (a) "consistent" and (b) "piercing" meanings of 〜とおす — determine from the sentence context.
- 話しかける marks its target with に (人に話しかける).
- Potential forms: みとおせる (to be able to see through), はなしかけられる.

## Summary

- 〜とおす = carrying through to the end (つらぬきとおす) or piercing through (みとおす).
- 〜かける = started & unfinished (かきかける) or directing an action (話しかける).
- Unlike 〜かけ: 〜かけ is a noun, 〜かける is a verb.
- Kanji: 露 霜 彗 瀬 洲 沖 崎 嵐 颯.
