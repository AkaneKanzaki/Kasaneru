## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can practice listening with the play-once protocol at full N3 speed: reading the questions in 20 seconds, noting keywords while the audio plays, and staying focused for a 35–40-minute block like the real listening section.

## Explanation

The N3 play-once protocol (use the audio feature in the app — the audio plays only once, with no repeats):

1. **Read the questions and options in the 20 seconds before the audio plays.** At N3, speakers' sentences are longer, so you must already know what information to look for before the first sound comes out.
2. **Note the 4Ks while listening**: when (time/date), where (place), numbers (price, time, quantity), and the decision (what was finally decided). Write briefly; abbreviations are fine.
3. **Listen until the last sentence.** At N3, decisions or plan changes often come at the end — that's what's usually asked.
4. **Answer immediately after the audio ends, then move on.** Don't keep replaying your memory. Train stamina for 35–40 minutes nonstop, like the real N3 listening section's duration.

Three habits to avoid: translating in your head while listening, panicking when there's one unknown word, and stopping your notes because you feel you've memorized it.

## Example sentences

Paste the dialogs below into the app's audio feature, listen ONCE at full speed, note the 4Ks, then check against the key.

**Scenario 1 (work meeting).** Two coworkers discuss postponing a product launch event.

> A: 来週のしんせいひんのはっぴょうかいだけど、かいじょうの予約がとれなくて、さらいしゅうの火曜日にえんきしない？
> B: 火曜日はぶちょうがしゅっちょうだよ。水曜日はどう？
> A: 水曜日のごご2時なら、かいじょうもあいている。さんかしゃは50人ぐらいだね。
> B: 分かった。じゃ、水曜日のごご2時、3かいのかいぎしつね。しりょうは月曜日までにおくるよ。
> What to note: the final day and time, the place, the number of participants.

**Scenario 2 (campus announcement).** Listen to the announcement and note its gist.

> おしらせします。来年度の奨学金のしんせいしめきりは、今げつまつから来月の10日にへんこうになりました。しんせいしょは学生課のまどぐちでうけとってください。また、ちゅうしゃじょうをりようする学生は、うんてんめんきょのコピーをていしゅつしてください。いじょうです。
> What to note: the new deadline, where to pick up the form, the parking requirement.

The correct 4K notes for scenario 1: when = さらいしゅうの水曜日ごご2時, where = 3かいのかいぎしつ, numbers = 50人, decision = 来週からさらいしゅうの水曜日にえんき. Note: 火曜日 is mentioned then cancelled because ぶちょうしゅっちょう — the final decision is Wednesday.

### Answer key

1. Read the questions and their answer options — because N3 sentences are long, you must know what information to look for before the audio starts.
2. さらいしゅうの水曜日のごご2時、3かいのかいぎしつ. 火曜日 is cancelled because ぶちょうがしゅっちょう.
3. Deadline: 来月の10日 (postponed from the end of this month); parking requirement: submit a photocopy of うんてんめんきょ. The form is picked up at 学生課のまどぐち.
4. Because the final decision or plan change often appears in the last sentence — that's what's usually asked.
5. Examples: translating in your head while listening; panicking when there's an unknown word; stopping your notes because you feel you've memorized it.

## Tips / key patterns

- Information mentioned at the start then cancelled is a classic distractor — always hold on to the latest version.
- Similar numbers (火曜日 vs 水曜日, げつまつ vs 来月の10日) must be written down while listening; don't rely on memory.
- At N3, one dialog can contain two topics (scenario 2) — note both, don't settle for one.
- If your notes are empty for one dialog, you were listening passively — repeat with a shorter dialog first.

## Summary

- Play-once protocol: read the questions first (20 seconds), note the 4Ks, listen to the end, answer immediately.
- 4Ks = when, where, numbers, decision.
- Stamina = 35–40 minutes nonstop, full N3 speed.
- Avoid: translating in your head, panicking over unknown words, stopping your notes.
- Kanji checklist (strokes, read without hesitation): 遂（遂に・ついに）・克（こくふくする・こくふくする）・潜（せんざいてきな・せんざいてきな）・避（避ける・さける）・免（うんてんめんきょ・うんてんめんきょ）。
