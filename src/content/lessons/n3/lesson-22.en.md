## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜ぶる to express "pretending" or "acting as if", and 〜めく to express "starting to show signs of". You can also tell the two apart and conjugate them correctly.

## Explanation

**1. 〜ぶる — "acting / pretending"**

- Noun + ぶる: がくしゃぶる (acting like a scholar)
- na-adjective: drop な + ぶる: げんきぶる (acting healthy)
- It means pretending: actually not that way, but acting as if it were.
- Its base form is a group 1 verb: ぶる → ぶって (て-form), ぶっています.

**2. 〜めく — "starting to show signs of / taking on a nuance of"**

- Noun + めく: はるめく (starting to feel like spring), なぞめく (mysterious)
- na-adjective: drop な + めく, or color noun: いろめく (starting to turn red)
- It means signs appearing naturally, without intention.
- A group 1 verb: めく → めいて (て-form), めいています.

Contrast:

| Pattern | Meaning | Intentionality | Example |
| 〜ぶる | acting, pretending | intentional | がくしゃぶる |
| 〜めく | starting to show signs | natural | はるめく |

The nine kanji of this lesson: 慕 (したう = to admire), 慨 (がい; in かんがい = deep emotion), 嘆 (なげく = to lament), 愁 (うれい = sorrow), 憂 (うれい; in 憂うつ ゆううつ = gloomy), 哀 (あわれ; in 哀しい かなしい = sorrowful), 歓 (よろこぶ; in かんせい = cheers), 忍 (しのぶ = to restrain oneself), 耐 (たえる = to endure).

## Example sentences

1. かれは がくしゃぶって、むずかしい ことばを つかいます。(He acts like a scholar and uses difficult words.)
2. びょうきでも、シンタさんは げんきぶっています。(Even though she's sick, Sinta is acting healthy.)
3. 3月に なって、ようやく はるめいてきました。(When March came, it finally started feeling like spring.)
4. あの じけんは なぞめいています。(That case is full of mystery.)
5. そらが いろめいて、ゆうひが きれいでした。(The sky started turning red, and the sunset was beautiful.)
6. ブディさんは しったかぶりを します。(Budi is a know-it-all.)
7. みんなは かれの かんせいに はげまされました。(Everyone was encouraged by his cheers.)
8. つらい ときも、かのじょは じっと たえました。(Even in hard times, she endured patiently.)

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜ぶる = pretending (intentional, often negative); 〜めく = naturally appearing signs. Don't mix them up.
- Both are group 1 verbs: ぶる → ぶって, めく → めいて. A wrong て-form is a common JLPT trap.
- 〜ぶる is often used to criticize: がくしゃぶる, しったかぶり. 〜めく is neutral: はるめく, いろめく.

## Summary

- 〜ぶる: acting or pretending (がくしゃぶる, げんきぶる).
- 〜めく: starting to show signs naturally (はるめく, いろめく, なぞめく).
- Conjugation: ぶって, めいて (both group 1).
- Kanji: 慕 したう, 慨 かんがい, 嘆 なげく, 愁 うれい, 憂 ゆううつ, 哀 かなしい, 歓 かんせい, 忍 しのぶ, 耐 たえる.
