## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the pattern 〜しだい for two different meanings: "depending on ..." and "as soon as ...". Both have a formal tone and are often used in announcements, business emails, and JLPT questions.

## Explanation

The pattern 〜しだい has **two meanings** you must distinguish from what comes before it:

1. **Noun + しだい (+ で)** = "depending on ...". 天気しだいで (depending on the weather), 結果しだい (depending on the results), 君しだいだ (it's up to you).
2. **Verb masu-stem + しだい** = "as soon as ...". つきしだい (as soon as you arrive), 分かりしだい (as soon as it's clear).

In this lesson the pattern is always written in hiragana (しだい). The second form is always followed by an action done immediately: わかりしだいれんらくします (as soon as it's clear, I'll contact you) — the gap must not be long; that's the core of its meaning.

Don't confuse it with the adverb しだいに, which means "gradually" — that's a different word even though it sounds similar. And don't equate the first meaning with 〜たら: たら is an ordinary "if", while N + しだい emphasizes that the result **depends** on N.

The seven kanji in this lesson: 柱 (はしら = pillar), 頬 (ほお = cheek), 顎 (あご = chin), 喉 (のど = throat), 拭 (ふく = to wipe), 洒 (しゃれ = joke; おしゃれ = fashionable), 濯 (すすぐ = to rinse; せんたく = laundry).

## Example sentences

1. 旅行に 行くかどうかは、天気しだいです。(Whether or not we travel depends on the weather.)
2. ごうかくできるかどうかは、どりょくしだいです。(Whether you can pass depends on your effort.)
3. わかりしだい、すぐ おしらせします。(As soon as it's clear, I'll let you know right away.)
4. つきしだい、電話してください。(Please call me as soon as you arrive.)
5. じじょうしだいでは、予定を へんこうします。(Depending on the circumstances, the schedule may change.)
6. あなたの へんじしだいで、わたしの 予定が きまります。(My schedule depends on your reply.)
7. けっかしだい、みんなに ほうこくします。(As soon as the results are out, I'll report to everyone.)
8. この 仕事を やるかどうかは、君しだいだ。(Whether or not to do this job is up to you.)

## Tips / key patterns

- The main distinction is in what comes before it: noun + しだい = "depending", verb + しだい = "as soon as ...".
- The "as soon as" pattern is always followed by immediate action. Don't leave a long gap between the two.
- 〜しだい has a formal, business tone; in ordinary conversation just use 〜たら.
- Watch out for しだいに ("gradually") — sounds similar, meaning far different.

## Summary

- Noun + しだい = "depending on ...": 天気しだい, 結果しだい.
- Verb masu-stem + しだい = "as soon as ...": わかりしだい, つきしだい.
- Both have a formal tone; good for announcements and email.
- Don't confuse with しだいに, which means "gradually".
- Kanji: 柱 (はしら), 頬 (ほお), 顎 (あご), 喉 (のど), 拭 (ふく), 洒 (しゃれ), 濯 (すすぐ).
