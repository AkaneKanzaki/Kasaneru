## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can do じょうほうけんさく: finding specific information (dates, times, places, names, items) from notices or schedules under time pressure, without reading everything word by word. No new grammar — this is a pure speed-and-accuracy drill.

## Explanation

### The じょうほうけんさく technique (4 steps)

1. **Read the task/question first (15–20 seconds).** Underline the question words: いつ (when), どこ (where), だれ (who), なに (what).
2. **Look for numbers and names.** Dates, times, phone numbers, and people's names are found fastest at a glance — and are asked about most often.
3. **Scan the document in one pass.** Don't stop at unknown words; guess from context and keep going.
4. **Answer, then verify.** Answer from memory, return to the document only to confirm a number or name.

No new grammar in this lesson. The focus is speed: one document + 3 tasks must be finished in 3 minutes total.

The ten kanji in this lesson are themed around objects and places: 桟 (さん = pier, as in 桟橋さんばし), 埠 (ふ = harbor, as in ふとう), 釘 (くぎ = nail), 鎌 (かま = sickle), 笠 (かさ = woven hat), 籠 (かご = basket), 樽 (たる = barrel), 壺 (つぼ = jar), 鎖 (くさり = chain), 錠 (じょう = lock, as in じょうまえ).

## Example sentences

*Register: formal announcement language (おしらせ).*

> ぼうさいくんれんのおしらせ
> ちょうないかいでは、かきのとおりぼうさいくんれんを行います。
> にちじ：十月二十五日（日）ごぜん九時からしょうごまで
> ばしょ：ちゅうおうこうみんかんとふとうのひろば
> もちもの：ぐんて、のみもの、笠
> ないよう：
> ごぜん九時〜　ひなんけいろのかくにん（桟橋からふとうまで）
> ごぜん十時〜　ぼうさいそうこのてんけん（釘・鎌・鎖・錠のかくにん、樽の水のいれかえ、籠と壺のせいり）
> ごぜん十一時〜　たきだしくんれん
> 雨のばあいは、こうみんかんの中で行います。
> といあわせ：ちょうないかいの山田さん（電話 090-1234-5678）

**Timed task 1 (30 seconds):** くんれんは十月なんにちのなんじからなんじまでか。
**Timed task 2 (30 seconds):** ぼうさいそうこのてんけんでなにをするか、ぜんぶ書きなさい。
**Timed task 3 (45 seconds):** 雨のばあいはどこで行うか。また、といあわせ先はだれか。

### Answer key

1. 十月二十五日（日）、ごぜん九時からしょうごまで。
2. 釘・鎌・鎖・錠のかくにん、樽の水のいれかえ、籠と壺のせいり。
3. こうみんかんの中で行う。といあわせ先はちょうないかいの山田さん（電話 090-1234-5678）。

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: reading the document from top to bottom before looking at the questions. Reverse the order.
- Numbers (にちじ, でんわばんごう) and names (ばしょ, じんめい) are visual anchors — train your eyes to jump to them.
- Watch for doubled information: this document has two ばしょ (こうみんかんとふとうのひろば, and 雨のばあい). Note the conditions.
- N3 target: one announcement document + 3 questions finished in 3 minutes total.

## Summary

- じょうほうけんさく = read the question first, scan for numbers and names, answer, verify.
- Don't translate word by word; find the information asked.
- Watch for doubled information with different conditions (e.g. 雨のばあい).
- Speed comes from repeated drills with a timer.
- Kanji: 桟 埠 釘 鎌 笠 籠 樽 壺 鎖 錠 — objects and places.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Task: find one real announcement (a campus/community activity), set a 3-minute timer, write 3 of your own じょうほうけんさく questions and answer them. SRS: じょうほうけんさく, 桟橋, ふとう, 釘, 鎌, 笠, 籠, 樽, 壺, 鎖.
