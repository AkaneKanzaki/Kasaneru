## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜がてら and 〜かたわら for "while" and "besides", and 〜や否や, 〜が早いか, and 〜なり for "the moment ... immediately ...". All five are formal in tone and often appear in JLPT reading passages.

## Explanation

**1. 〜がてら — "while" (a light bonus)**

- Noun + がてら: さんぽがてら (while taking a stroll), かいものがてら
- The main action is the stroll; stopping by to shop is the bonus.

**2. 〜かたわら — "besides" (main status)**

- Noun + の + かたわら: かいしゃいんのかたわら (besides being a company employee)
- Formal; for a main job or status plus a side activity.

**3. 〜や否や — "the moment ... immediately ..."**

- Verb dictionary form + や否や: いえに つくやいなや (the moment (I) arrived home)
- Formal, written style.

**4. 〜が早いか — same as 〜や否や**

- Verb dictionary form + が早いか: 見るが早いか (the moment (I) saw)

**5. 〜なり — "as soon as ... finished, immediately ..."**

- Verb dictionary form + なり: 帰るなり (as soon as (I) got home), 食べるなり (as soon as (I) finished eating)

The six kanji of this lesson: 契 (ちぎる; in 契約 けいやく = contract), 印 (しるし; in いんしょう = impression), 証 (あかし; in 証明 しょうめい = proof), 頼 (たのむ; in いらい = request), 娯 (たのしむ; in ごらく = entertainment), 婚 (こん; in 結婚 けっこん = marriage).

## Example sentences

1. さんぽがてら、コンビニで ぎゅうにゅうを かいました。(While taking a stroll, I bought milk at the convenience store.)
2. ブディさんは かいしゃいんのかたわら、日本語を おしえています。(Besides being a company employee, Budi teaches Japanese.)
3. いえに つくやいなや、あめが ふりだしました。(The moment I arrived home, it started raining.)
4. かれは わたしを みるがはやいか、にげて いきました。(The moment he saw me, he ran away.)
5. こどもは ばんごはんを たべるなり、ねてしまいました。(As soon as he finished dinner, the child fell asleep.)
6. ははは かいものがてら、ぎんこうに よりました。(While shopping, mother stopped by the bank.)
7. しゃちょうは へやに はいるなり、おおごえで わらいました。(The moment he entered the room, the company president laughed loudly.)
8. シンタさんは しゅふのかたわら、ブログを かいています。(Besides being a housewife, Sinta writes a blog.)

## Tips / key patterns

- がてら = while (a light bonus); かたわら = besides one's main status (formal). Don't mix them up.
- や否や, が早いか, なり all mean "the moment ... immediately ...". なり emphasizes "as soon as finished".
- All three attach to the dictionary form, not the た-form: つくやいなや, not ついたやいなや.

## Summary

- 〜がてら: while (さんぽがてら). 〜かたわら: besides one's main status (かいしゃいんのかたわら).
- 〜や否や and 〜が早いか: the moment ... immediately ... (つくやいなや, 見るが早いか).
- 〜なり: as soon as finished ... immediately ... (食べるなり).
- Kanji: 契 けいやく, 印 いんしょう, 証 しょうめい, 頼 いらい, 娯 ごらく, 婚 けっこん.
