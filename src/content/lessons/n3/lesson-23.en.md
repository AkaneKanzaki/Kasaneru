## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use three quantity-marking suffixes: 〜だらけ (full of, negative), 〜まみれ (covered in), and 〜ずくめ (entirely). You can also choose the right one for each nuance.

## Explanation

**1. 〜だらけ — "full of", always negative**

- Noun + だらけ: まちがいだらけ (full of mistakes), きずだらけ (full of wounds), どろだらけ (covered in mud)
- Used for unwanted things sticking to or filling something.

**2. 〜まみれ — "smeared with / covered in"**

- Noun + まみれ: どろまみれ (smeared with mud), あせまみれ (drenched in sweat)
- Specifically for liquids, dust, or sweat smearing the body.

**3. 〜ずくめ — "entirely / all", neutral or positive**

- Noun + ずくめ: くろずくめ (all black), いいことずくめ (all good things)
- Unlike the two patterns above, this pattern is not negative.

Contrast:

| Pattern | Meaning | Nuance | Example |
| 〜だらけ | full of | negative | まちがいだらけ |
| 〜まみれ | smeared with | negative, liquids/dust | あせまみれ |
| 〜ずくめ | entirely | neutral/positive | いいことずくめ |

The nine kanji of this lesson: 賢 (かしこい = wise), 愚 (おろか = foolish), 敏 (びん; in びんしょう = quick), 頑 (がん; in 頑固 がんこ = stubborn), 丈 (じょう; in じょうぶ = sturdy), 密 (ひみつ = secret), 疎 (うとい = distant), 粗 (あらい = rough), 滑 (すべる = to slip).

## Example sentences

1. テストは まちがいだらけでした。(My test was full of mistakes.)
2. こどもたちは どろだらけで かえってきました。(The kids came home covered in mud.)
3. かれは ころんで、からだが きずだらけに なりました。(He fell, and his body was full of wounds.)
4. サッカーの あとで、ブディさんは あせまみれでした。(After soccer, Budi was drenched in sweat.)
5. はたけ仕事の あと、ははは どろまみれに なりました。(After working in the field, mother was smeared with mud.)
6. 今日は あさから いいことずくめです。(Today has been all good since the morning.)
7. あの 人は いつも くろずくめの ふくを きています。(That person always wears all-black clothes.)
8. せんせいの 話は むずかしい ことばだらけでした。(The teacher's talk was full of difficult words.)

## Tips / key patterns

- だらけ and まみれ are both negative, but まみれ is specifically for things that smear (liquids, dust, sweat). ずくめ is not negative.
- どろだらけ (mud stuck all over) and どろまみれ (body smeared with mud) are close in nuance; both can be used for mud.
- Don't use ずくめ for bad things: avoid わるいことずくめ.

## Summary

- 〜だらけ: full of negative things (まちがいだらけ, きずだらけ).
- 〜まみれ: smeared with liquid or dust (どろまみれ, あせまみれ).
- 〜ずくめ: entirely, neutral or positive (くろずくめ, いいことずくめ).
- Kanji: 賢 かしこい, 愚 おろか, 敏 びんしょう, 頑 がんこ, 丈 じょうぶ, 密 ひみつ, 疎 うとい, 粗 あらい, 滑 すべる.
