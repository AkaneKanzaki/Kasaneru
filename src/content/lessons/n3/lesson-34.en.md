## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜きる ("to completion") and 〜ぬく ("to the end"), and know which verb pairs can naturally be combined.

## Explanation

### 1. 〜きる — doing to completion

Pattern: どうしますけい + きる = "to do until fully finished / used up", stronger than just 〜終わる.

- 読みきる (to read to the end), 使いきる (to use up), 食べきる (to eat up), やりきる (to see through to completion).

### 2. 〜ぬく — enduring / pushing through to the end

Pattern: どうしますけい + ぬく. Nuances: (a) enduring through something hard, (b) penetrating through to the far end.

- 耐えぬく (to endure through), いきぬく (to survive), はしりぬく (to run to the finish), かんがえぬく (to think through fully), かちぬく (to win through to the end).

### Warning: the lexical meaning must be right

Not every verb can be combined. Combinations like 「しにきる」 or 「ねぬく」 are unnatural and never used.

The ten kanji in this lesson: 吠 (ほえる = to bark), 唸 (うなる = to roar), 嗅 (かぐ = to smell), 匠 (しょう = expert, as in きょしょう), 彫 (ほる = to carve), 弦 (げん = string), 笛 (ふえ = flute), 鼓 (つづみ = drum), 雹 (ひょう = hail), 霞 (かすみ = thin mist).

## Example sentences

1. 三百ページの本を一晩で読みきった。(I finished reading a 300-page book in one night.)
2. お金を使いきってしまったので、バスで帰った。(Since I'd used up all my money, I went home by bus.)
3. かれはさいごまではしりぬいた。(He ran all the way to the finish.)
4. きびしいふゆをいきぬいたうさぎははるに子をうんだ。(The rabbit that survived the harsh winter gave birth in spring.)
5. 大きな雹にも耐えぬいた古いいえ。(An old house that endured even heavy hail.)
6. 霞の中をあるきぬけて、やっとちょうじょうについた。(Walking through the thin mist, I finally reached the summit.)
7. 朝までかんがえぬいて、こたえを出しきった。(I thought it through until morning and produced the answer in full.)
8. トーナメントをかちぬいてゆうしょうした。(He won the tournament and took the championship.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: attaching きる/ぬく to just any verb. Memorize the natural pairs: 読みきる・使いきる・食べきる・やりきる and 耐えぬく・いきぬく・はしりぬく・かんがえぬく・かちぬく.
- きる emphasizes "completion/exhaustion", ぬく emphasizes "struggle to the end". Different focus — don't mix them up.

## Summary

- 〜きる = doing to completion / exhaustion (読みきる, 使いきる).
- 〜ぬく = enduring or pushing through to the end (耐えぬく, はしりぬく).
- The lexical meaning must be right: not every verb can be combined.
- Kanji: 吠 唸 嗅 匠 彫 弦 笛 鼓 雹 霞 — sounds, craftsmanship, and weather.
