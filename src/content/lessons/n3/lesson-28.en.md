## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can distinguish 〜向け (aimed at a specific target) from 〜向き (suited to certain characteristics), and use both correctly in sentences.

## Explanation

**1. 〜向け — "aimed at" (an intentional target)**

- Noun + 向け: こどもむけ (for children), がいこくじんむけ (for foreigners)
- Something made or prepared for that target.

**2. 〜向き — "suited to" (matching characteristics)**

- Noun + 向き: こどもむき (suitable for children), しょしんしゃむき (suitable for beginners)
- The nature of the thing suits that person; often used negatively: 〜向きではない.

Contrast:

| Pattern | Meaning | Focus | Example |
| 〜向け | aimed at | intentional target | がいこくじんむけのきょうしつ |
| 〜向き | suited to | matching characteristics | しょしんしゃむきの仕事 |

The six kanji of this lesson: 葬 (ほうむる; in そうしき = funeral ceremony), 嫁 (よめ = daughter-in-law), 婿 (むこ = son-in-law), 妊 (はらむ; in にんしん = pregnancy), 孤 (こ; in こどく = loneliness), 遺 (のこす; in 遺産 いさん = inheritance).

## Example sentences

1. これは こどもむけの えほんです。(This is a picture book for children.)
2. あの ざっしは わかものむけです。(That magazine is aimed at young people.)
3. この 仕事は しょしんしゃむきでは ありません。(This job isn't suitable for beginners.)
4. からい りょうりは こどもむきでは ないですね。(Spicy food really isn't suitable for children.)
5. がいこくじんむけの 日本語きょうしつが ひらかれました。(A Japanese class for foreigners has opened.)
6. この アパートは ひとりぐらしむきです。(This apartment is suitable for someone living alone.)
7. じょうきゅうしゃむけの もんだいしゅうを かいました。(I bought a workbook for advanced learners.)
8. あの えいがは おとなむけですが、こどもむきでは ありません。(That movie is for adults, but it isn't suitable for children.)

## Tips / key patterns

- 向け = intentional target (made for); 向き = matching characteristics. Ask yourself: "made for whom?" → 向け; "suited to whom?" → 向き.
- 向き often appears in the negative form: 〜向きではない.
- Both attach directly to the noun without の: こどもむけ, not こどものむけ.

## Summary

- 〜向け: aimed at a specific target (こどもむけ, がいこくじんむけ).
- 〜向き: suited to certain characteristics (こどもむき, しょしんしゃむき).
- Contrast: がいこくじんむけのきょうしつ (made for foreigners) vs がいこくじんむきのしごと (a job suited to foreigners).
- Kanji: 葬 そうしき, 嫁 よめ, 婿 むこ, 妊 にんしん, 孤 こどく, 遺 いさん.
