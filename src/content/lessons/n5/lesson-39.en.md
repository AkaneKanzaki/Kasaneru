## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can state plans in two ways: ～つもりです for personal intentions, and 予定です for fixed schedules. You'll be able to tell when to use which in conversation.

## Explanation

There are two plan patterns in N5, and the difference matters:

**1. ～つもりです** = personal intention or decision.
Pattern: dictionary-form verb + つもりです.
Negative: ～つもりはありません.

**2. 予定です** = an already-scheduled plan.
Pattern: dictionary-form verb + 予定です, or noun + の + 予定です.
Negative: 予定はありません.

The core difference: つもり comes from your own desire ("I intend to …"), while 予定 already has a schedule ("it's already scheduled …"). Example: 明日休むつもりです (I intend to take the day off — my decision) vs 明日は休みの予定です (tomorrow is scheduled as a day off — already fixed).

## Example sentences

1. 夏に家族とバリへ行くつもりです。
This summer, I intend to go to Bali with my family.
2. 来週、同僚と食事をする予定です。
Next week, I have a scheduled meal with a coworker.
3. 明日は早く起きるつもりです。
Tomorrow I intend to wake up early.
4. 日曜日に友達と映画を見る予定です。
On Sunday I have plans to watch a movie with a friend.
5. 今年は海外旅行をするつもりはありません。
This year I don't intend to travel abroad.
6. 晩ごはんはホテルで食べる予定です。
Dinner is planned at the hotel.
7. 来月、日本語の試験を受けるつもりです。
Next month I intend to take a Japanese exam.

## Practice

1. Choose the correct one: 「来週バリへ＿＿。」(personal intention) a. 行くつもりです b. 行く予定です
2. Choose the correct one: 「会議は明日の3時の＿＿。」 a. つもりです b. 予定です
3. Fill in the blanks: 週末に友達と買い物をする＿＿＿。
4. Arrange into a sentence: [つもりです / 日本語を / 毎日 / 勉強する]
5. Translate into Japanese: "I don't intend to go anywhere tomorrow."
6. Translate into Japanese: "Dinner with the family is scheduled at the hotel."

### Answer key

1. a. 行くつもりです
2. b. 予定です
3. つもりです／予定です (both are possible, depending on context)
4. 毎日日本語を勉強するつもりです。
5. 明日はどこへも行くつもりはありません。
6. 晩ごはんはホテルで家族と食べる予定です。

## Tips / important patterns

- ～つもりです is not used for things outside human control. Wrong: 雨が降るつもりです. For a weather forecast, that's not an intention.
- JLPT questions often ask you to choose つもり vs 予定 from the sentence context: look for clues like "one's own decision" or "already scheduled".
- The negatives differ: つもりはありません vs 予定はありません. Don't mix them up.
- Noun + の + 予定です only works with 予定, not with つもり.

## Summary

- ～つもりです = personal intention (dictionary-form verb + つもりです).
- 予定です = fixed schedule (dictionary-form verb / noun + の + 予定です).
- Negative: ～つもりはありません and 予定はありません.
- Don't use つもり for things outside your control.
- The key to choosing in questions: own intention → つもり, already scheduled → 予定.
