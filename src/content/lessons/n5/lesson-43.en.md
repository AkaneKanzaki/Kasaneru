## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you have a "play-once" listening practice protocol: read the questions first, listen in one pass, jot down keywords, then answer. This simulates real JLPT conditions, where the audio is played exactly once.

## Why listening stamina matters

The N5 Listening section lasts 30 minutes with no breaks. The audio is played exactly once — there is no replay button. Many test-takers fail not because they don't know the vocabulary, but because they panic after missing one sentence and lose focus until the end. Stamina means: staying focused even when some part slips past you.

## The play-once drill protocol

Use the audio/TTS feature in the app to play the practice script below. Don't create or download audio files yourself — just use the voice in the app.

1. Read the question and its choices first (20 seconds). Underline the question words: いつ、どこ、だれ、何.
2. Play the audio exactly ONCE. Don't pause it.
3. While listening, note keywords: numbers, times, places, names, and yes/no answers.
4. Answer immediately after the audio ends. Don't second-guess yourself.
5. Only after answering, replay it to check and study the parts you missed.

## Practice script example

Have the app's audio feature read this dialog aloud once, then answer the two questions below.

> 男：すみません、この近くに銀行はありますか。
> 女：ええ、あの信号を右に曲がると、左にありますよ。
> 男：銀行の名前は何ですか。
> 女：ジャカルタ銀行です。午前9時から午後3時までです。

Question 1: 銀行はどこにありますか。
a. 信号を左に曲がって、右にある
b. 信号を右に曲がって、左にある
c. 駅の前にある

Question 2: 銀行は何時まで開いていますか。
a. 午前9時まで
b. 午後3時まで
c. 午後9時まで

### Answer key

1. b. 信号を右に曲がって、左にある
2. b. 午後3時まで

## Keyword note-taking strategy

- Numbers and times are always noted first: 9時、3時、右、左.
- The question word determines the target: どこ → note places; いつ → note times; だれ → note names/people.
- For yes/no questions, just write ○ or × as soon as you hear the answer.
- Don't write full sentences — abbreviations only, e.g. 「銀・右→左・3時まで」.

## Tips / key patterns

- If you miss one sentence, let it go and focus on the next sentence. Losing one question is better than losing five.
- Options that mention words heard exactly as in the audio are often distractors — listen to the sentence until the end.
- 10 minutes a day of practice with the play-once protocol is more effective than 1 hour of relaxed listening you can replay over and over.
- Get into the habit of reading the answer choices before the audio plays; that is half the work.

## Summary

- JLPT audio is played only once: train yourself under the same conditions.
- Protocol: read the questions → play once → note keywords → answer immediately.
- Note numbers, times, places, and names — not full sentences.
- Missing one part is normal; don't let it ruin your focus for the rest.
- Use the audio/TTS feature in the app for all play-once drills.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Do 3 rounds of the play-once protocol today using practice scripts in the app (or the dialog above). Record how many questions you get right in each round. Add to your SRS deck: 信号、曲がる、午前、午後、開く.
