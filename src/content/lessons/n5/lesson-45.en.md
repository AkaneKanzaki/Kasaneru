## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you know how to run a half mock under timed conditions (the Language Knowledge + Reading block) and how to review your results properly. This is the bridge between loose practice and the full mock in module M10.

## What a half mock is

A half mock = working through one question block (Vocabulary + Grammar + Reading) under a time limit, in one sitting, without a dictionary and without breaks. The Listening section is deliberately separated since it needs its own audio setup. Set aside about 60 minutes total: 45 minutes working + 15 minutes reviewing.

## Time allocation

| Block | Contents | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vocabulary | 10 minutes |
| 2 | Grammar | 15 minutes |
| 3 | Reading (short + medium texts) | 20 minutes |
| — | Total working time | 45 minutes |
| 4 | Reviewing results | 15 minutes |

Use a visible timer. If one block runs out of time, stop and move to the next block — exactly like the rules of the real exam.

## Rules of the game

1. Work in order from block 1 to 3, without going back to an earlier block.
2. No opening dictionaries, notes, or asking anyone.
3. Mark questions you answered hesitantly (circle their numbers). These marks are gold for review.
4. Don't leave answers blank — in the JLPT there is no score deduction for wrong answers.
5. Record the finish time of each block to evaluate your pacing.

## How to review: 3 steps

**Step 1 — Check the key and count your score per block.** Write each block's score, e.g. Vocabulary 12/20, Grammar 10/18, Reading 6/12. Don't look at the total only.

**Step 2 — Classify every mistake.** For each question you got wrong or were unsure about, write: (a) the question type (e.g. particle, ta-form, main idea), (b) why it was wrong (didn't know the vocabulary? tricked by an option? ran out of time?).

**Step 3 — Turn it into action.** Vocabulary mistakes → into your SRS deck today. Grammar pattern mistakes → reopen the lesson (L07–L40). Ran out of time in Reading → repeat the L41–L42 drills.

## After the mock: read the data

- Which block has the lowest score? That is your study priority this week.
- Many "unsure" marks but correct? Your pace is too slow — train your speed.
- Many mistakes in the last 5 questions of each block? That's a sign of running out of time, not of not knowing.

## Summary

- A half mock = Vocabulary + Grammar + Reading, 45 minutes, in one sitting, without a dictionary.
- Mark questions you answered hesitantly — they are the best review material.
- 3-step review: count the score per block, classify the mistakes, turn them into action.
- Don't leave answers blank; there is no penalty for wrong answers.
- Per-block data shows your study priorities, not just the total score.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Schedule one half mock this week using the question bank in the app. Get a timer and follow the rules above exactly. After reviewing, put all vocabulary that caused mistakes into your SRS deck and write one sentence: "This week's priority: ______."
