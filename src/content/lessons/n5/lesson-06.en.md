## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can introduce yourself fully: name, home country, job, and the languages you speak. You can also ask questions with か and soften sentences with ね・よ.

## Explanation

### Pattern 1: の (possessive / connector)

`A の B` — connects two nouns: "B belonging to / related to A".

Example: わたしのなまえ (watashi no namae) — "my name"; インドネシアのともだち (indoneshia no tomodachi) — "a friend from Indonesia".

### Pattern 2: か (question)

Add か at the end of a です sentence to ask a question — no question mark or special intonation needed:

- これはほんですか。(kore wa hon desu ka) — "Is this a book?"

### Pattern 3: ね and よ

- ね (ne) at the end of a sentence = seeking agreement, "…, right": にほんごはおもしろいですね。(nihongo wa omoshiroi desu ne) — "Japanese is interesting, right."
- よ (yo) at the end of a sentence = giving new information with emphasis: わたしはせんせいですよ。(watashi wa sensei desu yo) — "I'm a teacher, you know."

## Example sentences

1. わたしは Sinta です。(watashi wa Sinta desu) — I'm Sinta.
2. わたしはインドネシアじんです。(watashi wa indonesiajin desu) — I am Indonesian.
3. わたしはだいがくせいです。(watashi wa daigakusei desu) — I am a university student.
4. Budi はわたしのともだちです。(Budi wa watashi no tomodachi desu) — Budi is my friend.
5. これはわたしのかばんです。(kore wa watashi no kaban desu) — This is my bag.
6. あれはにほんごのほんですか。(are wa nihongo no hon desu ka) — Is that a Japanese book?
7. にほんごはおもしろいですね。(nihongo wa omoshiroi desu ne) — Japanese is interesting, right.
8. わたしはかいしゃいんですよ。(watashi wa kaishain desu yo) — I'm a company employee, you know.

## Practice

1. Fill in with の or か: わたし___なまえは Sinta です。
2. Turn into a question: これはにほんごのほんです。
3. Fill in: Sinta ___がくせいです___ — "Sinta is a student, right."
   a. は・ね b. の・よ c. は・か
4. Translate: "Are you a university student?"
5. Arrange: です / わたし / インドネシア / の / ともだち
6. Choose the sentence with よ that correctly gives new information:
   a. あしたはにちようびですよ。 b. あしたはにちようびですね。

### Answer key

1. わたしのなまえ (watashi no namae)
2. これはにほんごのほんですか。(kore wa nihongo no hon desu ka)
3. a. Sinta はがくせいですね。
4. あなたはだいがくせいですか。(anata wa daigakusei desu ka)
5. わたしはインドネシアのともだちです。(watashi wa indoneshia no tomodachi desu)
6. a. — よ gives new information.

## Tips / key patterns

- の can be chained: わたしのともだちのくるま — "my friend's car".
- か replaces question intonation on its own; the sentence intonation stays flat and polite.
- Don't use ね constantly — once per sentence is enough.
- The full self-introduction pattern (name–origin–job) is required speaking material for N5.

## Summary

- A の B = "B belonging to / related to A".
- か at the end of a です sentence turns it into a question.
- ね seeks the listener's agreement; よ emphasizes new information.
- The natural introduction order: name → origin → job → languages.
- All examples in this lesson use the polite です・ます forms.
