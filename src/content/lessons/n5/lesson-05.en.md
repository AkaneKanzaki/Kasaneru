## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can read and write the numbers 0–99 in Japanese, ask about and state ages using the 歳 counter, and say phone numbers digit by digit.

## Explanation

### Pattern 1: numbers 0–99

Numbers 1–10: いち, に, さん, し/よん, ご, ろく, しち/なな, はち, きゅう/く, じゅう.

Numbers 11–99 are formed from tens + ones, like in Indonesian:

- じゅういち (juuichi) = 11, にじゅう (nijuu) = 20, さんじゅうさん (sanjuusan) = 33.
- 100 and above use ひゃく (100) and せん (1000), but for N5 it's enough to go up to 99.

Note the numbers with two readings: 4 = よん (more common) or し, 7 = なな (more common) or しち, 9 = きゅう (more common) or く.

### Pattern 2: the 歳 age counter

`number + さい` — for age. Examples: にじゅっさい (20 years old), じゅうきゅうさい (19 years old).

Sound exceptions: 1歳 = いっさい, 8歳 = はっさい, 10歳 = じゅっさい, 20歳 = はたち (special form!).

## Example sentences

1. わたしはにじゅっさいです。(watashi wa nijussai desu) — I am 20 years old.
2. いもうとはじゅうごさいです。(imouto wa juugosai desu) — My younger sister is 15 years old.
3. でんわばんごうは 0812-3456-7890 です。(denwa bangou wa zero hachi ichi ni san yon go roku nana hachi kyuu zero desu) — The phone number is 0812-3456-7890.
4. これはさんぜんえんです。(kore wa sanzen'en desu) — This is 3,000 yen.
5. へやはにかいです。(heya wa nikai desu) — The room is on the second floor.
6. クラスはにじゅうごにんです。(kurasu wa nijuugonin desu) — The class has 25 people.

## Practice

1. Write in hiragana: 42 → ___.
2. 19 is read ___.
   a. じゅうきゅう (juukyuu) b. きゅうじゅう (kyuujuu) c. じゅうく (juuku)
3. "How old?" in Japanese: ___.
4. Translate: "I am 23 years old."
5. Phone numbers are read digit by digit. 090 is read ___.
6. Fill in: わたしは___さいです — "I am 17 years old."

### Answer key

1. よんじゅうに (yonjuuni)
2. a. じゅうきゅう (juukyuu)
3. なんさいですか。(nansai desu ka)
4. わたしはにじゅうさんさいです。(watashi wa nijuusansai desu)
5. ゼロきゅうゼロ (zero kyu zero)
6. じゅうなな (juunana)

## Tips / key patterns

- For ages, use よん・なな・きゅう (not し・しち・く): よんさい, ななさい, きゅうさい.
- 20 years old is はたち, not にじゅっさい — a special form that often appears in the JLPT.
- Phone numbers are always read digit by digit, not as whole numbers.
- じゅう (10) is short; じゅうきゅう (19): don't confuse it with きゅうじゅう (90).

## Summary

- Numbers 11–99 = tens + ones: にじゅうさん = 23.
- Age = number + さい; the exception はたち for 20 years old.
- The numbers 4/7/9 have two readings; for ages use よん・なな・きゅう.
- Phone numbers are read digit by digit.
- なんさい asks someone's age.
