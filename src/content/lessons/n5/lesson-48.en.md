## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you are ready to run your second full mock in Real mode — as close to real exam conditions as possible — and judge for yourself whether you meet the N5 readiness criteria. This is the finish line of this module.

## Checklist before Real mode

Real mode means: 90 minutes in one sitting, no breaks, no dictionary, no replaying the audio. Prepare the conditions the day before:

- [ ] A quiet room, no disturbances for 90 minutes. Tell everyone at home.
- [ ] Device fully charged, notifications off.
- [ ] Prepare paper and a pencil for noting keywords in the Listening section.
- [ ] Get enough sleep the night before — a tired brain lowers your listening score first.
- [ ] Eat enough 1–2 hours before starting.

## Checklist while working

- [ ] 90-minute timer running from the first question.
- [ ] Follow the official allocation: Vocabulary 20 minutes, Grammar + Reading 40 minutes, Listening 30 minutes.
- [ ] Listening audio played exactly once, without pauses.
- [ ] Mark unsure questions, don't linger long on one question.
- [ ] No answer left blank.
- [ ] After finishing, rest 10 minutes before reviewing — reviewing with a cool head is more honest.

## N5 readiness criteria

You are declared ready to take the real N5 exam if ALL of these criteria are met. The score criteria follow the official JLPT passing rules:

1. **Total score at least 80 out of 180.**
2. **Language Knowledge + Reading section at least 38 out of 120.** (In N5, vocabulary, grammar, and reading are combined into one section.)
3. **Listening section at least 19 out of 60.** No section may fall below the bar, even if the total passes.
4. **Two consecutive full mocks passing all the criteria above.** One pass could be luck; two in a row shows stable ability.
5. **No section overrunning its time.** Finishing on time is part of readiness.

Important note: scores in this app are estimates from your correct answers, not official JLPT scores which use statistical scaling. Treat the app's estimate as a practice benchmark — aim a bit above the bar to be safe.

## If you don't pass yet

That's normal — that's what mocks are for. Go back to L47: fill in the analysis table from this Real mode result, find 2 weakness patterns, repair for a week, then repeat Real mode. Most test-takers need 2–3 rounds before passing twice in a row. Don't register for the real exam before criterion 4 is met.

## If you've passed twice in a row

Congratulations — you're technically ready. Next steps:

1. Check the official JLPT test schedule in your city and the registration deadline.
2. In the week before the big day, keep your rhythm: one light half mock (L45), not new patterns.
3. On the big day, apply all strategies from L41 to L46 as usual. No last-minute strategy experiments.

## Summary

- Real mode = 90 minutes in one sitting, no breaks, no dictionary, audio played once.
- Prepare the environment, the device, and your body the day before.
- N5-ready criteria: total ≥ 80/180, Language Knowledge + Reading ≥ 38/120, Listening ≥ 19/60.
- You must pass TWO consecutive full mocks, not just one.
- App scores are estimates; aim a bit above the official bar to be safe.
- Not passing yet → go back to the error clinic (L47), repair, repeat.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Run one full Real mode mock this week following all the checklists above. Score it against the 5 readiness criteria and write your conclusion in one sentence: "I am / am not ready because ______." Whatever the result, you have completed 48 lessons — that's a real achievement.
