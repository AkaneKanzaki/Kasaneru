## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you know how to use Study mode for your first full mock: the time allocation for each section, strategies per section, and when to skip a question. This lesson contains strategy — the mock questions themselves come from the question bank in the app.

## What Study mode is

Study mode is a full mock (all N5 sections) under study conditions: you may pause between sections, see explanations after answering, and are not chased by as strict a timer as the real exam. The goal is not to test your nerves, but to map your ability: which parts are already strong, which parts are leaking.

## Time allocation per section (official N5 rules)

| Section | Official time |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary | 20 minutes |
| Grammar and Reading | 40 minutes |
| Listening | 30 minutes |
| Total | 90 minutes |

In Study mode, use this allocation as a guide, not a punishment. Record your actual time per section to compare.

## Strategies per section

**Vocabulary (20 minutes).** Work from what you know best: kanji-reading questions first, then word-choice questions. No more than 45 seconds per question — if you're stuck, mark it and move on.

**Grammar and Reading (40 minutes).** Split it in half: 20 minutes grammar, 20 minutes reading. In grammar, read each sentence to the end before looking at the options — half of all mistakes happen from rushing. In reading, apply the L41–L42 techniques: read the questions first, look for numbers and keywords.

**Listening (30 minutes).** Apply the play-once protocol from L43: read the questions before the audio plays, note keywords, answer immediately. Watch out for the sound distractors from L44, especially in number and time questions.

## When to skip a question

The practical rule:

1. If within 60 seconds you don't have an answer, mark it and move on. Don't spend 3 minutes on 1 point.
2. Return to marked questions only after all other questions are answered.
3. When you return, reread the question once — often the answer looks obvious with fresh eyes.
4. There is no penalty for wrong answers, so never leave one blank.

## How to use your Study mode results

After you finish, don't just look at the total score. For each section, record: (a) the score, (b) the number of questions marked as uncertain, (c) the question type you got wrong most often. This data is the raw material for L47 (the error clinic). A good Study mode run produces a fix list, not just a number.

## Summary

- Study mode = a full mock for mapping your ability; you may pause between sections.
- Official N5 time benchmarks: Vocabulary 20', Grammar + Reading 40', Listening 30'.
- Split Grammar and Reading into 20 minutes each.
- Skip questions stuck for more than 60 seconds; return at the end; never leave a blank.
- Study mode results feed into the error analysis in the next lesson.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Run one full Study mode mock in the app this week. Record your actual time and per-section score in your notebook. Bring the data to L47. There is no score target at this stage — the target is honest data.
