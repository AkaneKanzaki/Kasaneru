## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can string several activities in order with the ～て、～て pattern. You can describe a morning routine or a trip in one long, tidy sentence.

## Explanation

Sequences of actions use the pattern:

- **V-て、V-て、V-ます**

The last verb determines the sentence's tense. If it ends in ました, the whole sequence happened in the past.

- あさ おきて、かおを あらって、あさごはんを たべました。(In the morning I woke up, washed my face, then had breakfast.)

The order of mention = the order of events. Don't shuffle it.

- シャワーを あびて、ふくを きがえて、がっこうへ いきました。(I took a shower, changed clothes, then went to school.)

This sequence can also be used for light cause-and-effect or for how something is done, but at N5 level focus on the order of activities.

This lesson's three kanji: 座 (すわる = sit), 作 (つくる = make), 使 (つかう = use).

## Example sentences

1. あさ おきて、かおを あらって、あさごはんを たべました。(In the morning I woke up, washed my face, then had breakfast.)
2. シャワーを あびて、ふくを きがえて、がっこうへ いきました。(I took a shower, changed clothes, then went to school.)
3. えきで でんしゃを おりて、バスに のりました。(I got off the train at the station, then got on the bus.)
4. ほんを よんで、メモを とって、ともだちに おくりました。(I read the book, took notes, then sent them to a friend.)
5. かいものを して、りょうりを して、そうじを しました。(I went shopping, cooked, then cleaned.)
6. うみで およいで、すなはまを あるいて、しゃしんを とりました。(I swam in the sea, walked on the sand, then took photos.)
7. しごとが おわって、うちに かえって、テレビを みました。(Work finished, I went home, then watched TV.)
8. ともだちに あって、はなして、ごはんを たべました。(I met a friend, chatted, then ate.)

## Practice

1. Join with ～て: おきます。かおを あらいます。あさごはんを たべます。
2. Join with ～て: シャワーを あびます。ねます。
3. Arrange into a correct sentence: たべました / あらって / おきて / あさごはんを
4. Choose the correct ending: かいものを して、りょうりを (します / しました) — for yesterday.
5. Fill in: えきで でんしゃを ______、バスに のりました (get off).
6. Translate into Japanese: I woke up, took a shower, then went to campus.
7. Translate into Japanese: I went shopping then cooked.
8. Write your after-work routine in one ～て、～て sentence.

## Tips / important patterns

- The order of verbs = the order the events actually happened. Wrong order, wrong meaning.
- Only the last verb determines the tense (ます/ました). Verbs in the middle are always in the て form.
- Common mistake: using ます in the middle of a sequence (おきます、あらいます — wrong for joining).
- In the JLPT, questions often ask you to choose the correct て form to join two activities.

## Summary

- Pattern: V-て、V-て、V-ます — joining actions in order.
- The last verb determines the sentence's tense.
- Mention order matches event order.
- Kanji: 座 (すわる), 作 (つくる), 使 (つかう).
