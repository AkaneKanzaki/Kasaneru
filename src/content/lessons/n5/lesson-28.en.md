## Explanation

To express "but", Japanese uses **が** or **けど** after the plain form of the first sentence.

- が → **formal/written** style, used in essays and written exams.
- けど → **casual/spoken** style, very common in everyday conversation.

Both mean the same thing: contrast or exception. Example: おいしいけど、高いです (it's delicious, but it's expensive). In polite sentences, が/けど attach to the plain form (おいしいが/けど), then the second sentence uses です/ます. This pattern is often used to give opinions gently — mentioning the positive side first, then "but…".

## Example sentences

1. このサテはおいしいですが、ちょっと辛いです。
   - This satay is delicious, but it's a bit spicy.
2. ナシゴレンが好きだけど、あまり作れません。
   - I like nasi goreng, but I can't really make it.
3. 値段は安いけど、量が少ないです。
   - The price is cheap, but the portion is small.
4. コーヒーは苦いですが、毎朝飲みます。
   - Coffee is bitter, but I drink it every morning.
5. シンタさんは料理が上手ですが、お菓子はあまり作りません。
   - Sinta is good at cooking, but she rarely makes sweets.
6. 暑いけど、天気がいいから散歩しましょう。
   - It's hot, but since the weather is nice, let's take a walk.
7. この店は有名ですが、日曜日は休みです。
   - This shop is famous, but it's closed on Sundays.

## Practice

1. Multiple choice: The formal sentence for "cheap but far" is …
   - a. 安いけど、遠いです b. 安いですが、遠いです c. 安いから、遠いです
2. Fill in: 甘い（あまい）＿＿＿、カロリーが高いです。
3. Fill in: 日本語は難しい＿＿＿、おもしろいです。(choose the けど/が that fits casual conversation)
4. Arrange into a correct sentence: です / きれいだけど / 部屋は / せまい → …?
5. Translate into Japanese (formal): "This restaurant is lively, but the service is good."
6. Translate into Indonesian: 酸っぱいけど、体にいいです。
7. Multiple choice: が and けど are placed after …
   - a. the ます form b. the plain form c. the command form

### Answer key

1. b — 安いですが、遠いです
2. けど/が (甘いけど/が)
3. けど (casual conversation)
4. 部屋はきれいだけど、せまいです。
5. このレストランはにぎやかですが、サービスがいいです。
6. It's sour, but it's good for your health.
7. b — plain form

## Tips / important patterns

- In written JLPT exams, choose が for formal sentences; in conversation listening, けど appears far more often.
- Don't use です/ます before が/けど: wrong → おいしいですですが (with です) is not allowed. The correct form: おいしいですが.
- The "positive + が/けど + mild negative" pattern is a polite way to refuse or criticize in Japanese culture.
- Don't confuse this with the が subject particle — here が is a conjunction in the middle of the sentence.

## Summary

- が = but (formal/written); けど = but (casual/spoken).
- Attaches to the plain form of the first sentence.
- Useful for gentle opinions: the good side first, then the contrast.
- Today's kanji: 嫌 (hate; 嫌い), 暑 (hot weather; 暑い).
