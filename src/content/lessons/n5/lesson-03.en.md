## Lesson goals

### Dakuten (゛) — voiced sounds

| が (ga) | ぎ (gi) | ぐ (gu) | げ (ge) | ご (go) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ざ (za) | じ (ji) | ず (zu) | ぜ (ze) | ぞ (zo) |
| だ (da) | ぢ (ji) | づ (zu) | で (de) | ど (do) |
| ば (ba) | び (bi) | ぶ (bu) | べ (be) | ぼ (bo) |

### Handakuten (゜) — semi-voiced sounds

| ぱ (pa) | ぴ (pi) | ぷ (pu) | ぺ (pe) | ぽ (po) |

Dakuten changes K→G, S→Z, T→D, H→B; handakuten changes H→P.

## Tips / key patterns

- The sound ふ ("fu") is misread most often: don't read it as "hu" like in Indonesian.
- The Japanese R is pronounced with the tongue touching the roof of the mouth, between the Indonesian R and L.
- を is read "o" when it is a particle.
- Memorize the dakuten pattern: ka→ga, sa→za, ta→da, ha→ba→pa.

## Summary

- All 46 hiragana: vowels + the K, S, T, N, H, M, Y, R, W rows.
- ん is the only letter without a vowel.
- Dakuten (゛): voiced sounds (ga, za, da, ba). Handakuten (゜): the pa-pi-pu-pe-po sounds.
- Special sounds: ふ = fu, を = o (as a particle).
