## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can recognize and defeat sound distractors in Listening questions: word pairs that sound similar but differ in meaning, deliberately planted as wrong answer options.

## What sound distractors are

In N5 Listening questions, answer options often contain a word that sounds similar to a word in the audio. Classic example: the audio says びょういん (hospital), and the options include びよういん (beauty salon). If you only catch the sound in passing, you will pick the wrong one. The weapon against them: memorize the distractor pairs and listen to each sentence's context until the end.

## Must-memorize distractor pairs

| Pair | Meaning of each |
|---|---|
| びょういん / びよういん | hospital / beauty salon |
| おじさん / おばさん | uncle / aunt |
| 午前 / 午後 | morning (AM) / afternoon–evening (PM) |
| 四日 / 八日 | the 4th / the 8th |
| 来週 / 先週 | next week / last week |
| ここ / そこ / あそこ | here / there / over there |
| 右 / 左 | right / left |
| きのう / きょう / あした | yesterday / today / tomorrow |

## Drill: listen and choose

Have the app's audio/TTS feature read each sentence below aloud exactly once, then pick your answer before looking at the key.

1. 「あした、びょういんへ行きます。」男の人はあしたどこへ行きますか。
a. びょういん b. びよういん
2. 「会議は午後の2時からです。」会議は何時からですか。
a. 午前2時 b. 午後2時
3. 「おばさんが来週来ます。」いつ、だれが来ますか。
a. おじさんが来週来る b. おばさんが来週来る c. おばさんが先週来た
4. 「四日に出発します。」いつ出発しますか。
a. 4日 b. 8日 c. 14日

### Answer key

1. a. びょういん
2. b. 午後2時
3. b. おばさんが来週来る
4. a. 4日

## How to drill on your own

1. Pick one pair from the table, e.g. 午前 / 午後.
2. Have the app's TTS feature read a sentence using one of them aloud, once only.
3. Write which word you heard, then check.
4. Repeat 5 times per pair. Target: 5 out of 5 correct before moving to the next pair.
5. Pairs you still get wrong after 3 sessions go into your SRS deck as special listening cards.

## Tips / key patterns

- Distractors appear most often in number, date, and time questions — the parts that are genuinely hard to catch in passing.
- Don't choose just because it "sounds similar". Listen to the end of the sentence; the context (e.g. へ行きます vs で働きます) often exposes the distractor.
- Distinguish vowel length: おじさん (short) vs おばさん, ビョウ (long) vs ビヨ (short). Train your ears on this long–short difference.
- When torn between two similar options, pick the one whose sentence context makes the most sense.

## Summary

- A sound distractor = an option that sounds similar to a word in the audio but differs in meaning.
- Memorize the 8 distractor pairs in the table, especially numbers, dates, and times.
- Listen to each sentence until the end; context beats sound similarity.
- Self-drill: TTS reads it once, you guess, target 5 out of 5.
- Stubborn pairs go into your SRS deck as listening cards.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Make 4 sentences of your own using distractor pairs (e.g. one with 午前, one with 午後), then have the app's audio feature read them aloud to your study partner. Put the pairs you get wrong most often into your SRS deck.
