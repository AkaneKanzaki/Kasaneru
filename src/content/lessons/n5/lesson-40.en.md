## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can say how often you do something (always, often, sometimes) and how long it lasts (two hours, three times a week). This is important groundwork for talking about habits and routines.

## Explanation

**1. Frequency adverbs** are placed before the verb:

- いつも always
- よく often
- ときどき sometimes
- あまり + negative: rarely
- 全然 + negative: not at all

**2. The frequency counter ～回** (times) with the pattern **period + に + number + 回**:
週に三回 = three times a week. 一日に二回 = twice a day.

**3. Duration** uses 時間 (hours), 分 (minutes), 週間 (weeks):
二時間 = for two hours. 三十分 = for thirty minutes.
Pattern: duration + verb, e.g. 三時間勉強します (study for three hours).

Note: 時間 means "hours of duration", while the time "3 o'clock" uses 時 alone. Don't mix them up.

## Example sentences

1. 私は毎朝ジョギングをします。
I jog every morning.
2. シンタさんは週に三回プールへ行きます。
Sinta goes to the swimming pool three times a week.
3. ブディさんはときどき図書館で勉強します。
Budi sometimes studies at the library.
4. 一日に二時間、日本語を勉強します。
I study Japanese two hours a day.
5. 日曜日に三時間テニスをしました。
On Sunday (I) played tennis for three hours.
6. 私はあまりテレビを見ません。
I rarely watch television.
7. 毎晩三十分本を読みます。
Every night I read a book for thirty minutes.
8. 母はいつも朝早く起きます。
Mom always wakes up very early.

## Practice

1. Choose the correct one: 「私は＿＿テレビを見ません。」(rarely) a. よく b. あまり c. いつも
2. Fill in the blanks: 週に＿＿＿、ジムへ行きます。(twice)
3. Fill in the blanks: ＿＿＿三時間、映画を見ました。(for)
4. Arrange into a sentence: [行きます / 週に / 二回 / 母と / 市場へ]
5. Translate into Japanese: "I always have breakfast at seven o'clock."
6. Translate into Japanese: "Dad sometimes swims in the swimming pool."

2. 二回
3. 三時間

## Tips / important patterns

- あまり and 全然 must be paired with negative forms. Wrong: あまり見ます.
- The ～に～回 pattern often appears in vocabulary questions: 週に何回、日に何回. Memorize the pattern, not just the meaning.
- Distinguish 時 (clock time: 三時) from 時間 (duration: 三時間). One character different, different meaning.
- The natural order: period + に + number + 回 + verb (週に三回行きます).

## Summary

- いつも・よく・ときどき = always, often, sometimes; placed before the verb.
- あまり・全然 always with negative sentences.
- Frequency: period + に + number + 回 (週に三回).
- Duration: 時間・分・週間 (二時間、三十分).
- Don't mix up 時 (clock time) with 時間 (duration in hours).
