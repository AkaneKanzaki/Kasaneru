## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the two N1 patterns 〜も顧みず and 〜をものともせず ("without regard / without letting it stand in the way") to describe determination that brushes aside obstacles. Both often appear in essays, biographies, and news about people who break through hardship.

## Explanation

**1. 〜も顧みず (pattern 1).** Formula: noun + も顧みず. Meaning: "without regard for …" / "without looking back at …". The verb 顧みる (かえりみる) means "to look back / to pay attention to", so its ず form means ignoring it completely.

- 危険も顧みず火の中に飛び込んだ。(He rushed into the fire without regard for the danger.)
- 命も顧みず戦った。(They fought without regard for their lives.)

Standard expressions: 危険も顧みず, 命も顧みず, 危険を顧みず (with を also exists, but も is more common in this N1 pattern).

**2. 〜をものともせず (pattern 2).** Formula: noun + をものともせず. Meaning: "without letting … stand in the way". The word もの here is always written in kana.

- けがをものともせず出場した。(He played in the match without letting his injury stand in the way.)
- 困難をものともせず研究を続けた。(She continued her research without letting the difficulties stand in her way.)

Distinguish the nuance: 〜も顧みず = "ignoring, not looking back" (focus on the attitude of not caring); 〜をものともせず = "undaunted, not treating it as a problem" (focus on the resolve to face obstacles). In many contexts the two are interchangeable, but their standard collocations differ: 命・危険 → も顧みず; 困難・逆境・けが → をものともせず.

## Example sentences

1. 〔報道〕 消防士は危険も顧みず火の中に飛び込んだ。(The firefighter rushed into the fire without regard for the danger.)
2. 〔文語〕 彼は世論の反対も顧みず改革を断行した。(He carried out the reform without regard for public opposition.)
3. 〔報道〕 選手はけがをものともせず試合に出場した。(The athlete played in the match without letting his injury stand in the way.)
4. 〔文語〕 彼女は困難をものともせず研究を続けた。(She continued her research without letting the difficulties stand in her way.)
5. 〔文学〕 兵士は命も顧みず仲間を守った。(The soldier protected his comrades without regard for his own life.)
6. 〔日常〕 彼は周りの反対をものともせず留学を決めた。(He decided to go on 留学 without letting the opposition around him stand in the way.)
7. 〔文語〕 彼女は名誉も地位も顧みず真実を語った。(She spoke the truth without regard for her reputation or her position.)

## Exercises

1. Choose the correct one: 彼は命___戦った。(も顧みず / をものともせず)
2. Fill in with the correct pattern: 彼女は逆境___研究を続けた。
3. Translate into Japanese: "He pressed on without regard for the condemnation." → ______
4. 顧みる is read: (かえりみる / かえりみず).
5. 〜をものともせず means: (treating it as a big obstacle / not treating it as an obstacle).
6. Arrange into a correct sentence: 彼は / 危険 / も顧みず / 助けに / 向かった → ______

1. も顧みず
2. をものともせず
3. 彼は非難も顧みず突き進んだ。
4. かえりみる
5. not treating it as an obstacle
6. 彼は危険も顧みず助けに向かった。

## Tips / key patterns

- Memorize the collocations: 命・危険 → も顧みず; 困難・逆境 → をものともせず. Questions often test these standard collocations.
- The もの in 〜をものともせず is always kana — writing it with the kanji 物 is wrong.
- The ず form is the literary form of ない; don't confuse it with 〜も顧みない, which sounds like ordinary conversation.

## Summary

- 〜も顧みず (noun + も): "without regard for …" — an attitude of ignoring; collocations: 命も顧みず, 危険も顧みず.
- 〜をものともせず (noun + を): "without letting … stand in the way" — resolve in facing obstacles; もの is always kana.
- Nuance: 顧みず = not looking back / not caring; ものともせず = undaunted.
- Kanji: 啜戾鷙斜傾欹曄燿焜煌哄馨馥郁甘酸鹹嫩装髷.
