## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 社交辞令 — formal polite phrases — and choose the right letter closing (結語). You also know the pairing rule by heart: 拝啓↔敬具, 前略↔草々.

## Explanation

**1. 社交辞令.** These aren't empty pleasantries, but mandatory markers of respect in a formal letter. The standard formulas: ご自愛ください (take care of your health), ご健勝をお祈り申し上げます (we pray for your good health), 貴社のご隆盛をお祈り申し上げます (we pray for your company's prosperity). Choose according to the recipient: a person → ご健勝/ご自愛, a company → ご隆盛/ご繁栄.

**2. Letter closings (結語).** The options are graded: 敬具 (standard, pairs with 拝啓), 謹白 (more respectful), 敬白 (respectful), 頓首 (very formal, with a classical flavor), 草々 (for equals or those you're close with, pairs with 前略), 不一 (brief). The golden rule: 頭語 and 結語 must be a pair — 拝啓↔敬具, 前略↔草々. Don't mix them, e.g. 拝啓 with 草々.

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 時節柄、くれぐれもご自愛ください。(Given the season, please take good care of yourself.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 貴社のますますのご隆盛をお祈り申し上げます。(We pray for your company's ever-growing prosperity.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 皆様のご健勝とご多幸をお祈り申し上げます。敬具(We pray for the good health and happiness of you all. Respectfully,)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 まずは取り急ぎご報告まで。草々(For now, this is just a quick report.)
5. 〔日常〕 「お祈り申し上げます」は社交辞令の定番だよ。(お祈り申し上げます is a standard formal polite phrase.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 寒冷の折、皆様にはご清祥のことと存じます。(In this cold season, I trust you are all in good health.)
7. 〔文語〕 結語は頭語と対になるのが原則である。(The principle is that the letter closing pairs with its opening.)

## Tips / key patterns

- 社交辞令 goes in the 末文, right before the 結語 — that is the standard "closing prayer" position.
- Seasonal formulas (時節柄、寒冷の折、酷暑の折) follow the month of writing; the wrong season is immediately noticed.
- When in doubt about which 結語 to pick, use 敬具 — always safe for a standard business letter.

## Summary

- 社交辞令: ご自愛ください、ご健勝/ご隆盛をお祈り申し上げます — mandatory markers of respect in the 末文.
- Graded 結語: 敬具 (standard) → 謹白/敬白 → 頓首 (most formal); 草々 for those you're close with.
- Pairing rule: 拝啓↔敬具, 前略↔草々 — don't mix them.
