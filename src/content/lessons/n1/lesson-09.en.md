## Lesson goals

This lesson consolidates ultra-formal keigo: ご〜いただく (receiving humbly) and お〜申し上げる (conveying humbly). You can distinguish the direction of respect of each and avoid the classic mistake: using them for a superior's actions.

## Explanation

**1. ご〜いただく.** Formula: ご + する-noun + いただく. Meaning: "to receive ... (from you)". Examples: ご説明いただく (to receive an explanation), ご連絡いただく (to receive news). Direction of respect: the giver is elevated, the receiver (me) is humbled.

**2. お〜申し上げる.** Formula: お/ご + noun + 申し上げる. Meaning: "to convey ... (to you)". Examples: お詫び申し上げる (to convey an apology), ご報告申し上げる (to convey a report), お願い申し上げる (to convey a request). Direction of respect: my own action is humbled.

**3. The prohibition.** Both humble your own side, so don't use them for a superior's actions. Wrong: 「先生がご説明いただいた」. Right: 「先生にご説明いただいた」(I received an explanation from the teacher) or 「先生がご説明くださった」(the teacher graciously explained).

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 先日はご説明いただき、ありがとうございました。(Thank you for the explanation the other day.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 ご迷惑をおかけし、お詫び申し上げます。(For this inconvenience, we offer our apology.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 詳細は別紙にてご報告申し上げます。(The details will be reported on the enclosed sheet.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 お力添えをいただき、深く感謝申し上げます。(For your assistance, we thank you deeply.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 ご連絡をいただきましたら、すぐに対応いたします。(Once we receive word from you, we will act immediately.)
6. 〔日常〕 目上の人の行為に「いただく」は使えないよ。(You can't use いただく for a superior's actions.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 何卒よろしくお願い申し上げます。(We humbly ask for your continued support.)

## Exercises

1. Fill in the right one: 先日はご説明___、ありがとうございました。(a. いただき / b. くださり)
2. Translate into English: お詫び申し上げます。
3. Which is correct? (a. 先生がご説明いただいた / b. 先生にご説明いただいた)
4. Change to humble form: 報告します → ______
5. ご〜いただく elevates: (a. the giver / b. the receiver)
6. Translate into English: お力添えをいただき、深く感謝申し上げます。

### Answer key

1. a
2. We offer our apology.
3. b
4. ご報告申し上げます
5. a
6. For your assistance, we thank you deeply.

## Tips / key patterns

- The direction trick: いただく = "I receive" (I am humble), 申し上げる = "I convey" (I am humble). The subject is always your own side.
- The fixed pairings: ご説明・ご連絡・ご理解 + いただく; お詫び・ご報告・お願い + 申し上げる.
- For a superior's actions, use くださる/なさる — don't mix them with these humble patterns.
- Direction drill: every time you write いただく or 申し上げる, first ask "who is acting?" — if the answer is the superior, the pattern is wrong.

## Summary

- ご〜いただく: receiving humbly — ご説明いただく. Elevates the giver.
- お〜申し上げる: conveying humbly — お詫び申し上げる. Humbles your own action.
- Prohibition: don't use them for a superior's actions; the subject is always your own side.
