## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you understand five more core yojijukugo — their register and contexts — without confusing them with volume one.

## Explanation

**大器晩成 (たいきばんせい).** A great vessel takes long to finish: a truly talented person succeeds late in life. For encouraging a late bloomer. Register: formal, warm.

**以心伝心 (いしんでんしん).** From heart to heart: understanding each other without words. For deep relationships — old friends, couples, solid teams. Register: formal, poetic.

**四面楚歌 (しめんそか).** From the tale of a general surrounded, hearing the enemy's songs from all four directions: isolated, opposed by all sides. For political or organizational crises. Register: formal, heavy.

**臨機応変 (りんきおうへん).** Flexibly adapting one's response to the situation. For praising adaptability. Register: business-formal.

**五里霧中 (ごりむちゅう).** Lost in a fog five ri deep: totally confused, directionless. For mental dead ends. Register: literary-formal.

Tricky pairs: 以心伝心 (mutual understanding) vs 四面楚歌 (surrounded); 大器晩成 (late success) vs 五里霧中 (confused).

This lesson's kanji: 御 (ご), 主 (しゅ), 彦 (げん), 騨 (だ, e.g. 飛騨), 賀 (が, e.g. 賀正), 慶 (けい, e.g. 慶事), 応 (おう, e.g. 臨機応変), 疾 (しつ, e.g. 疾風), 風 (ふう, e.g. 疾風), 濤 (とう, e.g. 怒濤), 転 (てん), 捲 (けん), 睥 (へい, e.g. 睥睨), 句 (く), 茨 (し, e.g. 茨城), 栃 (とち, e.g. 栃木), 埼 (さい, e.g. 埼玉), 阜 (ふ, e.g. 岐阜), 滋 (じ, e.g. 滋賀), 媛 (えん, e.g. 愛媛).

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 大器晩成を 信じて、彼は 四十歳で 研究の 道に 入った。(Believing great talent matures late, he entered research at forty.)
2. 〔文学〕 長年 連れ添った 夫婦には、以心伝心の 会話が ある。(A long-married couple has heart-to-heart conversations.)
3. 〔報道〕 社内で 四面楚歌に 追い込まれた 社長が、辞任を 発表した。(Driven into isolation within the company, the president announced his resignation.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 現場では マニュアル通りより 臨機応変の 対応が 求められる。(On the ground, flexible responses are needed rather than just following the manual.)
5. 〔日常〕 資料が 多すぎて、今は 五里霧中の 状態だよ。(There are too many documents; right now I'm completely lost.)
6. 〔文語〕 疾風の 如く 過ぎた 青年期を、怒濤の 日々と 振り返った。(He looked back on his youth, which passed like a gale, as days of raging waves.)
7. 〔報道〕 飛騨の 山間の 村で、賀正の 挨拶が 交わされた。(In a mountain village in Hida, New Year greetings were exchanged.)
8. 〔文語〕 疑心暗鬼に 陥らず、大器晩成を 待つ 余裕が 大切だ。(It matters to have the composure to await late success without falling into blind suspicion.)

## Exercises

1. The yojijukugo for "succeeding late in life": ______.
2. The yojijukugo for "understanding each other without words": ______.
3. Choose the correct one: 敵に 囲まれて (四面楚歌 / 以心伝心) の 状態だ。
4. Choose the correct one: 状況に 合わせて 動ける人を (臨機応変 / 五里霧中) だと 褒める。
5. The pair with opposite meanings: (以心伝心–四面楚歌 / 大器晩成–臨機応変).
6. Translate: "I'm completely lost facing this mountain of documents." (use a yojijukugo) → ______

### Answer key

1. 大器晩成
2. 以心伝心
3. 四面楚歌
4. 臨機応変
5. 以心伝心–四面楚歌
6. この 書類の 山を 前に、五里霧中の 状態だ。

## Tips / key patterns

- Readings: たいきばんせい (not だいきばんせい), いしんでんしん, しめんそか.
- 四面楚歌 is heavy: *isolated and opposed by all sides* — don't use it for ordinary difficulties.
- 臨機応変 is praise in Japan's working world; slip it into essays about work experience.

## Summary

- 大器晩成: great talent, late success — encouragement.
- 以心伝心: understanding without words — deep relationships.
- 四面楚歌: surrounded and isolated — crisis.
- 臨機応変: flexible to the situation — professional praise.
- 五里霧中: totally confused — a dead end.
- Kanji: 賀正, 慶事, 疾風, 怒濤, 睥睨, 飛騨, 茨城, 栃木, 埼玉, 岐阜, 滋賀, 愛媛.
