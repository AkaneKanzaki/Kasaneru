## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can run a timed half-mock for the 言語知識・読解 block (55 minutes, half the question count per section) under exam-like conditions, then review the results with a 3-step method so each simulation truly raises your score.

## Explanation

**Half-mock protocol (55 minutes).** Take half the question count per section from the app's question bank. Proportional allocation from the full 110-minute block: 語彙 8 minutes, 文法 12 minutes, 読解 35 minutes.

**Exam-like conditions.** Timer runs continuously, no dictionary, no breaks, work in order with a separate answer sheet. Timer rings with questions remaining: stop, mark them as "time up".

**3-step review (30 minutes).** (1) Calculate 正答率 per section and record actual vs. allocated time. (2) Classify each 誤答: (a) didn't know the material, (b) got tricked, (c) careless, (d) time up. (3) For the most frequent pattern, write ONE concrete action ("memorize 20 collocations", not "study vocabulary again").

**Schedule.** One half-mock every 3–4 days during M9. Not every day — your analysis needs review time. Watch the score trend, not a one-off number.

The twenty kanji for this lesson (from the N1 outline's 1000-kanji list — themed on buildings and fabric; focus on recognizing readings): 鼻（はな・び）、樋（とい）、竪（たて）、器（うつわ・き）、組（くむ・そ）、真（ま・しん）、杖（つえ・じょう）、檐（のき・えん）、絞（しぼる・こう）、紅（べに・こう）、紬（つむぎ）、茜（あかね）、茎（くき・けい）、題（だい）、発（はつ）、第（だい）、旋（せん）、撰（えらぶ・せん）、仙（せん）、俊（しゅん）.

## Example sentences

1. 〔試験の指示〕 辞書を使ってはいけません。(No dictionaries allowed.)
2. 〔戦略〕 時間配分を守れなかった箇所が弱点だ。(The sections whose allocation blew up are your weaknesses.)
3. 〔記録例〕 語彙8分・文法14分・読解33分、正答率68%。(Vocabulary 8 min, grammar 14 min, reading 33 min, 68% correct.)
4. 〔分析〕 時間切れの誤答が3問ある。(There are 3 wrong answers from running out of time.)
5. 〔行動〕 今週は接続詞の見直しに集中する。(This week, focus on reviewing conjunctions.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 模試の点より、伸び方を見ろ。(Look at the growth trend, not the mock score.)

## Exercises

1. Full block 110 minutes (語彙15+文法25+読解70). The 読解 allocation for a 55-minute half-mock = ______ minutes.
2. The timer rings with 2 questions left. Correct action: a) continue b) stop, mark "time up" c) guess randomly d) restart from the beginning
3. You know both options' meanings but chose the one with the wrong collocation. Error type: a) didn't know the material b) got tricked c) careless d) time up
4. Concrete post-review action: a) study harder b) memorize the 15 near-synonym pairs you just missed c) read the book again d) rest
5. Suggested half-mock frequency in phase M9: a) daily b) every 3–4 days c) once a week d) only once
6. True or false: working without a separate answer sheet is enough as a simulation.

### Answer key

1. 35 minutes (70 × 55/110).
2. b — "time up" data is a finding, not a failure.
3. b — knew the material but got tricked by the distractor.
4. b — specific and executable this week.
5. b — daily doesn't leave room for review.
6. False — filling in the answer sheet is part of the simulation.

## Tips / important patterns

- Record actual time per section from the very first half-mock; without time data, review is just guessing.
- If 正答率 stalls twice in a row, the problem is usually time allocation, not knowledge.

## Summary

- Half-mock: 55 minutes, half the questions per section (語彙 8 + 文法 12 + 読解 35), full exam conditions.
- 3-step review: score & time per section → classify errors → one concrete action per pattern.
- Frequency: every 3–4 days; watch the trend, not a one-off number.
