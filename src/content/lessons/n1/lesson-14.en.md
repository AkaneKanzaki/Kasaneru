## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜を踏まえて to express decisions based on consideration of facts, and 〜を前提に to express something resting on a precondition or assumption. You can also distinguish them, as their meanings are close but their logic runs in opposite directions.

## Explanation

**1. 〜を踏まえて / 〜を踏まえた (based on consideration).** Formula: noun + を踏まえて (adverbial) or を踏まえた (modifying a noun). Derived from 踏まえる (to stand upon). Used when existing facts or data are considered and then made the basis of a decision. The logic runs: facts → decision.

- 調査結果を踏まえて 計画を 立てる。(Based on the survey results, the plan was drawn up.)

**2. 〜を前提に (on the precondition of).** Formula: noun + を前提に (sometimes を前提として). Used when something is assumed or required first, and the action rests upon it. The logic runs: assumption → action. Often followed by a conditional consequence.

- 合意を前提に 交渉する。(Negotiating on the precondition of an agreement.)

Distinguish: 踏まえて stands on facts that have already happened; 前提に rests on an assumed condition. Hence 成功を前提にした計画 (a plan assuming success) sounds critical, whereas 成功例を踏まえた計画 (a plan based on success stories) sounds positive.

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 調査結果を踏まえて、新戦略を 策定した。(Based on the survey results, a new strategy was formulated.)
2. 〔報道〕 被害状況を踏まえた 支援が 始まった。(Aid based on the damage situation has begun.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 合意を前提に 交渉を 進める。(Negotiations were advanced on the precondition of an agreement.)
4. 〔文語〕 成功を前提にした 計画は 脆い。(A plan that assumes certain success is fragile.)
5. 〔報道〕 気魄のある 若者の 声を踏まえて、条例が 見直された。(Based on the voices of spirited young people, the local ordinance was reviewed.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 顧客の 要望を踏まえ、仕様を 変更した。(Taking customer requests into consideration, the specifications were changed.)
7. 〔文語〕 予測を踏まえることと、予測を前提にすることは 異なる。(Considering a prediction differs from making it an assumption.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Remember the logic's direction: 踏まえて looks BACKWARD (existing facts); 前提に looks FORWARD (an assumed condition).
- 〜を踏まえた modifies a noun (a plan based on...), whereas 〜を踏まえて modifies a verb (based on..., then doing...).
- In an argumentative essay, 〜を踏まえて suits the evidence section (data/facts); 〜を前提に suits criticizing the opponent's assumptions.

## Summary

- 〜を踏まえて/〜を踏まえた: standing on considered facts (facts → decision).
- 〜を前提に: resting on a precondition/assumption (assumption → action).
- Quick test: "is the noun a fact or an assumption?" Fact → 踏まえて; assumption → 前提に.
- Kanji: 踏まえる, 前提, 気魄, 斟酌, 勘案.
