## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the N1 pattern 〜とは比べものにならない ("there is no comparison / incomparable") to express a very large difference. This pattern is typical of editorials and news when comparing the past with the present, or two things whose levels are far apart.

## Explanation

**〜とは比べものにならない (the single focus).** Formula: noun + とは比べものにならない. Meaning: "there is no comparison with …" / "incomparable to …" — emphasizing a very large difference, not just a slight one.

- 昔とは比べものにならない。(There is no comparison with the old days.)
- 被害の規模は昨年とは比べものにならない。(The scale of damage is incomparable to last year's.)

Note the particle とは — the sentence is still grammatical without は (〜と比べものにならない), but the form with は is more emphatic and appears more often in exam questions. This pattern is often strengthened with ほど: 昔とは比べものにならないほど便利になった (it has become convenient beyond comparison with the old days).

Compare with 〜に比べれば ("if compared"): 〜に比べれば is neutral, while 〜とは比べものにならない always emphasizes a huge gap. The word 比べもの itself means "something that can be compared" — so the pattern literally means "not something that can be compared".

## Example sentences

1. 〔報道〕 現在の通信速度は、十年前とは比べものにならない。(Today's communication speed is beyond comparison with ten years ago.)
2. 〔文語〕 彼の業績は前任者とは比べものにならない。(His achievements are incomparable to his predecessor's.)
3. 〔日常〕 ジャカルタの渋滞は、昔とは比べものにならないほどひどい。(Jakarta's traffic jams are terribly bad beyond comparison with the old days.)
4. 〔報道〕 被害の規模は昨年の台風とは比べものにならない。(The scale of damage is incomparable to last year's typhoon.)
5. 〔文語〕 この小説の完成度はデビュー作とは比べものにならない。(This novel's level of polish is incomparable to the debut work.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 今期の売上は前期とは比べものにならない。(This term's sales are incomparable to the previous term's.)
7. 〔報道〕 技術の進歩は一昔前とは比べものにならない。(Technological progress is beyond comparison with a decade ago.)

### Answer key

1. とは比べものにならない
2. とは比べものにならない
3. 今の人気は全盛期とは比べものにならない。
4. 昔とは比べものにならない。
5. a very large difference
6. (b) — the particle とは must not be dropped in this standard pattern

## Tips / key patterns

- Memorize it as one package: noun + とは比べものにならない. A common trap in questions is dropping the は.
- This pattern is not used for small differences. If things differ only slightly, use 〜に比べれば or 〜より.
- It often appears in the strengthened form 〜とは比べものにならないほど〜, as in the third example.

## Summary

- 〜とは比べものにならない (noun + とは): "there is no comparison / incomparable" — emphasizes a huge gap.
- The particle とは is mandatory in its standard form; without は it is considered wrong in exam contexts.
- A common strengthener: 〜とは比べものにならないほど.
- Kanji: 觜井鬼張翼軫 (part of the 二十八宿) and 甲乙丙丁戊己庚壬癸・丑寅卯巳午 (the 干支 system).
