## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can dissect mock results with an error-clinic table: each 誤答 classified by strand (語彙・文法・読解・聴解) and by type (knowledge / tricked / careless / time), then turned into specific improvement actions.

## Explanation

**Clinic-table template** (one row per 誤答):

| No | Strand | Error type | Specific cause | Improvement action |
| 12 | 語彙 | 引っかけ | mixed-up 作用/影響 collocation | memorize 10 medicine collocations |
| 31 | 読解 | 時間切れ | text 3 read linearly | 3-minute skimming drill ×5 |

**Four error types and their actions.**
1. **知識不足:** go back to the material — make SRS cards, not just "read it again".
2. **引っかけ:** dissect the distractor — write down its differentiator (L43). One dissected trap = immunity to ten traps.
3. **ケアレスミス:** find the trigger (rushing? tired?), then make a checking ritual (e.g.: read options to the end before choosing).
4. **時間切れ:** an allocation problem — revise your 時間配分 (L45/L46).

**Actions per strand.** 語彙: collocations + near-synonyms. 文法: rewrite the wrong pattern with 2 examples of your own. 読解: identify its type (主張理解/情報検索), drill that type. 聴解: listen again, find its deciding word (L44).

**Golden rule:** one mock produces at most 5 actions. More than that = nothing gets done. Pick the most frequent ones.

## Example sentences

1. 〔分析〕 誤答の7割が引っかけ型だった。(70% of errors were the tricked type.)
2. 〔分析〕 時間切れは知識の問題ではない。(Running out of time is not a knowledge problem.)
3. 〔行動〕 ケアレスミスの原因は、選択肢の読み飛ばしだ。(The cause of the careless mistake: skipping over the options.)
4. 〔行動〕 文法の誤答は例文ごと復習する。(Review grammar mistakes along with their example sentences.)
5. 〔戦略〕 対策は5つまでに絞る。(Limit countermeasures to 5.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 間違いの分類ができれば、半分直ったも同然だよ。(Once you can classify your mistakes, you're halfway to fixing them.)

## Exercises

Classify each case into a type: 知識不足 / 引っかけ / ケアレスミス / 時間切れ.

1. Didn't know the meaning of 漸く, then guessed. Type: ______
2. Knew the pattern, but the option used a ない form that negated the meaning. Type: ______
3. Chose (b) when you meant (c) — your finger slipped a row. Type: ______
4. The last text never got read. Type: ______
5. Right action for case no. 2: a) memorize the word's meaning b) dissect the distractor: write down the differentiator of the ない form c) sleep earlier d) work faster
6. True or false: every 誤答 must get an action, no matter how many.

### Answer key

1. 知識不足 — action: SRS card.
2. 引っかけ — action: dissect its differentiator.
3. ケアレスミス — action: row-check ritual.
4. 時間切れ — action: revise allocation.
5. b — dissect the trap until you're immune.
6. False — at most 5 actions; pick the most frequent.

## Tips / important patterns

- Classify right after the mock, while your memory of your thought process is still fresh.
- Actions must be checkably completable ("memorize 10 collocations"), not resolutions ("be more careful").

## Summary

- Clinic table: No | Strand | Type | Cause | Action.
- Four types: 知識不足 → SRS cards; 引っかけ → dissect the distractor; ケアレスミス → find the trigger + ritual; 時間切れ → revise allocation.
- Actions differ per strand; at most 5 actions per mock.
