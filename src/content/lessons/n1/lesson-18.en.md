## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜はさておき to set a topic aside for the moment ("set aside for now"), and 〜はおろか to express "let alone" with a graded pattern. You can also distinguish them, as both place two things side by side but their functions are opposite.

## Explanation

**1. 〜はさておき (set aside for now).** Formula: noun + はさておき (さておき = さて置き, "put aside for now"). Used to set one topic aside temporarily and move to the main topic. Neutral, with no critical tone: 冗談はさておき、本題に入ろう.

- 冗談はさておき、本題に 入ろう。(Jokes aside for now, let's get to the main topic.)

**2. 〜はおろか (let alone).** Formula: noun + はおろか. おろか was originally the adjective "foolish", here meaning "not worth discussing". The pattern is graded: A はおろか、B も〜ない — A is the lighter/easier thing, B the heavier one, and NEITHER happens. Don't mix up the order.

- 挨拶はおろか、目も 合わせない。(Let alone greeting, (he) won't even make eye contact.)

Distinguish: はさておき shifts the topic (neutral); はおろか asserts graded failure (negative). Both place two things side by side, but one means "set aside for now" and the other "both fail".

## Example sentences

1. 〔日常〕 冗談はさておき、本題に 入ろう。(Jokes aside for now, let's get to the main topic.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 予算はさておき、まず 方針を 決めよう。(The budget aside for now, let's first decide the policy direction.)
3. 〔日常〕 挨拶はおろか、目も 合わせない。(Let alone greeting, (he) won't even make eye contact.)
4. 〔文語〕 漢字はおろか、仮名も 読めない 子が いる。(There are children who, let alone kanji, cannot even read kana.)
5. 〔報道〕 謝罪はおろか、責任も 認めようとしない。(Let alone apologizing, (he) won't even admit responsibility.)
6. 〔文学〕 登竜門はおろか、予選すら 通過できなかった。(Let alone the gateway to success, (he) couldn't even pass the preliminaries.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 前置きはさておき、結論から 申し上げる。(The preface aside, I will state the conclusion first.)

## Exercises

1. Choose the correct one: 冗談 (はさておき / はおろか)、本題に 入ろう。
2. Choose the correct one: 挨拶 (はさておき / はおろか)、返事もしない。
3. Fill in with the correct pattern: 謝罪___、責任も 取らない。
4. Translate into Japanese: "Let alone writing, even reading is hard for him." → ______
5. In the pattern A はおろか、B も〜ない, A is the: (lighter/easier / heavier/harder) one → ______
6. 〜はさておき is used to: (shift the topic / express impossibility) → ______

### Answer key

1. はさておき
2. はおろか
3. はおろか
4. 書くことはおろか、読むことも 彼には 難しい。
5. lighter/easier
6. shift the topic

## Tips / key patterns

- Test the 〜はおろか order: A must be easier than B. ×博士号はおろか、九九も言えない (reversed; correct: 九九はおろか、博士号は夢のまた夢).
- 〜はおろか is always followed by a negative form (〜も…ない): if the second clause is positive, the pattern is wrong.
- 〜はさておき can be used at the start of a formal speech: 前置きはさておき — polite and efficient.

## Summary

- 〜はさておき (noun +): "set aside for now", shifting to the main topic; neutral.
- 〜はおろか (noun +): "let alone"; the pattern A はおろか、B も〜ない with A lighter; both fail.
- Quick test: "is the topic being shifted?" → さておき. "Do both fail in a graded way?" → おろか.
- Kanji: 登竜門, 墨守, 玉石混交, 旌旗, 舞い上がる.
