## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can catch the implied meaning of abstract lectures through three strategies: contrast markers, softening tone, and ironic examples.

## Explanation

N1 abstract lectures rarely state their conclusion bluntly. The implied meaning hides in three places:

**1. Contrast markers.** Words like しかし, とはいえ, もっとも reverse the direction. Rule: *the sentence after the contrast marker = the real message*. 「この理論は美しい。もっとも、現実には通用しない。」→ implied: the theory is useless.

**2. Softening tone (婉曲).** 〜と言えなくもない, 〜とは言い切れない, 一概には言えない weaken a claim — and that's precisely where the criticism lies. 「成功したとは言い切れない」= implied: it can't really be called a success.

**3. Ironic examples.** The speaker gives an extreme example and stops; the example mirrors the topic. Your task: ask "what is this example satirizing?"

**Mini-script 1 (TTS material).** 〔文語・講義〕 「言語の開闢について論じてきた。もっとも、開闢という言葉自体が神話である。盤石に見える理論も荒波の前では薄められていく。」→ Implied: the theory of language origins is fragile.

**Mini-script 2 (TTS material).** 〔文語・講義〕 「この学説は宝庫と言えなくもない。しかし、鍵を握るのはいつも少数派だ。」→ Implied: criticism of a few people monopolizing knowledge.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 「もっとも」という 一言が、講義の 向きを 変える。(The single word "mottomo" changes the lecture's direction.)
2. 〔文語〕 「成功したとは 言い切れない」には、婉曲な 批判が 含まれる。(There is a veiled criticism behind it.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 「検討させて いただきます」は、婉曲な 拒否の 含みが ある。(There is an implied polite refusal behind it.)
4. 〔日常〕 行間を 読むって、書いてない ことを 推測する ことだよ。(Reading gyokan means guessing what's not written.)
5. 〔文語〕 逆説的な 表現ほど、話者の 本音を 映す。(The more paradoxical the expression, the more it reflects the speaker's true feelings.)
6. 〔文語〕 前提を 疑うことから、講義の 含意は 開かれる。(Questioning the premise opens up the lecture's implied meaning.)
7. 〔報道〕 濃厚な 疑惑が 報じられたが、当局は 幕開けを 宣言した ばかりだ。(Thick suspicions were reported, yet the authorities have just declared an opening.)
8. 〔文学〕 珍しい 獣を 追うように、彼は 言葉の 含みを 追った。(Like chasing a rare beast, he chased the nuances of words.)

## Exercises

1. Script: 「この政策は立派だ。もっとも、現場では機能していない。」Implied meaning: ______.
2. Script: 「効果があったとは言い切れない。」Implied meaning: ______.
3. Choose the correct one: the sentence after しかし/もっとも is (empty politeness / the real message).
4. Choose the correct one: 「一概には言えない」 shows (full confidence / subtle doubt).
5. Strategy when the speaker gives an extreme example: ask ______.
6. Translate: "The implied criticism behind the praise." → ______

### Answer key

1. The policy doesn't work in the field (the praise is canceled by もっとも).
2. It can't be said there was an effect (= it most likely failed).
3. the real message
4. subtle doubt
5. "what is this example satirizing?"
6. 褒め言葉の 裏の 批判。

## Tips / key patterns

- When listening, note three things: contrast markers, softening expressions, extreme examples.
- Trap: options that repeat the speaker's literal words are almost always wrong — the correct answer paraphrases the implied meaning.
- Train your ears: read the mini-scripts aloud (or via TTS), then write their implied meaning in one sentence.

## Summary

- Implied meaning lives in: contrast markers, softening tone, extreme examples.
- The sentence after しかし/もっとも/とはいえ = the real message.
- 〜とは言い切れない = doubt implying criticism.
- Kanji: 開闢, 宝庫, 盤石, 両立, 幕開け, 荒波.
