## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you understand five core yojijukugo — their meaning, reading, formal/literary register — and can choose the right one for the context.

## Explanation

Yojijukugo are four-kanji idioms that compress life lessons. At N1 they appear in formal reading — essays, speeches, editorials. The key: understand *when* each is used.

**一期一会 (いちごいちえ).** From the tea ceremony: every encounter happens once in a lifetime, so treat it with your whole heart. For farewells, welcoming guests, precious moments. Register: formal, warm.

**温故知新 (おんこちしん).** From Confucian teaching: dig into the old to discover new knowledge. For education, research, speeches about tradition. Register: formal, intellectual.

**疑心暗鬼 (ぎしんあんき).** A suspicious heart breeds demons of darkness: once suspicious, everything looks suspicious. For excessive suspicion. Register: literary, psychological.

**起承転結 (きしょうてんけつ).** Essay structure: 起 opening, 承 development, 転 change of angle, 結 conclusion. For writing technique. Register: formal, technical.

**十人十色 (じゅうにんといろ).** Ten people, ten colors: everyone is different. For diversity of opinions or tastes. Register: neutral-formal.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 一期一会の 心で、お客様を お迎えする。(We welcome our guests with a "one meeting, one chance" heart.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 温故知新の 精神で、古典を 現代の 経営に 生かしたい。(With the spirit of learning the old to know the new, I want to apply the classics to modern management.)
3. 〔文学〕 疑心暗鬼に 陥った 彼は、友の 言葉さえ 疑った。(Fallen into blind suspicion, he doubted even his friend's words.)
4. 〔文語〕 この 文章は 起承転結が 明確で、読みやすい。(This text has a clear opening–development–turn–conclusion structure, making it easy to read.)
5. 〔日常〕 十人十色だから、みんなが 同じ 意見で なくて 当たり前だよ。(Everyone is different, so it's only natural that not everyone shares the same opinion.)
6. 〔文語〕 雪が 積もるように、知識も 日々 積もる。(Like snow piling up, knowledge too accumulates day by day.)
7. 〔報道〕 福祉の 光が、老いた 村にも 届き 始めた。(The light of welfare has begun to reach even the aging village.)
8. 〔文語〕 暗記だけでは、温故知新の 境地に 届かない。(Memorization alone won't reach the realm of learning from the past.)

## Exercises

1. The yojijukugo for "cherish every meeting because it happens only once": ______.
2. The yojijukugo for "learn from the past": ______.
3. Choose the correct one: 彼の 疑い深さは (疑心暗鬼 / 一期一会) そのものだ。
4. Choose the correct one: 文章の 構成を 論じる文脈で使うのは (起承転結 / 十人十色)。
5. Translate: "Everyone is different; cherish those differences." (use a yojijukugo) → ______
6. 一期一会 comes from the world of: (the tea ceremony / the battlefield).

### Answer key

1. 一期一会
2. 温故知新
3. 疑心暗鬼
4. 起承転結
5. 十人十色だから、その 違いを 大切にしたい。
6. the tea ceremony

## Tips / key patterns

- Don't use yojijukugo in casual conversation — it sounds stiff. Their place: essays, speeches, formal writing.
- Memorize the meaning–context pairs: 一期一会 = precious encounters; 温故知新 = education; 疑心暗鬼 = suspicion; 起承転結 = writing technique; 十人十色 = diversity.
- Readings: いちごいちえ (not いっきいちえ), おんこちしん.

## Summary

- 一期一会: cherish every encounter — farewells, welcomes.
- 温故知新: dig into the old for the new — education.
- 疑心暗鬼: suspicion makes everything look suspicious.
- 起承転結: essay structure — writing technique.
- 十人十色: everyone is different — diversity.
- Formal/literary register; avoid in casual conversation.
- Kanji: 暗記, 阿弥陀, 観音, 不動, 毘沙門天, 弁財天.
