## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the N1 pair 〜を余儀なくされる ("forced to …") and 〜を余儀なくさせる ("force … to") that appear very often in news. You can also tell them apart: in one, the subject is the party that is forced; in the other, the subject is the party doing the forcing.

## Explanation

**1. 〜を余儀なくされる (pattern 1).** Formula: noun + を余儀なくされる. Meaning: "forced to …" — the subject is the party forced by circumstances. The word 余儀なく is read よぎなく.

- 祭りは中止を余儀なくされた。(The festival was forced to be canceled.)
- 計画の縮小を余儀なくされた。(We were forced to downsize the plan.)

**2. 〜を余儀なくさせる (pattern 2).** Formula: (cause) が／は + (forced party) に + noun + を余儀なくさせる. Meaning: "force (someone) to …" — the subject is the cause of the circumstances.

- 台風が主催者に日程の変更を余儀なくさせた。(The typhoon forced the organizers to change the schedule.)
- 原材料の高騰が値上げを余儀なくさせている。(Soaring raw-material prices are forcing a price increase.)

Distinguish: in pattern 1, the subject of the sentence = the victim of circumstances (祭りは…された); in pattern 2, the subject = the cause of circumstances (台風が…させた). Both always use nouns like 中止, 延期, 縮小, 変更, 断念 — not verbs directly.

## Example sentences

1. 〔報道〕 台風の影響で、祭りは中止を余儀なくされた。(Due to the typhoon, the festival was forced to be canceled.)
2. 〔報道〕 資金不足により、計画の縮小を余儀なくされた。(Due to a lack of funds, we were forced to downsize the plan.)
3. 〔報道〕 大雪が主催者に日程の変更を余儀なくさせた。(Heavy snow forced the organizers to change the schedule.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 原材料の高騰が値上げを余儀なくさせている。(Soaring raw-material prices are forcing a price increase.)
5. 〔文語〕 社長は苦渋の決断で辞任を余儀なくされた。(The president was forced to resign in a bitter decision.)
6. 〔報道〕 感染拡大がイベントの延期を余儀なくさせた。(The spread of infection forced the event's postponement.)
7. 〔日常〕 雨続きで、ブディさんは旅行の断念を余儀なくされた。(With the continuous rain, Budi was forced to give up his travel plans.)

## Tips / key patterns

- First determine who the subject is: the victim of circumstances → される; the cause of circumstances → させる. This is the key to telling the two patterns apart.
- The nouns used are almost always about "stopping / changing plans": 中止, 延期, 縮小, 変更, 断念, 見直し.
- 余儀なく is always written in kana よぎなく in practice; its kanji form rarely appears in modern texts.

## Summary

- 〜を余儀なくされる (noun + を): "forced to …" — subject = the party forced by circumstances.
- 〜を余儀なくさせる (noun + を): "force … to" — subject = the cause of circumstances, in the frame (cause) が (party) に (action) を余儀なくさせた.
- Typical nouns: 中止, 延期, 縮小, 変更, 断念, 見直し.
- Kanji: 未酉戌亥, 乾坤震巽坎離艮, 蟄穀処, 連歌短俳浮錦.
