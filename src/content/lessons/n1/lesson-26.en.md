## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the N1 pair 〜てはばからない and 〜を憚らない ("to not hesitate / not hold back") typical of critical essays and opinion columns. You can also tell their forms apart: one attaches to the te-form of verbs, the other to nouns with the particle を.

## Explanation

**1. 〜てはばからない (pattern 1).** Formula: te-form verb + はばからない. Meaning: "not hesitate to …" / "without holding back …". The verb 憚る (はばかる) means "to hesitate out of reserve", so its negative form means daring to act without reserve.

- 権力を批判してはばからない。(Not hesitating to criticize power.)
- 作品を酷評してはばからない。(Not holding back a scathing review of the work.)

**2. 〜を憚らない (pattern 2).** Formula: noun + を憚らない. Same meaning: "not hesitate (to do) …".

- 真相の暴露を憚らなかった。(They did not hesitate to expose the truth.)
- 上司への異議を憚らなかった。(They did not hold back their objection to their boss.)

Distinguish: pattern 1 uses a verb (批判して), pattern 2 uses a noun (暴露を). The two cannot be mixed: 批判をはばからない ✓ (noun), 批判してはばからない ✓ (verb), but 批判をはばからって ✗.

Nuance: this pattern is not merely "being brave", but "being brave when people would normally hold back" — for example, criticizing one's boss or those in power. That is why it lives in critical essays, opinion columns, and editorials.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 彼は権力を批判してはばからない。(He does not hesitate to criticize power.)
2. 〔報道〕 記者は真相の暴露を憚らなかった。(The journalist did not hesitate to expose the truth.)
3. 〔文語〕 評論家はその作品を酷評してはばからない。(The critic does not hold back a scathing review of the work.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 彼は上司への異議を憚らなかった。(He did not hold back his objection to his boss.)
5. 〔文学〕 作家は社会の偽善を風刺してはばからなかった。(The writer did not hesitate to satirize society's hypocrisy.)
6. 〔文語〕 不正の告発を憚る理由はない。(There is no reason to hesitate to report wrongdoing.)
7. 〔日常〕 シンタさんは間違いを指摘してはばからない人だ。(Sinta is someone who does not hesitate to point out mistakes.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Look at the preceding word: te-form verb → はばからない; noun + を → 憚らない. This is the fastest way to answer questions.
- Doubled negative forms like はばからずに ("without hesitating") also often appear in formal texts.
- Its opposite is 〜をためらう or 〜を躊躇する ("to hesitate") — if a question asks for the antonym, this is the answer.

## Summary

- 〜てはばからない (te-form verb +): "to not hesitate …" — acting boldly.
- 〜を憚らない (noun + を): "to not hesitate (to do) …" — same meaning, different form.
- Nuance: bold when people would normally hold back; typical of critical essays and opinion pieces.
- Kanji: 邯鄲の夢, 塞翁が馬, 臥薪嘗胆, 呉越同舟, 画竜点睛, 鶏口牛後の構成要素.
