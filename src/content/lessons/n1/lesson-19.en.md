## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜もさることながら to express "besides A, B too" with emphasis on the second element. You also master the minimal-contrast technique for distinguishing near-synonymous words and patterns (near-synonyms), a core N1 skill.

## Explanation

**1. 〜もさることながら (besides A, B too).** Formula: noun + もさることながら. さる comes from 去る ("to leave/set aside"): "set A aside for now, but B also…". The emphasis falls on the SECOND element. Literary and formal in tone.

- 味もさることながら、見た目も 美しい。(Besides its taste, its appearance is also beautiful.)

**2. Minimal-contrast technique for near-synonyms.** Three steps: (1) find the shared core meaning; (2) build a minimal sentence pair — only ONE element differs; (3) test the difference in register, collocation, and emphasis. An application example with three near-synonymous patterns:

- 努力はもちろん、運も 要る。(Of course effort; luck is needed too.) → neutral.
- 努力は言うまでもなく、運も 要る。(It goes without saying; luck is needed too.) → emphasizes universality.
- 努力もさることながら、運が ものを 言う。(Besides effort, it is luck that decides.) → literary, strong emphasis on the second element.

The core meaning is the same ("not only A, but B too"), but their 語感 differs. This is what N1 tests: not memorized definitions, but 使い分け through minimal contrast.

## Example sentences

1. 〔日常〕 味もさることながら、見た目も 美しい。(Besides its taste, its appearance is also beautiful.)
2. 〔文語〕 楷書もさることながら、隷書の 味わいも 深い。(Besides the 楷書 style, the flavor of 隷書 is also profound.)
3. 〔報道〕 規模もさることながら、対応の 速さが 問われる。(Besides its scale, it is the speed of response that is questioned.)
4. 〔文語〕 努力はもちろん、運も 要る。(Of course effort; luck is needed too.)
5. 〔文語〕 努力もさることながら、運が ものを 言う。(Besides effort, it is luck that decides.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 価格もさることながら、納期が 決め手だ。(Besides the price, it is the deadline that decides.)
7. 〔文学〕 雁の 声もさることながら、池の 静けさが 心に しみた。(Besides the wild geese's cries, the pond's silence sank into the heart.)

## Tips / key patterns

- After 〜もさることながら, the second element often takes an emphatic particle: が・こそ・決め手だ. That is the sign of its emphasis.
- For N1 near-synonym questions, don't memorize definitions — build minimal pairs and feel the difference in 語感, as in this lesson's 努力 example.
- 〜もさることながら is written-style; in casual conversation, 〜はもちろん is more natural.

## Summary

- 〜もさることながら (noun +): "besides A, B too", emphasis on the second element, literary tone.
- Near-synonyms with the same meaning don't necessarily feel the same: はもちろん (neutral), は言うまでもなく (universality), もさることながら (literary + emphasis on B).
- Minimal-contrast technique: same core meaning → minimal pair → test register/collocation/emphasis.
- Kanji: 楷書, 隷書, 篆書, 楮, 雁, 伽藍, 庫裏.
