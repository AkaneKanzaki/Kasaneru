## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you have a stamina plan for the 60-minute N1 聴解 played once (play-once): quick note-taking technique, energy distribution per section, and a drill for telling similar options apart — without needing any audio files beyond the app's audio/TTS feature.

## Explanation

**The 60-minute field.** N1 聴解: 課題理解 (full memo), ポイント理解, 概要理解, 即時応答 (reflex), 統合理解 (long). All play-once. The common failure isn't "can't do it" but focus running out around minute 40.

**The 3-second memo technique.** Write only keywords: numbers, names, and main verbs. Use symbols: ↑ (up), ↓ (down), × (cancelled), ○ (correct), → (consequence), ? (unsure). Don't write full sentences — your hand can't keep up with the audio.

**Rules of play per question.** (1) During preparation time, read ALL options first and predict the topic. (2) While the audio runs, cross out options disqualified by a single word (usually a number, time, or 限定). (3) If unsure, choose and don't look back.

**Distinguish-the-options drill.** Take one listening question from the app's question bank, play it with the audio/TTS feature, then — without looking at the key — write ONE deciding word that separates the correct option from each distractor (e.g. 「3時」 vs 「4時」). If you can't find the deciding word, your memo isn't sharp enough.

## Example sentences

1. 〔試験の指示〕 音声は一度だけ流れます。(The audio plays only once.)
2. 〔メモ例〕 会議→3時×、4時○、場所？ (Meeting: 3 o'clock cancelled, 4 o'clock confirmed, venue unclear.)
3. 〔戦略〕 選択肢は音声の前に読む。(Read the options before the audio plays.)
4. 〔戦略〕 数字と限定語を逃すな。(Don't miss numbers and limiting words.)
5. 〔文語〕 集中力の配分が合否を分ける。(How you distribute concentration decides pass or fail.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 迷ったら選んで次へ進め、振り返るな。(If unsure, pick and move on — don't look back.)

## Exercises

1. The best memo for「来週の火曜日の3時から会議を行います。場所はまだ決まっていません」: a) 会議についての詳しい説明を聞いた b) 火3時○ 場所？ c) 来週会議があるらしい
2. Best technique for 即時応答: a) long memos b) reflex + crossing out odd options c) translating each sentence d) waiting for a repeat
3. The deciding word that most often separates the correct option from distractors: a) greetings b) numbers, times, and 限定 words c) people's names d) settings
4. Focus dies at minute 40. The structural solution: a) more caffeine b) save energy early with symbol memos c) skip part 4 d) work randomly
5. True or false: writing complete sentences while the audio runs is a good strategy.
6. This lesson's drill uses: a) self-composed audio files b) the app's audio/TTS feature + question bank c) random videos d) memorized transcripts

### Answer key

1. b — keywords + symbols only.
2. b — 即時応答 demands a quick response.
3. b — listening distractors live in numbers, times, and 限定.
4. b — energy management, not stimulants.
5. False — the hand can't keep up with the audio.
6. b — without composing audio files.

## Tips / important patterns

- Preparation time (before the audio) is half the victory: options you've already read make your ears hunt for keywords.
- If one question blanks you out, let it go in 5 seconds — one question must not ruin the next five.

## Summary

- 60 minutes play-once = a stamina test: save energy early, reserve focus for the final parts.
- Memo = keywords + symbols (↑↓×○→?), not sentences.
- Procedure: read options first → cross out while the audio runs → decide, don't look back.
