## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜べく to express purpose with strong determination ("in order to/so that") and 〜べくもない to express absolute impossibility ("utterly impossible") in formal arguments. You can also distinguish 〜べく from the more neutral 〜ために, since this difference in tone is often tested at N1.

## Explanation

**1. 〜べく (purpose + strong determination).** Formula: verb dictionary form + べく. The verb する becomes すべく (合格すべく), 来る becomes 来るべく. Meaning: "in order to/so that" with a nuance of strong determination. Unlike the neutral 〜ために, 〜べく emphasizes strong will and is almost always followed by verbs of effort such as 努力する・努める・断行する. If the main clause is not an action of effort, the pattern sounds odd.

- 合格すべく、毎日 努力する。(In order to pass, (he) strives every day.)

**2. 〜べくもない (absolute impossibility).** Formula: verb dictionary form + べくもない. Meaning: "utterly impossible". Used for things impossible to know or achieve, often with perception verbs like 知る・測る.

- 彼の 心中を 知るべくもない。(His innermost feelings are utterly unknowable.)

Don't confuse them: 〜わけにはいかない means "cannot because of circumstances", whereas 〜べくもない means "absolutely impossible".

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 合格すべく、毎日 十時間 机に 向かった。(In order to pass, he sat facing his desk ten hours every day.)
2. 〔文語〕 捏造を 暴くべく、記者は 一年がかりで 取材した。(In order to expose the fabrication, the journalist spent a year reporting.)
3. 〔報道〕 混沌を 収めるべく、政府は 対策を 断行した。(In order to bring the chaos under control, the government took resolute measures.)
4. 〔文語〕 彼の 心中を 知るべくもない。(His innermost feelings are impossible to know.)
5. 〔文語〕 挫折の 深さを 測るべくもない。(The depth of the failure's wounds cannot be measured.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 喫緊の 課題を 解決すべく、特別チームを 編成した。(In order to resolve the pressing issue, a special team was formed.)
7. 〔報道〕 黎明期の 産業を 育てるべく、基金が 設立された。(In order to nurture industries still in their dawn, a fund was established.)

### Answer key

1. すべく
2. 知るべくもない
3. すべく
4. 名誉を 取り戻すべく、休みなく 練習した。
5. 合格すべく毎日努力する。
6. 〜べく

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜べく is almost always followed by verbs of effort: 努力する・努める・励む・断行する. If the main clause is not an effort ("in order to pass, he is happy"), just use 〜ために.
- 〜べく has a literary, formal tone; don't use it in casual everyday conversation.
- 〜べくもない expresses absolute impossibility. Don't confuse it with 〜かねない, which instead means "might well happen".

## Summary

- 〜べく (verb dictionary form + べく; する→すべく): "in order to/so that" with strong determination, followed by an action of effort.
- 〜べくもない (verb dictionary form + べくもない): "utterly impossible".
- Unlike 〜ために: neutral, without emphasis on determination.
- Kanji: 捏造, 歪曲, 挫折, 岐路, 黎明, 喫緊, 逼迫, 混沌.
