## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you hold the final sign-off checklist: lecturer-verified readiness verification, plus an exam-day plan (logistics, mental, warm-up) — so all that remains is executing what you've trained.

## Explanation

**Sign-off checklist (get the lecturer's 承認).** All checked before H-3:
- [ ] 50 N1 lessons done; SRS without major backlog.
- [ ] Two full Real mocks meet the L48 criteria (total ≥100 estimated + floors).
- [ ] Clinic table (L47) filled for each mock; actions executed.
- [ ] Improvement plan (L49) completed with numerical evidence.
- [ ] One full exam-day simulation done.
- [ ] Lecturer's signature: __________ (symbolic — second human verification).

**Exam-day logistics.** 持ち物: 受験票, 身分証, 筆記用具 + spares, silent watch, food and water. Survey the venue beforehand; leave with a 60-minute buffer from 集合時間. The 前日 night: no new material, normal sleep, pack the bag.

**Morning warm-up (15 minutes).** Not new questions — just warm-up: 1 mastered short text, 1 familiar audio, 3 go-to patterns. The goal is to warm up the brain, not to test it.

**Mental.** 緊張 is normal — a signal the body is getting ready. The 3-deep-breath ritual (深呼吸) before each block. If you blank: close your eyes for 5 seconds, return to procedure. You've already paid with 50 lessons; today is the day to collect.

## Example sentences

1. 〔確認〕 受験票と身分証は鞄に入れたか。(Are the admission ticket and ID in the bag?)
2. 〔指示〕 会場には集合時間の一時間前に着く。(Arrive at the venue one hour before assembly time.)
3. 〔前日〕 前日は新しいことを学ぶな。(The day before, don't learn anything new.)
4. 〔当日〕 朝は慣れた文章でウォームアップする。(Warm up in the morning with a familiar text.)
5. 〔心構え〕 緊張は敵ではなく、合図だ。(Tension is not the enemy but a signal.)
6. 〔宣言〕 今日、全力を出し切る。(Today, give it everything you've got.)

## Exercises

1. Correct preparation order: a) survey the route → pack the bag at night → morning warm-up b) pull an all-nighter → leave at the last minute c) new material in the morning d) skip breakfast
2. The right morning warm-up: a) new questions b) 15 minutes of familiar material c) memorize 100 words d) not needed
3. Suggested departure buffer: a) 10 minutes b) 60 minutes from 集合時間 c) just in time d) 5 minutes
4. When blanking out mid-question: a) panic b) 5 seconds eyes closed → back to procedure c) skip the block d) ask the proctor
5. The lecturer's signature serves as: a) formality b) second human verification c) an exam requirement d) extra credit
6. True or false: new material on the 前日 night improves the score.

### Answer key

1. a — layered logistics: route, bag, warm-up.
2. b — warm-up warms, doesn't test.
3. b — 60 minutes absorbs all disruptions.
4. b — 5-second reset ritual, then procedure.
5. b — the second-human-verification principle.
6. False — new material adds anxiety.

## Tips / important patterns

- Print the checklist and stick it on the wall — visible readiness calms the mind.
- After the exam: don't dissect questions with friends. It's over — celebrate.

## Summary

- Sign-off: 50 lessons + 2 criteria-meeting mocks + clinic + improvement plan + exam-day simulation + lecturer 承認.
- Logistics: documents, spare stationery, route survey, 60-minute buffer.
- 15-minute warm-up with familiar material; H-1 no new material.
- Mental: 緊張 = readiness signal; 深呼吸 ritual + 5-second reset.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Task: complete the sign-off checklist, get the lecturer's 承認, and write your personal exam-day plan (hour-by-hour schedule). SRS: 準備, 持ち物, 受験票, 会場, 余裕, 緊張, 深呼吸, 前日, 当日.
