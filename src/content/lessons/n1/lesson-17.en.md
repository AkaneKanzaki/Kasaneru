## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜をよそに to express ignoring criticism or expectations, and 〜ならいざしらず to express "that's another matter" with a tone criticizing the actual state of affairs. You can also distinguish them from more neutral rejection patterns.

## Explanation

**1. 〜をよそに (ignoring).** Formula: noun + をよそに (よそ = elsewhere). Used when someone ignores criticism, expectations, or demands and acts as they please. The noun is usually something that "should" be heeded: 反対・批判・世論・期待. The nuance criticizes the doer.

- 反対をよそに 計画を 進めた。(Ignoring the opposition, the plan was pushed ahead.)

**2. 〜ならいざしらず (if it were…, that would be another matter).** Formula: noun + ならいざしらず (いざしらず = いざ知らず, "let it be unknown"). Used in the pattern: "if it were A, that would be another matter — but in reality it is not A, so it is unacceptable". It carries criticism of the actual situation. A is usually something excusable (子供・素人).

- 子供ならいざしらず、大人が 泣くものではない。(If it were children, that would be another matter; adults should not cry.)

Unlike 〜はともかく (neutral, merely setting aside): 〜ならいざしらず always carries disapproval of reality.

## Example sentences

1. 〔報道〕 反対をよそに、法案は 可決された。(Ignoring the opposition, the bill was passed.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 世論をよそに 値上げを 断行した。(Ignoring public opinion, the price hike was resolutely carried out.)
3. 〔日常〕 子供ならいざしらず、大人が 泣くものではない。(If it were children, that would be another matter; adults should not cry.)
4. 〔文語〕 素人ならいざしらず、専門家が 初歩的な 誤りを 犯すとは。(If it were a layperson, that would be another matter; who would expect an expert to make a basic mistake.)
5. 〔報道〕 批判をよそに、大統領は 外遊を 続けた。(Ignoring criticism, the president continued his overseas trip.)
6. 〔文語〕 脛に傷を 持つ 者ならいざしらず、潔白な 彼が 逃げる 理由はない。(If it were someone with a guilty secret, that would be another matter; there is no reason for an innocent man like him to flee.)
7. 〔日常〕 雨ならいざしらず、晴れているのに 傘を 差すのか。(If it were raining, that would be another matter; why open an umbrella when it's sunny?)

### Answer key

1. をよそに
2. ならいざしらず
3. をよそに
4. 初心者ならいざしらず、彼は もう ベテランだ。
5. an ignored hope/demand（criticism, 反対, 世論, 期待）
6. criticism of reality

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜をよそに criticizes the DOER (who ignores); 〜ならいざしらず criticizes the SITUATION (which is not as it should be). First determine who is being criticized.
- After 〜をよそに, the doer still acts: 反対をよそに進めた (still pushes ahead). If the doer stops, the pattern doesn't fit.
- Can 〜ならいざしらず be shortened to 〜はさておき? No — the meanings differ: はさておき neutrally shifts the topic, ならいざしらず criticizes.

## Summary

- 〜をよそに (noun +): "ignoring" criticism/expectations/demands; criticizing the doer who acts as they please.
- 〜ならいざしらず (noun + なら): "if it were… that would be another matter"; in reality it is not so, hence unacceptable; criticizing the situation.
- Unlike the neutral 〜はともかく.
- Kanji: 踵を返す, 脛に傷を持つ, 槍玉に挙げる, 薙刀, 下駄.
