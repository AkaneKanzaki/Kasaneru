## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can recognize the two classical negation forms 〜ぬ and 〜ず in literary texts, and translate their fossilized expressions such as 知らぬ間に and 思わず笑った. The classical forms in this module are for reading recognition only — you are not asked to produce classical sentences yourself.

## Explanation

**1. 〜ぬ (classical negation).** Formula: 未然形 form + ぬ, meaning "not". Examples: 知ら-ぬ, 言わ-ぬ, あり得-ぬ. This form is dead in modern Japanese, but lives on as fossils in fixed expressions: 知らぬ間に (without realizing it), 言わぬが花 (silence is golden), 思わぬ出来事 (an unexpected event).

**2. 〜ず (classical negation).** Formula: 未然形 form + ず, meaning "without" or "not". Examples: 思わ-ず, 知ら-ず, 惜しま-ず. Its modern fossils are very numerous: 思わず (involuntarily), 知らず知らず (without realizing it), 相変わらず (as usual), 堪らず (unbearable). Its continuative form 〜ずに means "without doing ...": 恐れずに進む (advance without fear).

Tell the two apart: 〜ぬ survives as a stiff classical negation fossil, while 〜ず is more alive as a fossil and has the 〜ずに form. Both only need to be recognized while reading, not used to write new sentences.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文学〕 知らぬ間に 涙が こぼれていた。(Without realizing it, tears had fallen.)
2. 〔日常〕 冗談が 面白くて、思わず 笑った。(The joke was so funny I laughed involuntarily.)
3. 〔文語・kuno〕 彼は 恐れず 真実を 語った。(He spoke the truth without fear.)
4. 〔文学〕 言わぬが花と、祖母は よく 言った。(Silence is golden, my grandmother used to say.)
5. 〔文語・kuno〕 彼は 一度も 振り返らず 歩き続けた。(He kept walking without looking back even once.)
6. 〔日常〕 彼女は 相変わらず 元気だ。(She is as well as ever.)
7. 〔文学〕 惜しまず 与える 心を 学んだ。(I learned about a heart that gives without hesitation.)
8. 〔文語・kuno〕 あり得ぬことが 目の 前で 起こった。(The impossible happened before my eyes.)

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜ぬ and 〜ず both mean "not", but 〜ず appears far more often as a modern fossil — memorize its pairs: 思わず、知らず知らず、相変わらず.
- The markers are clear: ぬ or ず attaches directly to the verb stem (知らぬ、思わず). When you find them in a text, translate them as "without/not".
- Don't try to write classical sentences yourself; just recognize the patterns and match them with the gloss while reading.

## Summary

- 〜ぬ: classical negation (未然形 + ぬ) — 知らぬ間に、言わぬが花、あり得ぬ. Recognition only.
- 〜ず: classical negation (未然形 + ず) — 思わず、知らず知らず、相変わらず、惜しまず、〜ずに. Recognition only.
- Both mean "not/without" and appear as fossils in modern Japanese.
