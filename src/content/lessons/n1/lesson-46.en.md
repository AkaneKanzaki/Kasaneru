## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can execute your first full mock in Real mode: 110 minutes of the 言語知識・読解 block + 60 minutes of 聴解, with time allocation per section, rules for when to skip questions, and recording discipline — exactly like exam day.

## Explanation

**The 170-minute map.** Block 1 (110 minutes): 語彙 ±15, 文法 ±20, 読解 ±70, 見直し 5. Block 2 (60 minutes): the L44 protocol. Between blocks: a full break — don't discuss block 1 questions, don't open notes.

**The 90-second rule.** One question with no progress after 90 seconds = mark it and 見送る. The 拾う・捨てる principle: take the "pick-up questions" (easy, fast) in the first round; the "discard questions" (long, tricky) in the second round. No question may hold you for more than 3 minutes in the first round.

**Block 1 order.** (1) 語彙: fast, ±30 seconds per question. (2) 文法: identify the pattern, choose. (3) 読解: 3-minute skimming per text (L41); do 情報検索 first, then 主張理解. (4) Second round: return to marked questions. (5) Final 5 minutes: make sure everything is filled in — no penalty for wrong answers.

**Recording discipline.** Record start/finish times per section, marked question numbers, and when your focus dropped. This data feeds L47 and L49.

**Real mode:** timer with no pauses, no dictionary, no peeking at the key, one run like exam day.

The twenty kanji for this lesson (from the N1 outline's 1000-kanji list — themed on waterbirds and nature; focus on recognizing readings): 鴎（かもめ）、外（そと・がい）、端（はし・たん）、江（え・こう）、自（みずから・じ）、感（かん）、覚（おぼえる・かく）、興（おこる・こう）、術（じゅつ）、無（ない・む）、代（かわる・だい）、塊（かたまり）、類（るい）、樹（き・じゅ）、國（こく — 旧字体 of 国）、織（おる・しょく）、光（ひかり・こう）、東（ひがし・とう）、圭（けい）、吾（われ・ご）.

## Example sentences

1. 〔戦略〕 90秒考えても進まなければ見送れ。(If 90 seconds of thought brings no progress, skip it.)
2. 〔戦略〕 拾う問題を先に、捨てる問題は後回しに。(Pick-up questions first, discard questions later.)
3. 〔試験の指示〕 空欄を残すな、全部埋めろ。(Leave nothing blank — fill in everything.)
4. 〔記録例〕 文法25分超過、読解にしわ寄せ。(Grammar over by 5 minutes; reading took the hit.)
5. 〔休憩〕 ブロック間の休憩では前の問題を考えるな。(During the break between blocks, don't think about previous questions.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 3分悩む問題は、後回しが正解だよ。(A question that makes you think for 3 minutes — postponing it is the right answer.)

## Exercises

1. Block 1 allocation: 語彙15 + 文法20 + 読解70 + 見直し5 = ______ minutes.
2. Question stuck after 90 seconds. Action: a) keep going b) mark and continue c) guess randomly d) skip the block
3. Recommended 読解 order: a) 主張理解 first b) 情報検索 first c) longest text first d) random
4. Per-question limit in the first round: a) 90 seconds with no progress → mark b) 5 minutes c) until certain d) 30 seconds
5. During the break between blocks: a) discuss block 1 questions b) open notes c) full break, reset d) do spare questions
6. True or false: leaving answers blank is better than guessing.

### Answer key

1. 110 minutes.
2. b — the 90-second rule.
3. b — 情報検索 is more mechanical.
4. a — no question may hold you for more than 3 minutes in the first round.
5. c — total reset.
6. False — no penalty for wrong answers; fill in everything.

## Tips / important patterns

- The first mock measures your baseline; be disappointed if you didn't record the data, not because of the number.
- Practice filling in the 解答用紙 from now on; row-shift mistakes are preventable.

- The 90-second rule, the 拾う・捨てる principle, second round for marked questions.
- Real mode: no pauses, no dictionary, no peeking at the key, one run.
