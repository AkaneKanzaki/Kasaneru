## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can read two opposing editorials (社説), grasp the 主張 (main argument) of each, find the points of difference and the points of agreement between them, and state your own judgment with 根拠. The ability to compare two texts is the heart of the N1 読解 section.

## Explanation

Steps to compare two opposing 社説:

1. **Grasp the 主張 of each in one sentence.** Ignore details first; ask "what does the writer want?".
2. **Map the 根拠.** What facts or examples are used to support that 主張?
3. **Find the 対立点.** On the same issue, what is A's answer and what is B's answer?
4. **Find the common ground.** There is almost always one 課題 that both sides acknowledge.
5. **State your judgment.** Choose a 立場, give 根拠, and admit one weakness of your own position — this is what makes a mature comparative essay.

The two 社説 below are original compositions written for practice, not quotes from any media. Both discuss the same theme: 観光立国. Read both, then do the exercises.

## Example sentences

〔報道・社説〕 社説A「観光立国こそ地方再生の鍵である」:

地方の人口減少はもはや止められない、という諦めが広がっている。しかし観光には、地方を救う力がある。外国人観光客の消費は地方の旅館や飲食店に利益をもたらし、若者の雇用を生む。北海道や九州の例を見れば、観光が地域経済の柱になり得ることは明らかだ。観光客が増えれば交通や宿泊の整備が進み、地域の魅力が高まるという好循環も生まれる。観光は、地方に残された数少ない成長の芽である。もちろん交通渋滞やごみ問題などの課題はあるが、それらは受け入れ体制の未整備が原因であって、観光自体の罪ではない。規制を強めて観光客を遠ざければ、地方はさらに衰えるだけだ。必要なのは体制の整備であり、観光そのものの否定ではない。観光立国の推進こそ、地方再生の現実的な鍵である。

〔報道・社説〕 社説B「量から質へ、観光政策の転換を」:

観光客の増加が手放しで喜べる時代は終わった。京都では住民の生活道路が観光客で埋まり、ごみや騒音への苦情が絶えない。利益の多くは大手資本に吸い上げられ、地域に残るのは負担ばかりだという指摘もある。宿泊施設の不足や交通機関の混雑も深刻だ。夜間の騒音やごみの不法投棄など、マナーの問題も後を絶たない。観光立国を目指して数だけを追う政策は、もはや限界である。一度壊れた暮らしと景観は、簡単には戻らない。求められるのは量から質への転換だ。入域料の導入や人数制限、予約制の普及など、住民の生活を守る規制を整えるべきである。観光は地域の暮らしを壊してはならない。地域に根ざした持続可能な観光こそ、次の時代の目標である。

## Exercises

1. Summarize the 主張 of 社説 A in one English sentence.
2. Summarize the 主張 of 社説 B in one English sentence.
3. Both 社説 acknowledge the same 課題. What is it?
4. Explain their 対立点: A answers "how should we face tourists?" — what does B answer?
5. Name two 具体策 proposed by 社説 B.
6. Which 立場 are you closer to? Write 3 sentences with your 根拠.

### Answer key

1. Tourism is the realistic key to reviving the regions, so the 観光立国 campaign should continue with improved reception systems, not by rejecting tourism.
2. The policy of chasing tourist numbers has reached its limit; what is needed is a shift from quantity to quality through regulations that protect residents' lives.
3. Both acknowledge the same 課題: 社説 A mentions "交通渋滞などの課題", while 社説 B mentions complaints about garbage, noise, and the burden on residents — the negative impact of tourism is acknowledged by both sides.
4. A: face it by strengthening the reception system (体制) and rejecting regulations that drive tourists away. B: face it with regulations (入域料, 人数制限) that limit and manage tourists to protect residents.
5. 入域料の導入 (introducing an entry fee) and 人数制限 (limiting visitor numbers).
6. Sample answer: I lean closer to B, because tourism profits often don't reach local residents (根拠: the 指摘 about 大手資本). But I admit B's weakness: excessive regulation could kill the economy of regions that depend on tourists.

## Tips / key patterns

- When summarizing the 主張, look for the closing sentence of the 社説 — the 主張 is almost always asserted at the end ("〜である", "〜べきである").
- The common ground (a 課題 acknowledged by both) is the key to writing a 折衷案: "A is right about X, B is right about Y, so the solution is Z".
- In exams, comparison questions often ask "what the two writers do NOT agree on" — the answer lies in the 対立点, not the common ground.

## Summary

- Steps: 主張 → 根拠 → 対立点 → common ground → your own judgment.
- A (pro): 観光立国 = the key to 地方再生, strengthen the system (体制), reject regulations that drive tourists away.
- B (con): chasing numbers has hit its limit, shift to quality via 入域料 and 人数制限.
- Common ground: the negative impact of tourism is acknowledged by both.
