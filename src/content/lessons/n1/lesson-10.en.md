## Lesson goals

This lesson consolidates all of module two: you assemble a complete business letter from 拝啓 to 敬具 using every device learned so far — 時候の挨拶, 謹んで/恐れながら, ご〜いただく/お〜申し上げる, and 社交辞令. There are no new patterns.

## Explanation

The complete business letter checklist has seven items. **1. 頭語**: 拝啓. **2. 時候の挨拶** in the 前文. **3. 名乗り**: introducing yourself, for example 「ジャカルタ支社のデウィと申します」. **4. 主文**: the 用件 in correct keigo — ご〜いただく, お〜申し上げる, and 恐れながら before an important request. **5. 結びの挨拶** in the 末文: a wish or hope. **6. 社交辞令** before the closing. **7. 結語**: 敬具, then 日付・署名・宛名.

The flow looks like this: 拝啓 → 時候の挨拶 → 名乗り → さて、ご案内申し上げます → 恐れながらご出欠のご連絡をお願い申し上げます → ご参加をお待ち申し上げております → ご自愛ください → 敬具. Every layer uses this module's devices — none may be missing.

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 拝啓 時下ますますご清祥のこととお慶び申し上げます。(Dear Sir/Madam, I hope you are in ever greater health and prosperity.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 私はジャカルタ支社のデウィと申します。(I am Dewi from the Jakarta branch.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 さて、来月開催の研修につきましてご案内申し上げます。(Regarding the training to be held next month, please accept this invitation.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 ご多忙のところ恐れ入りますが、ご出欠を今月末までにご連絡いただきたく存じます。(We apologize for troubling you amid your busy schedule; please let us know your attendance by the end of this month.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 皆様のご参加を心よりお待ち申し上げております。(We sincerely look forward to your attendance.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 まずは書面にてご案内申し上げます。敬具(For now, please accept this invitation in writing. Respectfully,)
7. 〔日常〕 手紙は「誰が・何を・いつまでに」が明確だと伝わりやすいよ。(A letter is easy to understand when "who, what, by when" are clear.)

## Exercises

1. Order them: (a. 敬具 / b. 拝啓 / c. 主文 / d. 時候の挨拶) → ______
2. Translate into English: ご出欠を今月末までにご連絡いただきたく存じます。
3. The self-introduction part "〜と申します" is called: (a. 名乗り / b. 結語)
4. Which is a 結びの挨拶? (a. まずは書面にてご案内申し上げます / b. 拝啓)
5. Change to formal letter language: 資料を送ります → ______
6. The 拝啓–敬具 package must not be mixed with the: (a. 前略–草々 / b. 時候の挨拶)

2. Please let us know your attendance by the end of this month.

## Tips / key patterns

- Write the seven-layer outline first, then fill in the sentences — a letter that skips layers immediately feels amateurish.
- The three layers most often missing: 名乗り, 恐れながら before a request, and 社交辞令 before 敬具. Check all three.
- One letter, one 用件. If there are two matters, write two letters.

## Summary

- Seven layers: 拝啓 → 時候の挨拶 → 名乗り → 主文 (correct keigo) → 結びの挨拶 → 社交辞令 → 敬具.
- Module 2 consolidation: all devices — 謹んで/恐れながら, ご〜いただく/お〜申し上げる, 拝啓↔敬具 — used together.
- Principle: one letter, one matter; the direction of respect is always correct.
