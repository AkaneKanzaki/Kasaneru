## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can handle 統合理解 questions: combining two listening sources by comparing them, finding contradictions, and drawing a combined conclusion.

## Explanation

統合理解 questions give you two audios (A and B). The answer comes from *combining* the two. Three steps:

**1. Separate (分離).** Note down A's and B's main claims each in 3 words. Don't mix them yet.

**2. Compare (対照).** Find their relationship: 一致 (reinforcing), 補完 (complementing), 食い違い (contradicting). N1 questions love subtle 食い違い.

**3. Draw a combined conclusion (統合).** The answer = "A says X, B adds to / contradicts it with Y, therefore Z". Nobody says Z.

**Mini-script A (TTS material).** 〔文語・講義〕 「希臘の教育は対話を重んじた。問答を通じて真理に至る。」→ Claim A: Greek education = dialogue leading to truth.

**Mini-script B (TTS material).** 〔文語・対談〕 「対話は大切だ。ただ、埃及や緬甸では師の言葉の暗記が重んじられた。」→ Claim B: other traditions = memorization; partially opposes A.

**統合:** Both agree education matters (一致) but differ in method: dialogue vs memorization (食い違い). There is no universal method; each is valid in its tradition.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 二つの 資料を 照合して、初めて 全体像が 見えた。(Only after matching the two documents did the whole picture emerge.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 A案と B案の 食い違いを 整理するのが、統合の 第一歩だ。(Sorting out the discrepancies between plan A and plan B is the first step of integration.)
3. 〔文語〕 両者の 主張は 一致して いるが、根拠が 異なる。(Both claims agree, but their grounds differ.)
4. 〔日常〕 二人の 話を 統合すると、結局 お金が 足りないって ことだよ。(Combining the two stories, it basically comes down to not having enough money.)
5. 〔報道〕 墺太利と 匈牙利の 首脳は、難民政策で 相違点を 残したまま 会談を 終えた。(The Austrian and Hungarian leaders ended their talks with differences on refugee policy unresolved.)
6. 〔文語〕 対照的な 二説を 並べることで、問題の 輪郭が 浮かぶ。(Placing two contrasting theories side by side reveals the problem's outline.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 顧客の 要望と 現場の 実情を 統合した 企画を 出したい。(I want to propose a plan that combines the client's wishes with on-the-ground reality.)
8. 〔文語〕 補完し合う 二つの 視点こそ、統合理解の 醍醐味である。(Two mutually complementing perspectives — that is the true joy of it.)

## Exercises

1. Script A: "Dialogue leads to truth." Script B: "Memorizing the master's words matters." Their relation: (一致 / 食い違い).
2. The first step of 統合理解: (conclude right away / separate each source's claim).
3. Choose the correct one: the answer to a 統合理解 question lies in (one source alone / the combination of both sources).
4. Script A: "Method X is best." Script B: "Method X is good, but method Y complements it." The relation and combined conclusion: ______
5. Translate: "Compare those two opinions." → ______

### Answer key

1. 食い違い
2. separate each source's claim
3. the combination of both sources
4. 補完 — Method X is good, but best when complemented by method Y.
5. 二つの 意見を 対照しなさい。

## Tips / key patterns

- Make an A | B column plus a "combined" row; don't answer from one source.
- Keywords: しかし/ただ = 食い違い; さらに = 補完; 同様に = 一致.
- Be suspicious of options that quote a source word for word.

## Summary

- Steps: 分離 → 対照 (一致/補完/食い違い) → 統合 (combined conclusion).
- The answer is born from your comparison, not spoken by either source.
- Watch out for options that quote only one source.
- Kanji: 芬蘭, 墺太利, 匈牙利, 希臘, 埃及, 緬甸, 錫蘭, 朝鮮, 濠太剌利, 紐西蘭, 哥倫比亜, 伯剌西爾.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Tasks: listen to (via TTS) mini-scripts A and B, write each source's claim and their relation, then formulate a combined conclusion. Add to your SRS: 統合, 照合, 対照, 補完, 食い違い, 一致, 相違点, 要約.
