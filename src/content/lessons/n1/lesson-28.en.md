## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use two N1 patterns with opposite meanings: 〜甲斐もなく ("in vain") for efforts that bear no fruit, and 〜たが最後 ("once … then …") for consequences that are unavoidable once something happens. Both often appear in reflective essays and narratives.

## Explanation

**1. 〜甲斐もなく (pattern 1).** Formula: noun + の甲斐もなく. Meaning: "in vain …" / "without bearing fruit". The word 甲斐 (かい) means "result, worth", so this pattern expresses efforts that end without result.

- 努力の甲斐もなく、計画は白紙に戻った。(The effort was in vain; the plan went back to square one.)
- 必死の捜索の甲斐もなく、発見されなかった。(The desperate search was in vain; they were not found.)

Its counterpart is 〜甲斐があって／〜た甲斐があった ("efforts bore fruit"): 苦労した甲斐があった (the toil paid off). Note: the positive form uses た甲斐があった, while the "in vain" negative form uses の甲斐もなく.

**2. 〜たが最後 (pattern 2).** Formula: ta-form verb + が最後. Meaning: "once … then (certainly) …" — stating that once something happens, the following consequence is unavoidable. Often sounds negative or resigned.

- 開けたが最後、閉まらなくなった。(Once opened, it could not be closed again.)
- 酒を飲んだが最後、止まらなくなった。(Once he drank, he could not stop.)

Its hallmark: the second clause describes a consequence that cannot be prevented. Compare with 〜たら ("if"): 〜たら is neutral, while 〜たが最後 emphasizes the inevitability of the consequence.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 長年の努力の甲斐もなく、計画は白紙に戻った。(Years of effort were in vain; the plan went back to square one.)
2. 〔報道〕 必死の捜索の甲斐もなく、登山者は発見されなかった。(The desperate search was in vain; the climber was not found.)
3. 〔日常〕 ダイエットを始めたが最後、三日で諦めた。(Once I started the diet, I gave up after three days.)
4. 〔報道〕 堤防が決壊したが最後、町は水に飲まれた。(Once the levee broke, the town was swallowed by water.)
5. 〔文学〕 彼女の尽力の甲斐もなく、店は閉店した。(Her best efforts were in vain; the shop closed.)
6. 〔日常〕 一度怠けたが最後、勉強が手につかなくなった。(Once I slacked off, I could no longer focus on studying.)
7. 〔文語〕 忠告を無視したが最後、彼は後悔することになった。(Once he ignored the advice, he came to regret it.)

## Exercises

1. Choose the correct one: 努力の___、夢は叶わなかった。(甲斐もなく / 甲斐もないで)
2. Fill in with the correct pattern: 一度手を出したが___、借金が膨らむ一方だった。
3. Translate into Japanese: "His efforts were in vain." → ______
4. 〜たが最後 implies a consequence that is: (avoidable / unavoidable).
5. Choose the WRONG sentence: (a) 努力の甲斐もなく (b) 努力した甲斐があった (c) 努力する甲斐もなく
6. Arrange into a correct sentence: が最後 / 飲んだ / 酒を / 止まらなくなった → ______

### Answer key

1. 甲斐もなく
2. 最後
3. 彼の努力は甲斐もなく終わった。
4. unavoidable
5. (c) — the "in vain" pattern uses noun + の甲斐もなく, not the dictionary form
6. 酒を飲んだが最後、止まらなくなった。

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜甲斐もなく is always preceded by noun + の. If the question gives a verb, convert it into a noun first (努力する → 努力の).
- 〜たが最後 must use the ta-form. The dictionary form (〜るが最後) is a wrong trap.
- The positive counterpart 〜た甲斐があった is often tested alongside 〜の甲斐もなく — memorize both at once.

## Summary

- 〜甲斐もなく (noun + の): "in vain" — effort without result; its counterpart is 〜た甲斐があった.
- 〜たが最後 (ta-form verb +): "once … then …" — unavoidable consequence; often sounds negative.
- Key for questions: の甲斐もなく uses a noun; が最後 uses the ta-form.
