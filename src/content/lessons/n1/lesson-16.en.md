## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜にかこつけて to expose a pretext used to cover one's true intent, and 〜にかまけて to express being so absorbed that one neglects obligations. You can also distinguish them, as both are negative in nuance but their logic differs.

## Explanation

**1. 〜にかこつけて (under the pretext of).** Formula: noun + にかこつけて. Derived from かこつける (to use as a pretext). Used when someone uses A as an excuse to do B, which is what they really want. The nuance is negative: the speaker doubts the sincerity of the excuse. The neutral equivalent is を口実に, while 〜を名目に is more formal.

- 出張にかこつけて 旅行する。(Under the pretext of a business trip, he went sightseeing.)

**2. 〜にかまけて (absorbed in).** Formula: noun + にかまけて. Used when someone is so absorbed in A that they neglect B (duties, health, family). The pattern: A にかまけて、B をおろそかにする. Unlike the neutral-positive に熱中して, 〜にかまけて is always followed by a bad consequence.

- 仕事にかまけて 健康を 損ねた。(Absorbed in work, he ruined his health.)

Distinguish: かこつけて = a false excuse for a hidden motive; かまけて = absorption to the point of neglecting obligations.

## Example sentences

1. 〔日常〕 出張にかこつけて、温泉に 寄った。(Under the pretext of a business trip, he stopped by a hot spring.)
2. 〔報道〕 視察にかこつけた 外遊だと 批判された。(Criticized as an overseas trip under the pretext of an inspection.)
3. 〔文語〕 仕事にかまけて、健康を 損ねた。(Absorbed in work, he ruined his health.)
4. 〔日常〕 ゲームにかまけて、宿題を 忘れた。(So absorbed in games that he forgot his homework.)
5. 〔文語〕 研究にかまけて 家庭を かえりみない 学者が いた。(There was a scholar so absorbed in research that he paid no attention to his family.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 開発にかまけて、顧客対応が おろそかになった。(Absorbed in development, customer service was neglected.)
7. 〔日常〕 忙しさにかこつけて、連絡を 怠った。(Under the pretext of busyness, he neglected to keep in touch.)

## Tips / key patterns

- After 〜にかこつけて, an activity enjoyable for the doer usually follows (旅行・温泉・外遊): that is the "pretext" clue.
- After 〜にかまけて, a bad consequence always follows (健康を損ねる・おろそかになる): if the consequence is good, the pattern is wrong — use に熱中して.
- 〜にかこつけた (attributive form) can modify a noun: 視察にかこつけた外遊.

## Summary

- 〜にかこつけて (noun +): "under the pretext of", a false excuse covering the true intent; suspicious nuance.
- 〜にかまけて (noun +): "absorbed in", too engrossed to the point of neglecting obligations; followed by a bad consequence.
- Quick test: "is there a hidden motive?" Yes → かこつけて. "Is there a neglected obligation?" Yes → かまけて.
- Kanji: 口実, 蟷螂, 蜥蜴, 鰐, 鮑, 茸, 鞘.
