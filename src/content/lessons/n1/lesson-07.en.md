## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use two ultra-formal expressions correctly: 謹んで to convey something important with full respect, and 恐れながら as a cushion before speaking to a superior. Misusing either sounds odd to native speakers.

## Explanation

**1. 謹んで (つつしんで).** Meaning: "with full respect and humility". Used when conveying something important from your own side: official announcements, apologies, condolences, and formal thanks. Pattern: 謹んで + 申し上げる/お詫び申し上げる/ご報告申し上げる. Direction: humbling yourself.

**2. 恐れながら (おそれながら).** Meaning: "with deference, forgive my presumption". Used as a cushion before giving an opinion, making a request, or raising an objection to a superior. Pattern: 恐れながら + 申し上げます/お願い申し上げます. Direction: elevating the listener.

Distinguish: 謹んで = a standalone attitude of respect (announcements, apologies, mourning); 恐れながら = an opener that "asks permission" before the content of what you say. They are not interchangeable: congratulations use 謹んで, not 恐れながら.

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 謹んで新年のお慶びを申し上げます。(Respectfully, we offer our New Year greetings.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 この度の不手際につきまして、謹んでお詫び申し上げます。(For the mishap on this occasion, we humbly offer our apology.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 恐れながら、一言申し上げます。(Forgive my presumption; allow me to say one thing.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 恐れながら、資料の再送をお願い申し上げます。(With deference, we ask that the documents be resent.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 ご逝去の報に接し、謹んで哀悼の意を表します。(Upon hearing the sad news, we respectfully express our condolences.)
6. 〔日常〕 「恐れながら」は目上に意見を言う前のクッション言葉だよ。(恐れながら is a cushioning phrase before giving an opinion to a superior.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 皆様のご健勝を、謹んでお祈り申し上げます。(Respectfully, we pray for the good health of you all.)

## Tips / key patterns

- The standard pairings: 謹んで + 申し上げる (announcements/apologies/mourning/congratulations), 恐れながら + 申し上げます/お願い申し上げます (opinions/requests to a superior).
- 恐れながら without anything following sounds unfinished — always follow it with the point of what you're saying.
- In letters, 謹んで often appears in the 前文 or 末文; 恐れながら appears in the 主文 before an important request.

## Summary

- 謹んで: with full respect — for announcements, apologies, condolences, congratulations. Humbles yourself.
- 恐れながら: with deference — a cushion before an opinion or request to a superior. Elevates the listener.
- The two are not interchangeable; watch the direction of respect.
