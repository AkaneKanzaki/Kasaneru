## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can distinguish the two classical past forms 〜き and 〜けり in literary texts: 〜き for memories of personal experience, 〜けり for hearsay or a poetic tone. Like the previous lesson, both are for reading recognition only.

## Explanation

**1. 〜き (classical past).** Formula: 連用形 form + き for verbs, adjective stem + き for adjectives. Meaning: "has/had" with a nuance of personal remembered experience. Examples: 美し-き思い出 (a beautiful memory), 若き日 (youthful days), 降り-き (has fallen — I remember).

**2. 〜けり (classical conjectural past).** Formula: 連用形 form + けり. Meaning: "has/had" with a nuance of hearsay or an admiring poetic tone. Example: あり-けり (they say there was). The folktale opening 昔々…ありけり uses this pattern.

Distinguish: 〜き = "I experienced it and remember it", 〜けり = "so they say" or a poetic "indeed it has ...". Both are only recognized while reading, not produced in your own writing.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・kuno〕 美しき 思い出が 今も 胸に 残っている。(A beautiful memory still remains in my heart.)
2. 〔文語・kuno〕 昔々、山の 村に 老夫婦が ありけり。(Long ago, they say, in a mountain village there lived an old couple.)
3. 〔文学〕 若き 日の 友と 十年ぶりに 再会した。(I was reunited with a friend from my youth after ten years.)
4. 〔文語・kuno〕 遠き 山に 初雪が 降りき。(The first snow has fallen on the distant mountain — I remember.)
5. 〔文学〕 懐かしき 故郷の 川の 音。(The sound of the river of my longed-for hometown.)
6. 〔日常〕 昔話は「むかしむかし、あるところに」で 始まるのが 定番だ。(Folktales typically begin with "long ago, in a certain place".)
7. 〔文学〕 幼き 頃の 夏休みが 懐かしい。(I miss the summer holidays of my childhood.)
8. 〔文語・kuno〕 姫は 静かに 涙を 流しき。(The princess silently shed tears — I remember.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Quick trick: き = known-by-heart memory (both start with k), けり = hearsay. If the sentence is about "my" experience, it's 〜き; if it's a "they say" folktale, it's 〜けり.
- Adjective + き (美しき、若き、懐かしき) still lives as a fossil in poetic modern writing — recognize the pattern.
- Don't mix these up with last lesson's 〜ぬ/〜ず: き/けり are about past time, ぬ/ず about negation.

## Summary

- 〜き: classical past for personal memories (連用形 + き, adjective stem + き) — 美しき思い出、若き日. Recognition only.
- 〜けり: classical past with a hearsay or poetic nuance (連用形 + けり) — ありけり, the folktale opening. Recognition only.
- Distinguish by context: your own experience → き, hearsay or poetic tone → けり.
