## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can compose a Japanese business letter with the correct structure: 拝啓 → 時候の挨拶 → 主文 → 結びの挨拶 → 敬具. You also understand the direction of respect: the writer always humbles themselves.

## Explanation

A complete business letter has five layers. **First**, 頭語: 拝啓, always on its own at the very first line. **Second**, 前文 contains the 時候の挨拶 — the standard seasonal greeting, for example 「時下ますますご清祥のこととお慶び申し上げます」. **Third**, 主文: the body of the matter in correct honorific language, for example 「つきましては、別紙の通りご送付申し上げます」. **Fourth**, 末文 contains the 結びの挨拶, for example 「まずは書面にて御礼申し上げます」. **Fifth**, 結語: 敬具 at the end, paired with 拝啓.

The direction of respect is one-way: the writer humbles themselves (拝受、申し上げる), the recipient is elevated (ご清祥、貴社). Don't mix them up.

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 拝啓 時下ますますご清祥のこととお慶び申し上げます。(Dear Sir/Madam, I hope you are in ever greater health and prosperity.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 さて、先日はお忙しい中お時間をいただき、誠にありがとうございました。(Thank you very much for your time the other day despite your busy schedule.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 つきましては、別紙の通りご送付申し上げますので、ご査収ください。(Accordingly, we are sending the enclosed documents; please kindly review them.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 まずは書面にて御礼申し上げます。敬具(For now, please accept my thanks in writing. Respectfully,)
5. 〔日常〕 敬具は拝啓とセットで使うんだよ。(敬具 is used as a set with 拝啓.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 ご多忙のところ恐れ入りますが、ご返信をお待ちしております。(We apologize for troubling you amid your busy schedule; we look forward to your reply.)
7. 〔文語〕 書面の基本は頭語・前文・主文・末文・結語である。(The basics of a letter are the opening, the body, and the closing.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Memorize the package: 拝啓↔敬具 must not be mixed with other packages (前略↔草々 is covered in L08).
- 時候の挨拶 has standard seasonal formulas — don't invent your own; use the established ones.
- 主文 always starts with a formal connective: さて、つきましては. This is the formal marker that the body has begun.

## Summary

- Structure: 拝啓 → 時候の挨拶 (前文) → 主文 → 結びの挨拶 (末文) → 敬具.
- Direction of respect: the writer humbles themselves, the recipient is elevated.
- 拝啓 and 敬具 are an inseparable pair.
