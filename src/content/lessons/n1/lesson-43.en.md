## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can eliminate distractors made of near-synonyms (words with similar meanings) using 4 differentiators: subject, scope, collocation, and register. This is your main weapon against trap options in 語彙 and 読解.

## Explanation

**Four differentiators to dissect near-synonyms.**

1. **Subject:** who acts? 主張 = a claim the author champions; 意見 = anyone's opinion; 見解 = an institution's official view. A 主張理解 question is never answered with 意見.
2. **Scope:** how broad? 原因 = the specific main cause; 要因 = one of many factors. If the text mentions many factors, the answer is 要因.
3. **Collocation:** which words does it usually pair with? 影響を与える, 効果が上がる, 作用する (medicine). 格差を解消する, 問題を解決する, 困難を克服する. Wrong collocation = distractor.
4. **Register:** 査定 is used for formal appraisals (prices, performance); 評価 for general. N1 loves to swap the two.

**How to eliminate during the exam:** read the question sentence, determine the required subject + collocation, cross out options whose collocation is impossible, then choose between the rest using the scope differentiator. Don't choose because "the meaning is similar" — that similarity is precisely the trap.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語〕 筆者の主張と一般の意見は区別すべきだ。(The author's claim and general opinion must be distinguished.)
2. 〔報道調〕 売上の増加が三期連続で続いている。(Sales growth has continued for three consecutive quarters.)
3. 〔文語〕 水分は生命にとって不可欠である。(Water is indispensable to life.)
4. 〔文語〕 事故の要因は複数あると見られる。(The accident is thought to have multiple contributing factors.)
5. 〔文語〕 この政策は地域経済に好影響を与えた。(This policy had a positive impact on the regional economy.)
6. 〔文語〕 格差の解消には時間がかかる。(Closing the gap takes time.)
7. 〔ビジネス調〕 新規事業を推進するため、専門チームを設けた。(To advance the new business, a specialized team was formed.)

## Exercises

1. Choose the correct one: 売上の___が続いている。(増加 / 上昇)
2. Choose the correct one: この薬の___について調べる。(影響 / 作用)
3. Choose the correct one: 格差の___に取り組む。(解決 / 解消)
4. Choose the correct one: プロジェクトを___する。(促進 / 推進)
5. A question asks for 要因 (factor), and one option reads 「事故の原因は速度超過だ」. This option is wrong because: a) wrong collocation b) scope too narrow — 原因 points to a single cause, not a factor c) wrong register d) wrong subject
6. Choose the correct one: 彼の業績を___する。(評価 / 査定)

### Answer key

1. 増加 — 売上 grows in quantity; 上昇 is for prices/graphs that rise.
2. 作用 — fixed collocation: 薬の作用 (a medicine's action).
3. 解消 — fixed collocation: 格差を解消する.
4. 推進 — fixed collocation: プロジェクトを推進する (to advance).
5. b — 原因 points to the main/single cause; the question asks for 要因 (one of several factors).
6. 査定 — formal performance appraisal; 評価 is too general for this context.

## Tips / important patterns

- Build your own list of fixed collocations (at least 30 pairs) — these are what most often appear as 語彙 distractors.
- When torn between two options, ask: "what words does this word usually pair with?" — not "what does it mean?".
- Beware options that are dictionary-correct but collocationally wrong; those are N1's slickest distractors.

## Summary

- Four near-synonym differentiators: subject, scope, collocation, register.
- Elimination procedure: determine what the sentence demands → cross out impossible collocations → differentiate by scope.
- Fixed collocations (薬の作用, 格差の解消, プロジェクトの推進) are high-value memorization patterns.
