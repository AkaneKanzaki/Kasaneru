## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can execute the second full mock with a strict Real-mode checklist, then assess your readiness with the N1-ready criteria: total ≥100/180 (estimated) + section floors, met in two consecutive mocks.

## Explanation

**Real-mode checklist (before mock #2).** Environment: quiet room, phone off, clean desk. Equipment: answer sheet, timer, spare stationery. Condition: enough sleep, meals like on exam-day plan. Rules: no dictionary, no breaks outside the inter-block rest, no peeking at the key. One point violated = the mock doesn't count as "Real".

**N1-ready criteria.** Declared ready if TWO consecutive mocks meet:
1. Total ≥100/180 (estimated — the official score uses 換算, not raw addition).
2. Each section ≥19/60 (estimated) — no 足切り.
3. Trend not declining: mock #2 ≥ mock #1 (reasonable margin).

**Why two in a row?** One good mock could just mean questions that happened to suit you. 再現性 — the ability to repeat results — is the proof of readiness. If mock #2 drops, go back to L47/L49, not straight to mock #3.

**After mock #2.** Compare per strand with mock #1: what went up, what stagnated? Whatever stagnated goes into the L49 plan.

## Example sentences

1. 〔確認〕 携帯の電源は切ったか。(Is the phone turned off?)
2. 〔基準〕 合計100点、かつ各セクション19点が目安だ。(The benchmark: 100 total, 19 per section.)
3. 〔分析〕 二回連続で基準を満たした。(Met the standard two times in a row.)
4. 〔分析〕 聴解だけが足切りに引っかかった。(Only listening hit the minimum cutoff.)
5. 〔心構え〕 模試の点は推定値にすぎない。(Mock scores are only estimates.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 一回の好調はまぐれかも、二回続けば実力だよ。(One good run may be luck; two in a row is real ability.)

## Exercises

1. Mock #1: total 104, lowest section 21. Mock #2: total 98, lowest section 17. Status: a) ready b) not yet — mock #2 fails the total & floor c) ready with notes d) redo mock #1
2. A mock score is: a) the official score b) an estimate — the official score uses 換算 c) useless d) the same as exam day
3. Checklist violated (opened the dictionary once). Then: a) still counts b) doesn't count as Real c) subtract 5 points d) redo the questions
4. 再現性 means: a) memorizing questions b) the ability to repeat good results c) speed d) number of mocks
5. After mock #2, what's compared with mock #1: a) total only b) per strand: what rose, what stagnated c) question count d) meal timing
6. True or false: mock #2 below criteria → straight to mock #3 tomorrow.

### Answer key

1. b — total <100 and a section <19.
2. b — the official score is a scaled score.
3. b — one violation cancels Real status.
4. b — reproducibility of results is the proof of readiness.
5. b — per-strand analysis determines the plan.
6. False — go back to L47/L49 first.

## Tips / important patterns

- Treat mock #2 as exam day: wake, eat, and prepare like your exam plan.
- Record non-technical conditions too: sleep, meals, distractions — exam day is 30% body management.

## Summary

- Real checklist: environment + equipment + condition + rules; one violation = not Real.
- N1-ready = total ≥100/180 (estimated) + each section ≥19/60 (estimated), in two consecutive mocks.
- One good mock = luck; two in a row = 再現性 = ready.
- Below criteria → L47/L49, not mock #3 right away.
