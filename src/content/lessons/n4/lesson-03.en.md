## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the 〜ながら pattern to express two actions done at the same time, and you can distinguish it from the 〜たり pattern, which expresses alternating actions. You also know which action is the sentence's main focus.

## Explanation

The pattern **〜ながら** means "while ...". To form it: **take the verb's ます form, drop ます, add ながら**.

- うたう → うたい → うたいながら (while singing)
- のむ → のみ → のみながら (while drinking)
- あるく → あるき → あるきながら (while walking)
- みる → み → みながら (while watching)

The important rule: **the action written after ながら is the main action**, while the action before ながら is secondary. Example: おんがくを ききながら あるく — the main action is walking, the music is just the accompaniment.

Don't confuse it with 〜たり〜たり: ながら is used for two actions happening **at the same time**, while 〜たり is used for actions happening **alternately** or as examples of activities.

This lesson's three kanji: 拾 (ひろう = to pick up), 捨 (すてる = to throw away), 払 (はらう = to pay).

## Example sentences

1. わたしは おんがくを ききながら あるきます。(I walk while listening to music.)
2. シンタさんは うたいながら りょうりを します。(Sinta cooks while singing.)
3. ブディさんは コーヒーを のみながら しんぶんを よみます。(Budi reads the newspaper while drinking coffee.)
4. かれは あるきながら でんわを します。(He makes a phone call while walking.)
5. かのじょは なきながら はなしました。(She spoke while crying.)
6. わたしは みちで ごみを ひろいながら さんぽしました。(I took a walk while picking up trash on the road.)
7. ちちは テレビを みながら ごはんを たべます。(My father eats while watching TV.)
8. わたしは はたらきながら にほんごを べんきょうしています。(I am studying Japanese while working.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: attaching ながら to the dictionary form (うたうながら). The correct way is always from the ます form: うたいながら.
- Remember the order: the main action always comes after ながら. うたいながら あるく = the focus is walking.
- ながら is not used for two contradictory actions or to express a sequence of events — for that, use the て form or 〜たり.
- In the JLPT, questions often test the difference between ながら (simultaneous) and 〜たり (alternating). Watch for "while" vs "sometimes".

## Summary

- 〜ながら = "while ..."; formed from the verb's ます form + ながら.
- The two actions happen at the same time; the action after ながら is the main one.
- Different from 〜たり〜たり, which expresses alternating actions or examples of activities.
- Kanji: 拾 (ひろう = to pick up), 捨 (すてる = to throw away), 払 (はらう = to pay).
