## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you'll be able to use 〜そうだ to pass on information from other people ("I heard…"), and to tell it apart precisely from the appearance 〜そうだ in L31 — the twin patterns that are most often confused in N4 tests.

## Explanation

〜そうだ (hearsay) means "I heard…". Use it when the information comes from SOMEONE ELSE — something you heard or a rumor.

Formation: plain form + そうだ, without trimming anything.

- いく → いくそうです (I heard they're going)
- きれいだ → きれいだそうです (I heard it's beautiful)
- おいしい → おいしいそうです (I heard it's delicious — the い is NOT dropped!)
- ない → ないそうです (I heard it's not…)

Compare directly with L31 using this distinguishing table:

| | 〜そうだ appearance (L31) | 〜そうだ hearsay (L32) |
| Base form | V-ます / i-adjective (drop い) / na-adjective | plain form (+ だ) |
| Meaning | looks like (your own guess) | I heard (someone else's info) |
| Negative verb | ふらなさそうだ | ふらないそうだ |
| i-adjective example | おいしそうだ | おいしいそうだ |

The quick way: if そうだ attaches to a "trimmed" form (ふり, おいし), it's a guess; if it attaches to the full form (ふる, おいしい), it's hearsay.

This lesson's four kanji: 波 (なみ = wave), 岩 (いわ = rock), 桜 (さくら = sakura), 梅 (うめ = plum).

## Example sentences

1. てんきよほうに よると、あしたは あめだそうです。(According to the weather forecast, I heard it will rain tomorrow.)
2. Budiは あした やすむそうです。(I heard Budi is taking tomorrow off.)
3. さくらが もう さいたそうです。(I heard the sakura have already bloomed.)
4. うめの はなは とても きれいだそうです。(I heard plum blossoms are very beautiful.)
5. きょうの なみは たかいそうです。サーフィンは やめましょう。(I heard the waves are high today. Let's skip surfing.)
6. さくらが さきそうです。(The sakura look like they'll bloom — I saw the buds myself.)
7. さくらが さいたそうです。(I heard the sakura have bloomed — a friend told me.)
8. いわの うえに たつと、うみが よく みえるそうです。(I heard the sea looks great from on top of the rocks.)

## Practice

1. Change to hearsay 〜そうだ: やすむ → ______そうです。
2. Change: きれいだ → ______
3. Change: おいしい → ______
4. Change negative: いかない → ______
5. Choose the one that means "I heard": あめが（ふりそうだ / ふるそうだ）。
6. Translate into Japanese: "I heard Sinta is sick." (sick = びょうきだ)
7. Distinguish: write the appearance form of たかい → ______, and the hearsay form → ______.
8. Fix: さくらが さきそうだそうです → write the correct one for the meaning "I heard they've bloomed".

### Answer key

1. やすむ
2. きれいだそうです
3. おいしいそうです
4. いかないそうです
5. ふるそうだ
6. Sintaは びょうきだそうです。
7. たかそうだ / たかいそうだ
8. さくらが さいたそうです。

## Tips / key patterns

- A favorite test trap: ないそうだ is CORRECT for hearsay negatives, but WRONG for guess negatives (a guess must be なさそうだ).
- Hearsay marker words: 〜によると, うわさでは, きいたところでは. When you see these, it's almost certainly hearsay 〜そうだ.
- After hearsay 〜そうだ, the original sentence stays in plain form — don't "trim" it like L31.
- Hearsay sentences very often end simply with 〜そうです in polite form.

## Summary

- Plain form + そうだ = "I heard".
- i-adjectives, na-adjectives, nouns: the い and だ are NOT dropped.
- Negative: ないそうです (different from L31: なさそうだ).
- The key to telling L31/L32 apart: "trimmed" form = guess; full form = hearsay.
- Kanji: 波 (なみ), 岩 (いわ), 桜 (さくら), 梅 (うめ).
