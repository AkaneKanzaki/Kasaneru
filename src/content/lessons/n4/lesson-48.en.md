## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you are ready to run your second full mock in Real mode (115 minutes, conditions as close to the real exam as possible) and judge the N4-ready criteria yourself.

## Explanation

**Checklist the day before:** a quiet room for 115 minutes with no interruptions (tell your household); device fully charged + notifications off; paper and pencil for 4K notes; enough sleep (a tired brain drops its listening score first); eat moderately 1–2 hours before starting.

**Checklist while working:** a 115-minute timer from the first question, no breaks between sections; pacing Vocabulary 25' / Grammar + Reading 55' / Listening 35'; Listening audio EXACTLY once; mark uncertain questions, maximum 60 seconds per question; fill in answers directly per question; nothing left blank (no penalty for wrong answers); when done → rest 10 minutes, then review.

**N4-ready criteria (all must be met, as estimates):**

1. Estimated total ≥ 90 out of 180.
2. Language Knowledge + Reading above the estimated threshold (±38/120).
3. Listening above the estimated threshold (±19/60) — no section may be below the threshold even if the total passes.
4. Two full mocks in a row pass all the criteria above.
5. No section ran out of time.

Note: app scores are ESTIMATES, not official JLPT scores. Aim slightly above the thresholds.

## Example sentences

The correct pacing for 115 minutes:

- 0–25: Vocabulary (±60 seconds/question). At minute 25: stop, move on.
- 25–50: Grammar. Read the sentence to the end first, then look at the options.
- 50–80: Reading. Short texts first, medium text last (L41–L42).
- 80–115: Listening. Read the questions before the audio, note the 4Ks, answer immediately.
- Remaining time per section: return to marked questions, DON'T switch sections.

Common failure: Listening starts in a panic because reading overran by 15 minutes. The solution is the discipline to move on when the block timer runs out — like a proctor collecting the papers.

## Practice

1. Name 3 differences of Real mode compared to Study mode.
2. Stuck 90 seconds on one question in Real mode — what do you do?
3. Name the 5 N4-ready criteria + the note about the scores.
4. Scenario: estimated total 95/180, Listening 15/60. Ready? Next step?

### Answer key

1. 115 minutes with no breaks between sections; audio exactly once; no dictionary/explanations.
2. Mark and move on — 90 seconds robs the time of 2–3 other questions. Return at the end if time allows.
3. Total ≥90/180; LK + Reading > ±38/120; Listening > ±19/60; pass two mocks in a row; no section ran out of time. App scores = ESTIMATES.
4. Not ready yet — one section below the threshold is disqualifying. Fill in the L47 clinic table, fix listening for one round (L43–L44), repeat Real mode.

## Tips / key patterns

- Schedule Real mode when you're fresh (morning/afternoon). An evening mock gives a score below your true ability.
- Being nervous is normal; what counts is your obedience to pacing. Trust the timer, not your feelings.
- Strategy is decided BEFORE the timer starts; don't change it mid-way.

## Summary

- Real mode = 115 minutes in one go, no breaks, no dictionary, audio played once.
- Pacing: Vocabulary 25', Grammar + Reading 55', Listening 35'.
- Fill in answers directly; mark the uncertain ones; leave nothing blank.
- N4-ready: estimated total ≥90/180 + each section above the threshold + two consecutive passes + no section ran out of time.
- Not passed yet → error clinic (L47) → fix → repeat.
