## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you know how to use Study mode for your first full mock: time allocation per section, strategy per section, how to use the explanations, and how to log error patterns. This lesson contains strategy — the questions come from the app's question bank.

## Explanation

**Study mode** = a full N4 mock under study conditions: pauses allowed between sections, explanations viewable after answering, timer as a guide. The goal is mapping your ability — its data is used in L47 (error clinic).

**Official N4 times (115 minutes):** Vocabulary 25 minutes, Grammar + Reading 55 minutes, Listening 35 minutes. In Study mode, split the middle block: 25 minutes grammar + 30 minutes reading. Record your actual time per section.

**Estimated target scores (practice benchmarks):** total ≥90/180 with each section above its estimated threshold — Language Knowledge + Reading above ±38/120, Listening above ±19/60. Realistic target for the first mock: LK + Reading ≥50/120 and Listening ≥30/60 (estimated). Remember: app scores are ESTIMATES, not official JLPT scores (statistical scaling).

**Using the explanations:** read them for wrong answers AND correct-but-uncertain answers. Each problematic question → one log line: section | question type | cause. Example: "Reading | main idea | chose a detail as the answer".

## Example sentences

Strategy per section:

**Vocabulary (25').** Work from what you know best first; maximum 60 seconds/question; stuck → mark and move on.

**Grammar + Reading (55').** Grammar (25'): read the sentence to the END before looking at the options. Reading (30'): apply L41–L42.

**Listening (35').** The L43 protocol (read questions first, note the 4Ks) + watch for the 4 distractors from L44.

The wrong example: 40 minutes spent on grammar, reading done in a 15-minute panic. Split the middle block with a timer: when the 25 grammar minutes are up → move to reading.

## Practice

1. The official time allocation per N4 section + the Study-mode split of the middle block?
2. How does Study mode differ from Real mode regarding pauses and explanations?
3. Besides wrong answers, what other questions must have their explanations read? Why?
4. The estimated target score for the first mock + the note about app scores?
5. Write the format of one error-log line + one example.

### Answer key

1. Vocabulary 25', Grammar + Reading 55', Listening 35' (total 115'). Middle block: 25' + 30'.
2. Study allows pauses between sections and viewing explanations; Real does not.
3. Correct-but-uncertain questions — "correct by guessing" can fail in the real exam.
4. LK + Reading ≥50/120, Listening ≥30/60, total ≥90/180 (estimated). App scores = ESTIMATES, not official scores.
5. section | question type | cause. Example: "Grammar | conditionals | mixed up たら/ば".

## Tips / key patterns

- A good Study mode produces an improvement list, not just a number.
- Don't repeat the same mock — memorized questions ruin the measurement. Use a different set.
- Breaks between sections are allowed; record them so the comparison with Real mode is honest.
- Reading an explanation without writing an action = not learning. Write the action.

## Summary

- Study mode = a full mock to map your ability; pauses and explanations allowed.
- Official times: Vocabulary 25', Grammar + Reading 55' (25+30), Listening 35'.
- Target: estimated total ≥90/180, each section above its threshold with margin.
- One log line per problematic question — the raw material for L47.
