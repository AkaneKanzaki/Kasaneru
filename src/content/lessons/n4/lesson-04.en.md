## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can list several reasons at once with the 〜し pattern, and you understand its nuance: there are more reasons left unsaid.

## Explanation

The pattern **〜し** is used to list several reasons or situations in a row. To form it: **add し to the plain form** (the dictionary form, not the ます form).

- i-adjectives: やすい → やすいし, あつかった → あつかったし
- na-adjectives: しずかだ → しずかだし (na-adjectives use だ, not な)
- Nouns: がくせいだ → がくせいだし
- Verbs: たべる → たべるし, たべない → たべないし

The important nuance: with 〜し, the speaker gives the impression that **"there are more reasons I haven't mentioned"**. That's why 〜し is often used for complaints or reasons with a somewhat emotional tone.

This lesson's three kanji: 届 (とどく = to arrive), 追 (おいかける = to chase), 逃 (にげる = to run away).

## Example sentences

1. この しょくどうは やすいし、おいしいし、よく いきます。(This cafeteria is cheap and tasty, so I go there often.)
2. きのうは あつかったし、つかれたし、はやく ねました。(Yesterday was hot and I was tired, so I went to bed early.)
3. この へやは せまいし、くらいし、すみたく ありません。(This room is cramped and dark; I don't want to live here.)
4. かれは しんせつだし、あたまが いいし、みんなに すかれています。(He's kind and smart, so everyone likes him.)
5. あの みせは とおいし、たかいし、いきません。(That shop is far and expensive, so I don't go there.)
6. こどもが びょうきだし、わたしも つかれているし、きょうは やすみます。(My child is sick and I'm tired too, so I'm taking today off.)
7. にほんごは むずかしいし、かんじも おおいし、でも おもしろいです。(Japanese is difficult and has lots of kanji, but it's interesting.)
8. バスが こないし、タクシーも ないし、あるいて いきました。(The bus didn't come and there were no taxis, so I walked.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: using な for na-adjectives (しずかなし). The correct form uses だ: しずかだし.
- 〜し attaches to the plain form, not the ます form. Wrong: たべますし. Right: たべるし.
- Distinguish it from 〜から, which gives one firm reason. 〜し lists several reasons with an "and so on" nuance.
- In the JLPT, 〜し often appears in complaint dialogues. Pay attention to the tone of the sentence.

## Summary

- 〜し = listing several reasons in a row; formed as plain form + し.
- Na-adjectives and nouns use だし (しずかだし, がくせいだし).
- The nuance: there are more reasons left unsaid; often with a complaining tone.
- Different from 〜から, which states one firm reason.
- Kanji: 届 (とどく = to arrive), 追 (おいかける = to chase), 逃 (にげる = to run away).
