## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you have an objective final-readiness checklist, a concrete exam-day plan, and a final sweep list for the day before the exam.

## Explanation

**Final readiness checklist (all must be YES):**

- [ ] Two consecutive passing mocks: estimated total ≥90/180 AND each section above the estimated threshold (±38/120 LK+Reading, ±19/60 Listening).
- [ ] No section ran out of time in either of those mocks.
- [ ] Official-sample calibration (L49) valid: per-section gap ≤±10%, no section below the threshold.
- [ ] Your top 2 weakness patterns (L47) are fixed, not just recorded.
- [ ] You can explain your own strategy: pacing, when to skip, the first 5 minutes.

**Exam-day plan:** the night before, sleep 7–8 hours (no new grammar patterns); pack your bag — exam admission ticket, ID card, 2B pencils + eraser + sharpener, silent watch, drinking water, a snack. Morning: eat as usual, leave with 30–45 minutes of leeway. **First 5 minutes of each section:** Vocabulary — the 3 easiest questions first; Grammar + Reading — write "25' / 30'" on your scratch paper and obey it; Listening — prepare your 4K notes, read the first part's questions before the sample audio ends.

**Iron rule:** no last-minute strategies — repeat L41–L50.

## Example sentences

The final sweep list (1 day before the exam, maximum 2 hours — review, not new study):

**High-frequency N4 grammar:** conditionals と・ば・たら・なら | 受け身・可能・使役 | 〜そうだ伝聞 vs 様態 | 敬語: honorific/humble pairs + who→whom | 〜てあげる・もらう・くれる | 〜ておく・〜てしまう・〜てみる | 〜ないで・〜なくてもいい・なければならない | 〜たことがある・〜ようになる・〜ことにする | 〜はずだ・〜わけだ・〜みたいだ・〜らしい | 〜ために・〜ように | 〜ても・〜のに | 〜すぎる・〜やすい/にくい | 〜し・〜たり〜たり・〜ながら | 〜ばよかった・〜てよかった.

**High-frequency vocabulary:** 自動詞/他動詞 pairs (あく/あける, きまる/きめる, つく/つける, なおる/なおす) | similar sounds (ビル/ビール, おじさん/おじいさん) | verb + fixed particle (〜に慣れる, 〜に間に合う, 〜を心配する) | notice & email vocabulary (L41) | numbers, time, counters (〜時半, 〜週間, 〜回).

The sweep: per pattern → write ONE example sentence from memory. Can → mastered; can't → mark it, don't learn anything new.

## Practice

Answer honestly (YES/NO):

1. Last two mocks: estimated total ≥90/180 and all sections above the threshold.
2. In both of those mocks, no section ran out of time.
3. I know my 2 weakness patterns and have fixed them.
4. The day before the exam: (a) stay up late memorizing new vocabulary, (b) sleep enough + light review?
5. The correct first five minutes of the Listening section are...?

### Answer key

1. Must be YES. If NO — repeat L47–L48 for one round; don't register yet.
2. Must be YES. If NO — it's a rhythm problem; repeat the L41–L42 and L45 drills.
3. Must be YES. If NO — fix the 2 most frequent patterns first (the 2-pattern rule, L47).
4. (b) — new material the day before the exam only adds anxiety.
5. Prepare your 4K notes and read the first part's questions before the sample audio ends.

## Tips / key patterns

- "Ready" is PROVEN by data (two mocks + calibration), not felt.
- One checklist still NO a week before registration closes → postpone to the next period.
- Don't compare your speed with other examinees in the exam room.

## Summary

- Readiness = two consecutive passing mocks (total + each section, estimated) + safe pacing + valid calibration + 2 patterns fixed.
- Exam day: sleep 7–8 hours, bag packed the night before, arrive 30–45 minutes early.
- First 5 minutes: easiest questions first, write the time split, prepare 4K notes.
- The day before: light sweep ≤2 hours, no new material.

## Task & SRS notes

Check off your final readiness checklist this week. Write your exam-day plan on one small card. Add to your SRS deck: 最終確認、前日、当日、心構え、頻出、自信を持つ.
