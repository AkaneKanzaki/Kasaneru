## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can change verbs of all three groups into the causative form 〜させる. You understand its two meanings — "to force someone" and "to let/allow someone" — and can choose the particle を or に correctly.

## Explanation

The causative form means "to make/force someone do something" or "to let someone do something". The subject is the party who orders, and the party being ordered is marked with を or に.

**Forming the causative:**

- **Group 1** — a-sound + せる: たつ → たたせる, のむ → のませる, なく → なかせる, あそぶ → あそばせる.
- **Group 2** — drop る, add させる: たべる → たべさせる, みる → みさせる, ねる → ねさせる.
- **Group 3** — memorize: する → **させる**, くる → **こさせる**.

**The two meanings and their particles** — this is a favorite exam point:

- **を = to force**: せんせいは せいとを たたせた (the teacher made the student stand — an order).
- **に = to allow**: ははは こどもに テレビを みさせた (the mother let the child watch TV — permission).

The formula: a tone of "just do it!" → を; a tone of "go ahead" → に. Don't mix them up: させる (causative, "to make someone do") vs される (passive, "to be subjected to").

The eight kanji of this lesson: 難 (むずかしい = difficult), 失 (うしなう = to lose), 芸 (げい = performing arts), 欠 (かける = to become incomplete), 結 (むすぶ = to tie), 健 (すこやか = healthy), 告 (つげる = to inform), 砂 (すな = sand).

## Example sentences

1. せんせいは せいとを たたせた。(The teacher made the student stand.)
2. ははは こどもに テレビを みさせた。(The mother let her child watch TV.)
3. あねは わたしを なかせた。(My older sister made me cry.)
4. しゃちょうは しゃいんに やすみを とらせた。(The company president let the employees take leave.)
5. わたしは おとうとに ケーキを かわせた。(I let my younger brother buy cake.)
6. ちちは わたしを りゅうがくさせた。(My father sent me to study abroad.)
7. ははは あかちゃんが 健(すこ)やかに そだつように ねがった。(My mother prayed that the baby would grow up healthy.)
8. かれは やくそくを まもらず、しんらいを 失(うしな)った。(He didn't keep his promise and lost people's trust.)

## Tips / key patterns

- を to force, に to allow. If the question's context is "ordered even though unwilling", the answer is を.
- くる becomes こさせる (not きさせる). It parallels its passive form: こられる.
- Don't mix them up: させる = to make someone do (active), される = to be subjected to (passive). Pronounce the difference clearly when memorizing.
- Causatives of transitive verbs (whose を is already used for the object) use に for the person: こどもに テレビを みさせた.

## Summary

- Group 1: a-sound + せる (たたせる, のませる). Group 2: drop る + させる.
- Group 3: する → させる, くる → こさせる.
- Dual meaning: を = to force, に = to allow. An exam favorite.
- Distinguish させる (causative) from される (passive).
