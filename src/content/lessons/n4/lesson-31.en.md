## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you'll be able to use 〜そうだ to guess what looks like it's about to happen or how something appears to look, based directly on what you see yourself. You'll also be able to tell it apart from the 〜そうだ that means "I heard" — we'll cover that thoroughly in L32.

## Explanation

〜そうだ (appearance) means "it looks…" or "it seems about to…". Use it when you're GUESSING from what you directly SEE or feel — not from someone else's report.

Formation:

- Verb ます-form (drop ます) + そうだ: ふります → ふりそうです (it looks like it's about to fall)
- i-adjective (drop い) + そうだ: おいしい → おいしそうです, さむい → さむそうです
- na-adjective (unchanged, without だ) + そうだ: きれい → きれいそうです, げんき → げんきそうです

WARNING — the negative verb form does NOT use ないそうだ. The rule is: ない-form (drop い) + さそうだ.

- ふらない → ふらなさそうです (it looks like it won't fall) ✓
- ふらないそうです ✗ (this reads as "I heard it won't fall" — that's the L32 pattern!)

This lesson's five kanji: 雪 (ゆき = snow), 雲 (くも = cloud), 星 (ほし = star), 島 (しま = island), 岸 (きし = shore).

## Example sentences

1. そらを みると、ゆきが ふりそうです。(Looking at the sky, it looks like snow is about to fall.)
2. くもが おおいですね。あめが ふりそうです。(There are a lot of clouds. It looks like rain is about to fall.)
3. この カレーは からそうですが、あじは ちょうど いいです。(This curry looks spicy, but the taste is just right.)
4. よるに なると、ほしが きれいそうです。(Once night comes, the stars look beautiful.)
5. しまへ いく ふねは こみそうです。はやく いきましょう。(The boat to the island looks full. Let's leave early.)
6. あしたは あめが ふらなさそうです。ピクニックに いけますね。(Tomorrow it looks like rain won't fall. We can have a picnic.)
7. きしの ちかくで かぜが つよく ふきそうです。(Near the shore the wind looks like it'll blow hard.)
8. Sintaは さびしそうです。ともだちと あそびに いきましょう。(Sinta looks lonely. Let's go play with our friends.)

## Tips / key patterns

- A classic mistake Indonesian learners make: writing ないそうだ for a negative guess. Remember the rule: ない → なさそうだ.
- Appearance 〜そうだ is always based on your own observation. If the information comes from someone else, it's the hearsay 〜そうだ (L32).
- A small exception that often appears in tests: いい → よさそうです (looks good), not いいそうだ.
- Polite form: 〜そうです. Plain form: 〜そうだ.

## Summary

- V-ます + そうだ / i-adjective (drop い) + そうだ / na-adjective + そうだ = "it looks like".
- Negative verb: ない → なさそうだ (not ないそうだ).
- Always based on your own observation, not someone else's report.
- Kanji: 雪 (ゆき), 雲 (くも), 星 (ほし), 島 (しま), 岸 (きし).
