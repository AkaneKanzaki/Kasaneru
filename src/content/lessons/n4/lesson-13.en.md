## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the conditional と for certain or natural consequences, and 〜たら for general conditions — the difference between them often appears in JLPT questions.

## Explanation

**と** is used when the consequence is certain to happen or is natural, like a law of nature or a habit that always repeats. The pattern: plain form + と.

- はるに なると さくらが さきます (when spring comes, the cherry blossoms will bloom)
- あめが ふると みちが ぬれます (when rain falls, the road gets wet)
- おゆを いれると おちゃに なります (when you pour hot water, it becomes tea)

The hallmark of と: the sentence after it must not contain a command, an invitation, or the speaker's desire — はるに なると いきましょう is wrong.

**〜たら** is used for general conditions: "when… / if…". The pattern: た form + ら.

- えきに ついたら でんわしてください (when you arrive at the station, please call me)
- この くすりを のんだら すぐに ねてください (after you take this medicine, go to sleep right away)
- あした ひまだったら いっしょに カフェに いきませんか (if you're free tomorrow, shall we go to a café together?)

Unlike と, after 〜たら you may have a command, an invitation, or a desire — for example the second half can contain ください, ましょう, or たいです.

This lesson's kanji: 頃 — read ころ or ごろ, meaning "around", for example しちじごろ (around seven o'clock).

## Example sentences

1. はるに なると さくらが さきます。(When spring comes, the cherry blossoms bloom.)
2. あめが ふると みちが ぬれます。(When rain falls, the road gets wet.)
3. おゆを いれると おちゃに なります。(When you pour hot water, it becomes tea.)
4. えきに ついたら わたしに れんらくしてください。(When you arrive at the station, please contact me.)
5. この くすりを のんだら すぐに ねてください。(After you take this medicine, please sleep right away.)
6. あした ひまだったら いっしょに カフェに いきませんか。(If you're free tomorrow, shall we go to a café together?)
7. デパートの まえを とおったら おおきい ほんやが みえます。(If you pass in front of the department store, you'll see a big bookstore.)
8. しちじごろに おきたら あさごはんを たべます。(If I wake up around seven, I have breakfast.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Quick rule: certain or natural consequence → と; command, invitation, or desire after it → 〜たら.
- と cannot be followed by ください, ましょう, or たいです.
- 〜たら uses the た form, so master the た form first.
- Na-adjectives and nouns use だったら, for example ひまだったら, あめだったら.

## Summary

- と = condition with certain or natural consequence; cannot be followed by commands or invitations.
- 〜たら = た form + ら; general condition "when…", can be followed by commands and invitations.
- Second half containing ください or ましょう → choose 〜たら.
- Kanji: 頃 (ころ、ごろ) = around (a time).
