## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜てしまう for its two meanings: fully completed and regret over something that has already happened. You'll also learn its casual forms, 〜ちゃう and 〜じゃう, which often appear in conversation and listening questions.

## Explanation

**〜てしまいます** has two different meanings, and the sentence's context decides which one.

**Meaning 1: fully completed.** States that a task is completely finished, often with the word もう or ぜんぶ.

- しゅくだいを もう してしまいました (I've finished my homework)
- ごはんを ぜんぶ たべてしまいました (I've eaten all the rice)

**Meaning 2: already happened / regret.** States that something undesired has already happened, and the speaker regrets it. Often with the word うっかり.

- でんしゃの なかで さいふを わすれてしまいました (I left my wallet on the train — I regret it)
- うっかり ぎゅうにゅうを こぼしてしまいました (I carelessly spilled the milk)

In casual conversation, 〜てしまう is shortened: て forms ending in て become **〜ちゃう**, those ending in で become **〜じゃう**.

- たべてしまう → たべちゃう, してしまう → しちゃう
- のんでしまう → のんじゃう, よんでしまう → よんじゃう

These casual forms are not used in formal situations or with respected people.

This lesson's five kanji: 混雑 (こんざつ) = crowded and packed, 危険 (きけん) = dangerous, 橋 (はし) = bridge.

## Example sentences

1. しゅくだいを もう してしまいました。(I've finished my homework.)
2. ごはんを ぜんぶ たべてしまいました。(I've eaten all the rice.)
3. でんしゃの なかで さいふを わすれてしまいました。(I left my wallet on the train.)
4. こんざつした バスの なかで かさを なくしてしまいました。(On the packed bus, I lost my umbrella.)
5. はしの うえは きけんですから、はしらないでください。(It's dangerous on the bridge, so please don't run.)
6. あめが ふってきて、くつが ぬれてしまいました。(It suddenly started raining and my shoes got wet.)
7. うっかり ぎゅうにゅうを こぼしてしまいました。(I carelessly spilled the milk.)
8. おなかが すいたから、ケーキを たべちゃった。(I was hungry, so I ate the whole cake.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Decide the meaning from context: a desired result → fully completed; a harmful result → regret.
- The keywords もう and ぜんぶ usually mark the "fully completed" meaning; うっかり marks the "already happened" meaning.
- 〜ちゃう / 〜じゃう are only for casual situations. In exams or formal letters, keep writing 〜てしまう.
- Be careful when writing: たべてしまう becomes たべちゃう (not たべじゃう), のんでしまう becomes のんじゃう (not のんちゃう).

## Summary

- 〜てしまいます = (1) fully completed, (2) already happened / regret.
- Casual forms: 〜ちゃう (from て) and 〜じゃう (from で).
- Context decides the meaning: desired → completed; harmful → regret.
- Kanji: 混雑 (こんざつ), 危険 (きけん), 橋 (はし).
