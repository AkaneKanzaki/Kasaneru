## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can form the causative-passive 〜させられる, meaning "to be forced to do something". You can also use 〜ずに ("without doing …") and 〜たびに ("every time …") with the correct forms.

## Explanation

**1. Causative-passive 〜させられる** = "to be forced to do". It's easy to make: take the causative form, then add られる.

- たべる → たべさせる → **たべさせられる** (to be forced to eat)
- のむ → のませる → **のませられる** (to be forced to drink)
- する → させる → **させられる** (to be made to do)
- くる → こさせる → **こさせられる** (to be forced to come)
- かく → かかせる → **かかせられる** (to be forced to write)

The subject is the party being forced; the party doing the forcing is marked with に: わたしは ははに やさいを たべさせられた ("my mother forced me to eat vegetables"). The nuance is always negative. In conversation, させられる is sometimes shortened to される, but for the JLPT use the full form.

**2. 〜ずに** = "without doing …". Form it from the ない form: change ない to ず, then add に.

- たべない → たべ**ずに**, みない → み**ずに**, しない → せ**ずに**, こない → こ**ずに**, いかない → いか**ずに**

Example: あさごはんを たべずに がっこうへ いった ("went to school without breakfast").

**3. 〜たびに** = "every time …". Attach it to the dictionary form: きく → きくたびに, いく → いくたびに, あう → あうたびに. Example: この うたを きくたびに ふるさとを おもいだす ("every time I hear this song, I remember my hometown").

The six kanji of this lesson: 済 (すむ = to be finished), 裁 (さばく = to judge), 札 (さつ = ticket), 刷 (する = to print), 産 (うむ = to give birth), 士 (し = samurai).

## Example sentences

1. ははは わたしに やさいを たべさせられた。(My mother forced me to eat vegetables.)
2. まいにち ざんぎょうを させられる。(Every day I am made to work overtime.)
3. こどもの ころ、ちちに かんじを かかせられた。(When I was little, my father forced me to write kanji.)
4. あさごはんを たべずに がっこうへ いった。(I went to school without breakfast.)
5. じしょを みずに こたえた。(I answered without looking at the dictionary.)
6. この うたを きくたびに ふるさとを おもいだす。(Every time I hear this song, I remember my hometown.)
7. しゅくだいが 済(す)んだら、あそびに いこう。(Once the homework is done, let's go play.)
8. かれは さいばんしょで 裁(さば)かれた。(He was tried in court.)

## Tips / key patterns

- The order: causative first, then passive (たべさせる → たべさせられる).
- Trap: くる becomes こさせられる (not きさせられる), and こずに (not きずに).
- 〜ずに always means "without …". Don't confuse it with 〜ないで, which is similar but a different lesson.
- 〜たびに attaches to the dictionary form, not the ます form. きくたびに, not ききますたびに.

## Summary

- Causative-passive: 〜させられる = "to be forced to do". Made from the causative form + られる.
- The party doing the forcing is marked with に. The nuance is always negative.
- 〜ずに = "without doing", from the ない form (たべずに, せずに, こずに).
- 〜たびに = "every time", attached to the dictionary form.
