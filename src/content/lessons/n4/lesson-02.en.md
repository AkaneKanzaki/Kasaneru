## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can turn group 1 (godan) verbs into the potential form, use ことができる as a neutral alternative, and use the standard form こられる for くる.

## Explanation

For group 1 (godan) verbs, the pattern is: **change the final -u sound to -e, then add る**.

- よむ → よめる (can read)
- はなす → はなせる (can speak)
- かく → かける (can write)
- のむ → のめる (can drink)
- あらう → あらえる (can wash)

Two exceptions you must memorize: する → **できる**, and くる → **こられる**. Note: the standard form for くる is こられる, not これる. これる does show up in casual speech, but it's considered non-standard and should not be used in the JLPT.

Besides the potential form, there is the pattern **〜ことができる**, which also means "can": dictionary form of the verb + ことができる. This pattern is more neutral and formal, while the potential form is more concise and casual.

- ギターが ひける。 (can play the guitar — concise, casual)
- ギターを ひくことが できる。 (can play the guitar — neutral, formal)

This lesson's three kanji: 終 (おわる = to end), 選 (えらぶ = to choose), 探 (さがす = to look for).

## Example sentences

1. ブディさんは にほんごが はなせます。(Budi can speak Japanese.)
2. シンタさんは ひらがなが よめます。(Sinta can read hiragana.)
3. わたしは さけが のめません。(I can't drink sake.)
4. あした ともだちが うちに こられますか。(Can your friend come to the house tomorrow?)
5. かれは ピアノを ひくことが できます。(He can play the piano.)
6. かのじょは ギターが ひけます。(She can play the guitar.)
7. しけんが 終わったあと、みんなで えいがを みることが できました。(After the exam ended, we could watch a movie together.)
8. きのうは びょうきだったので、かいぎに こられませんでした。(Yesterday I was sick, so I couldn't come to the meeting.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: using これる for くる. The standard form is こられる — memorize it as an exception.
- Careful with かく (to write): its potential form is かける, which sounds identical to the verb かける (to call, to hang). Tell them apart from context.
- 〜ことができる is used with the particle を before the dictionary form: すしを たべることが できる.
- For the JLPT, choose the standard potential forms (よめる, こられる), not the shortened casual forms.

## Summary

- Group 1 potential: final -u sound becomes -e + る (よむ → よめる).
- Exceptions: する → できる, くる → こられる (not これる).
- 〜ことができる = a neutral and formal alternative for saying "can".
- The potential form is more concise and casual; 〜ことができる is more formal.
- Kanji: 終 (おわる = to end), 選 (えらぶ = to choose), 探 (さがす = to look for).
