## Explanation

The clinic table template. Copy it into your notebook and fill it from your mock log (L46):

| Section | Question type | Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar | conditionals | mixed up たら/ば | drill 10 questions, done by tomorrow |

The cause must be SPECIFIC ("don't know the difference between たら and ば" can be fixed; "grammar is weak" can't). The action must have a deadline.

Six typical N4 errors and their fixes:

1. **Mixed-up conditionals (と・ば・たら・なら).** 4-column table + 10-question drill.
2. **Passive vs potential (double 〜られる).** Ask "who is the actor?" — a suffering actor (雨に降られた) = passive; ability = potential. Drill 10 sentences: decide first, then translate.
3. **Double 〜そうだ.** Plain form + そうだ = 伝聞; masu stem + そうだ = 様態. Memorize from the form BEFORE そうだ.
4. **Misdirected keigo.** Rewrite 5 sentences switching subjects (teacher↔me); use the L44 list.
5. **に vs で.** に = point of existence/destination; で = place of action. Underline the verb first.
6. **Similar vocabulary (あく/あける, きまる/きめる).** Pair cards + one example sentence per word, into SRS.

## Practice

Classify the 5 errors below (section | type | cause | action):

1. Chose 降りそうです although the context is "they say it will rain" (weather forecast).
2. Answered a main-idea question with the ticket-price option.
3. In listening, chose 3 o'clock although the dialog decided on 4 o'clock.
4. Chose 私がおっしゃいました in a keigo question.
5. Chose 部屋であきます for the meaning "open the room".

### Answer key

1. Grammar | double そうだ | mixed up 様態/伝聞 | drill 10 questions on the before-そうだ pattern, 2 days.
2. Reading | main idea | chose a detail | L42 skimming (first/last paragraphs), 2 texts.
3. Listening | numbers | chose the canceled initial number | drill 5 dialogs + 4K.
4. Grammar | keigo | misdirected (should be 申します) | rewrite 5 sentences switching subjects.
5. Vocabulary | 自他動詞 | mixed up あく/あける | pair cards + example sentences, SRS today.

## Tips / key patterns

- The 2-pattern rule: fix the 2 most frequent patterns per round, not everything.
- "Ran out of time" = a rhythm problem; the action is timed drills (L41–L42, L45).
- A recurring pattern = your main enemy.
- Don't write "careless" as a cause — that's a symptom. Find the technical cause.
