## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can practice listening with the play-once protocol like the real exam: read the questions first, jot down keywords while the audio plays, and answer right after the audio ends. You also build stamina: full focus throughout a listening block without breaks.

## Explanation

The play-once protocol (use the audio/TTS feature in the app — the audio plays only once, no repeats):

1. **Read the questions and options before the audio plays (20 seconds).** You must already know what information to look for before the first sound comes out.
2. **Note the 4Ks while listening**: **K**apan (time/date), **K**e mana (place), **K**uantitas (numbers: price, time, amount), and **K**eputusan (what was finally decided). Write briefly; abbreviations are fine.
3. **Focus on the end of the conversation.** In 課題理解 and ポイント理解 types, the final decision often comes in the last sentence — and that is usually what gets asked.
4. **Answer immediately after the audio ends.** Don't replay your memory over and over. Build stamina with 25–30 minutes nonstop, like the real listening section (35 minutes).

## Example sentences

A completed sample drill (課題理解 type). Paste this dialog into the app's TTS feature, listen once, then compare your notes with the example below.

> A: もしもし、ブディさん？明日のサッカーの件だけど、雨の予報だから日曜日に変更しない？
> B: いいよ。何時にどこで会う？
> A: 日曜日の午前9時に、駅前の公園で。参加費は一人2万ルピアね。
> B: わかった。じゃ、日曜日の9時、駅前の公園ね。

The correct 4K notes: When = 日曜日午前9時 / Where = 駅前の公園 / Quantity = 2万ルピア / Decision = moved from tomorrow to Sunday. Question: サッカーはいつ、どこで行われますか。→ Answer: 日曜日の午前9時、駅前の公園. Note: "明日" is mentioned at the start but canceled — the final decision is Sunday.

## Practice

Do the 3 scenarios below with the app's TTS feature: listen to each dialog ONCE, note the 4Ks, then check against the key.

**Scenario 1 (課題理解) — library.** A student calls the library because the borrowed book isn't finished yet.

> A: すみません、先週借りた本なんですが、まだ読み終わらなくて。
> B: 返却日は明後日ですね。1週間だけ延長できますよ。
> A: じゃ、お願いします。延滞料金はいくらですか。
> B: 延長は無料です。新しい返却日は来週の火曜日です。
> What to note: the new return date and the fee.

**Scenario 2 (ポイント理解) — station announcement.** Listen to the announcement and note its gist.

> お知らせします。8時15分発の急行は、事故のため20分遅れています。プラットホームが3番から5番に変更になりました。ご注意ください。
> What to note: which train, how late it is, which platform.

**Scenario 3 (会話) — choosing a restaurant.** Two friends plan dinner.

> A: 今度の土曜日、レストランに行かない？イタリアンはどう？
> B: いいね。でも、僕はエビのアレルギーがあるから、エビ料理が多い店はちょっと。
> A: じゃ、駅前のパスタ屋は？エビを使わないメニューもあるし、予算も一人10万ルピアぐらいだよ。
> B: うん、そこにしよう。土曜日の7時に駅で待ち合わせね。
> What to note: the final decision (which restaurant, what time, where to meet).

### Answer key

1. New return date: 来週の火曜日 (next Tuesday); fee: 無料 (free). "明後日" was the old deadline that no longer applies.
2. The 8時15分発の急行; 20分 late; platform changed 3番→5番. Two platform numbers are mentioned — the correct one is the last one.
3. The pasta place in front of the station (has shrimp-free menu items); meet Saturday at 7 at the station; budget about 10万ルピア per person. A generic Italian restaurant was rejected because of the shrimp allergy.

## Tips / key patterns

- Information mentioned early then "canceled" is a classic distractor — always hold the latest version.
- Similar numbers (3番 vs 5番) must be written down as you hear them; don't rely on memory.
- In the ポイント理解 type, listen until the very last sentence; the point of the announcement is there.
- If your notes are empty for a dialog, you were listening passively — repeat with a shorter dialog.

## Summary

- The play-once protocol: read the questions first (20 seconds), note the 4Ks, answer immediately.
- 4K = When, Where, Quantity (numbers), Decision.
- The final decision is usually in the last sentence — listen until the end.
- Stamina = 25–30 minutes nonstop; the real N4 listening section is 35 minutes.
