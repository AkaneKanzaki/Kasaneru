## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can change group 1, 2, and 3 verbs into the volitional form. You can also use it for two purposes: inviting friends to do something ("let's…") and stating your own intention or determination ("I will…").

## Explanation

The volitional form is the verb form for expressing invitations or intentions. The way to make it differs by verb group.

**Group 1** — change the last sound to the お row, then add う:

- かく → かこう, のむ → のもう, あそぶ → あそぼう
- はなす → はなそう, まつ → まとう, かう → かおう
- よむ → よもう, いそぐ → いそごう

**Group 2** — drop る, add よう:

- たべる → たべよう, みる → みよう, ねる → ねよう

**Group 3** — just memorize two:

- する → しよう, くる → こよう

This form has two main functions. First, an **invitation** to the listener: いっしょに えいがを みよう ("let's watch a movie together"). Second, the speaker's own **intention or determination**: あした はやく おきよう ("tomorrow I will wake up early").

You already know 〜ましょう from N5: that form is more polite, for older people or superiors, while the volitional form is more casual for close friends and more natural for stating personal intentions.

This lesson's kanji: 昨 (さく) — used in the word きのう (yesterday).

## Example sentences

1. あした いっしょに プールへ いこう。(Let's go to the pool together tomorrow.)
2. きょうの ばんごはんは わたしが つくろう。(I'll cook dinner tonight.)
3. さむいから まどを しめよう。(It's cold, so let's close the window.)
4. きのうの しゅくだいを もういちど しよう。(Let's do yesterday's homework once more.)
5. こんど ジャカルタへ あそびに いこう。(Next time, let's go have fun in Jakarta.)
6. えきの まえで まちあわせよう。(Let's meet in front of the station.)
7. しんぶんを よんでから ねよう。(Let's sleep after reading the newspaper.)
8. シンタさんが さそってくれたから パーティーに いこう。(Since Sinta invited us, let's go to the party.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: よむ becoming よむう. Remember the pattern: the last sound moves to the お row, then add う — the result is よもう, not よむう.
- Don't use the volitional form with lecturers or respected people; in formal situations keep using 〜ましょう.
- The volitional form often appears before とおもいます to state intentions politely, for example がんばろうと おもいます.

## Summary

- Group 1: last sound → お row + う (かく → かこう). Group 2: drop る + よう (たべる → たべよう). Group 3: する → しよう, くる → こよう.
- Functions: invitation "let's…" and intention "I will…".
- 〜ましょう is more polite; the volitional is more casual and suits personal intentions.
- Kanji: 昨 (さく), used in きのう = yesterday.
