## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you recognize the four types of distractors typical of N4 listening and know how to eliminate each type before choosing your answer. This ability saves points on questions whose answers are "almost correct".

## Explanation

A catalog of 4 N4 listening distractors, each with its elimination method:

**Type 1 — Similar sounds.** Pairs like ビル/ビール, おばさん/おばあさん, じゅうじ (10 o'clock)/じゅういちじ (11 o'clock). How to eliminate: listen for long-short vowels and the presence of っ. Write what you heard as-is — don't "correct" it in your head.

**Type 2 — Similar numbers and times.** 3時 vs 3時半, 800円 vs 8000円, 火曜日 vs 木曜日. How to eliminate: write down ALL numbers as soon as you hear them. An option that uses a number mentioned then canceled is a trap.

**Type 3 — Misdirected keigo.** 先生がおっしゃいました (correct: the teacher said it) vs 私がおっしゃいました (wrong: おっしゃる isn't for yourself; the correct one is 私が申しました). How to eliminate: ask "who→to whom?" — respectful verbs aren't used for yourself.

**Type 4 — "Both / neither".** Options like AもBも行きます when the dialog decided only one. How to eliminate: match with the FINAL decision; if only one was decided, cross out the "both" option.

## Example sentences

A completed elimination example (type 2). Listen once via the app's TTS:

> A: 明日、3時に図書館で会おう。
> B: ごめん、3時半の方がいいな。授業が3時に終わるんだ。
> A: わかった。じゃ、3時半に図書館ね。

Question: 二人はいつ会いますか。a. 3時 b. 3時半 c. 4時
Elimination: a (3時) is the initial time that B CANCELED — cross it out. c (4時) was never mentioned — cross it out. Answer: b. The pattern: the trap option uses a number that really appears in the dialog, but it isn't the final decision.

## Practice

Listen to each mini-dialog once via the app's TTS, choose your answer, then check the eliminations in the key.

1. (Type 1) > A: すみません、トイレはどこですか。B: あそこのビルの2階です。
Question: トイレはどこですか。a. ビールの2階 b. ビルの2階 c. 病院の2階
2. (Type 2) > A: パーティーは来週の土曜日、7時からです。B: 土曜日はだめなんだ。日曜日の7時なら行けるよ。A: じゃ、日曜日の7時にしましょう。
Question: パーティーはいつですか。a. 土曜日の7時 b. 日曜日の7時 c. 土曜日の8時
3. (Type 3) > A: 先生は昨日の会議について何とおっしゃいましたか。B: 先生は「来週の月曜日までにレポートを出すように」とおっしゃいました。
Question: 正しい文はどれですか。a. 先生がおっしゃいました b. 私がおっしゃいました c. 先生が申しました
4. (Type 4) > A: 週末、山に行かない？B: 山はちょっと。海なら行きたいな。A: じゃ、海に行こう。
Question: 二人は週末どこに行きますか。a. 山に行きます b. 海に行きます c. 山も海も行きます

### Answer key

1. b. ビルの2階 — a is the wrong sound (ビール = beer); c was never mentioned.
2. b. 日曜日の7時 — a is the initial plan that was canceled; c combines a wrong day with an unmentioned time.
3. a. 先生がおっしゃいました — b is misdirected (おっしゃる for yourself); c is misdirected (申す is for yourself, not for the teacher).
4. b. 海に行きます — a is the initial invitation that was declined; c is the "both" type although only 海 was decided.

## Tips / key patterns

- Common mistake: choosing an option that "was heard in the dialog" without checking whether it's the final decision.
- Type 1: train your ears with minimal pairs in SRS (ビル/ビール, おじさん/おじいさん).
- Type 3: memorize the direction pairs — おっしゃる・ご覧になる・なさる (for others) vs 申す・いたす・拝見する (for yourself).
- When two options are similar, the difference is usually a small word: も, は, っ, or a long vowel.

## Summary

- 4 N4 distractors: similar sounds, similar numbers/times, misdirected keigo, "both" options.
- Type 1: hear long-short vowels and っ; write what you heard as-is.
- Types 2 & 4: hold the FINAL decision of the dialog, cross out canceled versions.
- Type 3: ask "who→to whom"; respectful verbs aren't for yourself.
