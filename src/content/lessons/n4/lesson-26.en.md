## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use special sonkeigo verbs to talk about superiors and outsiders respectfully. You understand the direction of use — for the respected other person, not for yourself — and the general pattern お + ます-stem + になる.

## Explanation

Sonkeigo is respectful language that elevates the listener: it is used for superiors, customers, and people outside the company. Ordinary verbs are replaced with special verbs:

- いる・いく・くる → **いらっしゃる** (to be, to come, to go)
- いう → **おっしゃる** (to say)
- する → **なさる** (to do)
- みる → **ごらんになる** (to see)
- たべる・のむ → **めしあがる** (to eat, to drink)
- しっている → **ごぞんじだ** (to know)

Besides the memorized list above, there is a general pattern: **お + ます-stem + になる**. Examples: よむ → およみになる, かえる → おかえりになる, かく → おかきになる, もつ → おもちになる. This pattern does not apply to verbs that already have a special form (never make おみになる — use ごらんになる).

The direction of use is everything: sonkeigo is used for the respected **other person**. Using it for yourself is a fatal mistake: わたしが おっしゃいました is a completely wrong sentence. For yourself, use kenjougo in L27.

The two kanji of this lesson: 社 (しゃ = company), 営 (いとなむ = to run a business).

## Example sentences

1. ぶちょうは もう いらっしゃいましたか。(Has the section chief arrived yet?)
2. しゃちょうが こう おっしゃいました。(The company president said so.)
3. しりょうを ごらんになりましたか。(Have you looked at the materials?)
4. おちゃを めしあがってください。(Please have some tea.)
5. せんせいは あした なにを なさいますか。(What will the teacher do tomorrow?)
6. この けんを ごぞんじですか。(Do you know about this matter?)
7. ちちは さかなやを 営(いとな)んでいます。(My father runs a fish shop.)

## Tips / key patterns

- ます-form trap: なさる becomes なさいます (not なさります). Memorize it as an exception.
- いらっしゃる covers three meanings: いる, いく, and くる. Context decides the meaning: もう いらっしゃいましたか = "have they arrived yet?".
- The お + ます-stem + になる pattern is only for verbs without a special form. Since ごらんになる already exists, there is no おみになる.
- Direction test: if the subject is "I", sonkeigo is definitely wrong. This is a quick way to answer JLPT questions.

## Summary

- Sonkeigo elevates the listener: for superiors, customers, outsiders.
- Memorize the six special verbs: いらっしゃる, おっしゃる, なさる, ごらんになる, めしあがる, ごぞんじ.
- General pattern: お + ます-stem + になる (およみになる, おかえりになる).
- Never use sonkeigo for yourself. The ます form of なさる is なさいます.
