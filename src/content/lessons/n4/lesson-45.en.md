## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can run a timed half-mock (Vocabulary + Grammar + Reading blocks) under exam conditions and review the results the right way. This lesson contains strategy — the questions come from the app's question bank.

## Explanation

A half-mock = the Vocabulary + Grammar + Reading blocks in one go, no dictionary, no breaks. Listening is separated (already trained in L43–L44).

**Time allocation (±55 minutes, practice scale):** Vocabulary 15 minutes (±45–60 seconds/question), Grammar 20 minutes (±60–75 seconds/question), Reading 20 minutes (±6–8 minutes per text + questions). Official N4 times: Vocabulary 25 minutes, Grammar + Reading 55 minutes. This 55-minute scale is stricter — if you pass here, your official pacing is safe.

**Skip & return rules:** stuck >60 seconds → mark and move on (one question = one point); return to marked questions only after all are answered; re-read once with a fresh mind; no penalty for wrong answers — NEVER leave blank.

**3-step review (15 minutes):** (a) score per block, not just the total; (b) for each wrong/uncertain question: write the question type + cause (vocabulary? tricked? time?); (c) turn it into action: vocabulary → SRS today; pattern → open its lesson; time → L41–L42 drills.

## Example sentences

The correct Reading block rhythm (20 minutes: 2 short texts + 1 medium text):

- Minutes 0–4: two short texts — questions first, scanning, answer.
- Minutes 4–12: medium text — skim first/last paragraphs, answer 3–4 questions.
- Minutes 12–20: return to questions marked uncertain, then recheck.

The wrong example: 12 minutes spent on the medium text because of one unknown word, then the short texts done in a 3-minute panic. One unknown word isn't worth 3 missed questions.

## Practice

1. What is the time allocation per block in this practice scale?
2. You've been stuck 60 seconds on one question — what do you do?
3. Your Grammar block is the lowest with many uncertain marks. Name two actions.
4. May you leave answers blank? Why?
5. You finished Vocabulary in 10 minutes. What do you do with the remaining time?

### Answer key

1. Vocabulary 15', Grammar 20', Reading 20' (total ±55').
2. Mark and move on; return at the end if time allows.
3. Reopen the lesson for the pattern that caused the errors; train speed with timed drills.
4. No — there is no score deduction for wrong answers.
5. Recheck uncertain answers in the same block; don't move to another block.

## Tips / key patterns

- Set a visible timer and scratch paper BEFORE starting; tell your household (70 minutes without interruptions).
- Score per block matters more than the total: the weakest block = your priority this week.
- Many wrong answers in the last 5 questions of each block = a rhythm problem, not an ability problem.

## Summary

- Half-mock = Vocabulary + Grammar + Reading, ±55 minutes, one go, no dictionary.
- Allocation: 15' vocabulary, 20' grammar, 20' reading.
- Skip anything stuck >60 seconds; return at the end; never leave blank.
- Review: score per block → cause → concrete action.
