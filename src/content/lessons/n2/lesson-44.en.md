## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you will have an exam-style N2 listening drill protocol: audio played ONCE, full 50 minutes. You will master keyword notes (4K), how to tell apart similar-sounding options, focus per question type, and a two-week drill plan using the app's audio feature.

## Explanation

**Golden rule: PLAY ONCE.** Don't replay the audio during drills — the real exam gives no second chance. Replay is allowed ONLY during review, after you've answered.

**4K note technique** (short, English/kana — not full sentences):

- だれ (who speaks/acts)
- いつ (when)
- どこ (where)
- なにを (what must be done / the gist)

**Read the question + options BEFORE the audio.** Predict the topic, mark the DIFFERENTIATOR word of each option (numbers, places, actors, tense); hunt for those words while the audio plays.

**Focus allocation per question type:**

1. 課題理解 — take 4K notes; the final decision is often at the END of the dialog; ignore rejected proposals.
2. ポイント理解 — focus on the word the speaker emphasizes or repeats.
3. 概要理解 — don't note details; catch only the topic + the speaker's stance.
4. 即時応答 — listen to only the LAST sentence; answer within 1 second.
5. 統合理解 — note per speaker (A=X, B=Y); the question asks about their combination.

**Two-week plan (25–30 min/day):** week 1 = one type per session + review; week 2 = 統合理解 + mixed, closed with a full 50-minute once-played simulation.

## Example sentences

**4K note example** (illustration, not audio):

〔会話の例〕
男：すみません、明日の会議の資料、コピーしておきましたか。
女：はい、20部コピーして、会議室の机の上に置いておきました。
男：ありがとう。じゃ、9時からの会議、よろしく。

4K notes: だれ＝女の人 / なにを＝資料を20部コピー、机の上に置いた / いつ＝明日の9時からの会議.

Question: 女の人は何をしましたか。→ 資料を20部コピーして、会議室の机の上に置いた.

**Example of distinguishing similar-sounding options:**

a. 会議室でコピーした
b. 20部コピーして、机の上に置いた
c. 明日コピーする
d. 9時に会議室に行く

Differentiators: 20部 (number), 机の上 (place), した (past). Hunt for all three; ignore words common to all options.

## Practice

1. Why must the audio not be replayed during drills? When may it be replayed?
2. What goes into 4K notes, and what are the writing rules?
3. For 概要理解 questions, what is noted and what is not?
4. What is the technique for distinguishing similar-sounding options?
5. Write your own 2-week drill schedule (25–30 minutes/day).

### Answer key

1. Because the real exam plays it only once; a replayed drill gives a fake score. Replay is allowed only during review after answering.
2. だれ・いつ・どこ・なにを; written short (English/kana), not full sentences.
3. Note: topic + speaker's stance. Don't note: number/name details.
4. Mark the differentiator word between options before the audio; hunt for it and ignore words common to all options.
5. Correct example: week 1 one type per session + review; week 2 統合理解 + mixed + full 50-minute simulation.

## Tips / key patterns

- The most common N2 listening failure is fatigue at minutes 30–50 — stamina is built with full simulations, not 10-minute drills.
- Writing too much = missing the next sentence. 4K notes max 10–15 words.
- Proposals REJECTED mid-dialog are favorite distractors — wait for the final decision.
- During review, replay ONLY the part that caused the error, then note its words into SRS.

## Summary

- Drills = play once, no pauses; replay the audio only during review.
- Take short 4K notes; read the question + options before the audio and mark differentiator words.
- Focus per type: final decision / emphasis / topic+stance / last sentence / per speaker.
- 2-week plan: per type → mixed → full 50-minute simulation.

## Tasks & SRS notes

Run one 25-minute once-played session this week with 4K notes on paper; count questions missed due to "missed hearing" vs "misunderstood". Add to your SRS deck: 聞き取る、聞き逃す、選択肢、注意点、概要理解.
