## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the three "regardless of" patterns and choose the right one for the nuance: 〜を問わず (not made a condition), 〜にかかわらず (regardless of, the most common), and 〜によらず (without depending on the means). This lesson introduces no new kanji.

## Explanation

**1. 〜を問わず.** The pattern: noun + を問わず. Its literal meaning is "without asking", so the nuance is: the thing **is not made a condition / is not an issue**. The nouns used are usually attributes people commonly ask about: 年齢, 性別, こくせき, 経験.

- 年齢を問わず応募できる (regardless of age, you can apply)
- 老若男女を問わず楽しめる (enjoyable for everyone, young and old)

**2. 〜にかかわらず.** The pattern: noun + にかかわらず. This is the most common and broadest: "regardless of". Its nouns are usually things beyond one's control: 理由, 天候, せいひ. This pattern can also follow a question clause: 参加するかしないかにかかわらず (whether or not you participate).

- 理由にかかわらず返金しない (regardless of the reason, no refunds)
- 天候にかかわらず実施する (regardless of the weather, it will go ahead)

**3. 〜によらず.** The pattern: noun + によらず. The nuance: "without depending on" — its nouns are **means or methods**: 手段, 方法, 形式.

- 手段によらず連絡する (contact by any means)
- 形式によらず受け付ける (accepted in any format)

**Summary of differences:**

| Pattern | Nuance | Typical nouns |
| 〜を問わず | not made a condition | 年齢, 性別, こくせき, 経験 |
| 〜にかかわらず | regardless of (most common) | 理由, 天候, せいひ |
| 〜によらず | without depending on (means) | 手段, 方法, 形式 |

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 年齢・性別を問わず、ご応募いただけます。(Regardless of age and gender, you are welcome to apply.)
2. 〔文語・である体〕 理由にかかわらず、締め切り後の提出は認められない。(Regardless of the reason, submissions after the deadline are not accepted.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 手段によらず、至急ご連絡ください。(By any means, please contact us urgently.)
4. 〔文語・である体〕 天候にかかわらず、式典は挙行される。(Regardless of the weather, the ceremony will be held.)
5. 〔話し言葉〕 老若男女を問わず楽しめる祭りだよ。(It's a festival everyone can enjoy, young and old.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 参加するかしないかにかかわらず、会費は必要です。(Whether or not you participate, the fee is required.)

## Tips / key patterns

- If the noun is an attribute people usually "ask about" (age, gender, experience) → 〜を問わず.
- If the noun is a means/method (means of communication, format) → 〜によらず.
- If in doubt, 〜にかかわらず is almost always safe because it's the most common — but in the exam, the nuance that fits best is what's graded.
- 〜にかかわらず can follow a question clause (〜か〜ないかにかかわらず); the other two patterns only follow nouns.

## Summary

- 〜を問わず = regardless of (not made a condition): 年齢を問わず.
- 〜にかかわらず = regardless of (most common): 理由にかかわらず; can follow a question clause.
- 〜によらず = without depending on (means/methods): 手段によらず.
- All three are formal written language; choose according to the noun's nuance.
- This lesson introduces no new kanji (like in an N5 review lesson).
