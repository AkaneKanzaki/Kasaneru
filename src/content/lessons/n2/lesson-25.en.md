## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use three appearance-and-impression suffixes: 〜ざま for states or appearances (often with a negative tone), 〜ぶり for a characteristic style or manner, and 〜じみる for an impression of "seeming like …". You can also explain the difference between the three without mixing them up.

## Explanation

**1. 〜ざま = state / appearance (often negative).** It attaches to na-adjectives or the ます-stem of verbs, and carries the speaker's judgment — usually pity, disgust, or sarcasm.

- Na-adjective + なざま: 無残なざま (pitiful state), 惨めなざま
- V-ます stem + ざま: 死にざま (way of dying — sarcastic tone), 泣きざま

**2. 〜ぶり = characteristic style / manner.** It attaches to nouns (often from the ます-stem of verbs) and describes the distinctive way someone does something — without a negative judgment.

- 仕事ぶり (way of working), 話しぶり (way of speaking), 暮らしぶり (way of life)
- Special pattern: time period + ぶり = "after so long": 三年ぶり (after three years)

**3. 〜じみる = giving the impression of ….**

- Na-adjective + じみる: 古じみる (to seem old-fashioned)
- Noun + じみる: 子どもじみる (childish), 素人じみる (like an amateur), 皮肉じみる (sarcastic)

The nuance is "it feels like …, and that's not good" — often a gentle criticism.

**4. This lesson's kanji (11).** 忖 (そん = to sense feelings, e.g. 忖度), 慟 (どう = to lament, e.g. 慟哭), 悼 (いたむ = to mourn, e.g. 追悼), 戚 (せき = relatives, e.g. 親戚), 渾 (こん = thickly blended, e.g. 渾然), 然 (ぜん = thus, e.g. 自然), 厖 (ぼう = large, e.g. 厖大), 汎 (はん = broad and general, e.g. 汎用), 杳 (よう = with no news, e.g. 杳として), 冥 (めい = dark and otherworldly, e.g. 冥王星), 王 (おう = king).

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・である体〕 負けたチームの無残なざまが、テレビに映っていた。(The pitiful state of the losing team was shown on TV.)
2. 〔話し言葉〕 彼の仕事ぶりは丁寧で、みんなに信頼されている。(His way of working is meticulous, so everyone trusts him.)
3. 〔文語・である体〕 その発言は子どもじみていて、聞くに堪えない。(That remark is childish and unbearable to hear.)
4. 〔話し言葉〕 三年ぶりにジャカルタへ帰った。(I went back to Jakarta after three years.)
5. 〔文語・である体〕 彼の服装は古じみているが、本人は気にしていない。(His clothes seem old-fashioned, but he himself doesn't care.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 シンタさんの話しぶりは落ち着いていて聞きやすい。(Sinta's way of speaking is calm and easy to listen to.)
7. 〔文語・である体〕 泥まみれのざまで家に帰った子どもを、母は笑って迎えた。(The mother welcomed with a laugh the child who came home covered in mud.)

## Practice

1. Choose the correct one: 無残(なざま / ぶり) — for a pitiful state.
2. Choose the correct one: 彼の仕事(ざま / ぶり)は丁寧だ。
3. Change: 古い + じみる → ______
4. What does 三年ぶり mean? → ______
5. Translate into Japanese: "That remark is childish." → ______
6. Explain: why does 死にざま sound sarcastic while 仕事ぶり is neutral? → ______

### Answer key

1. なざま
2. ぶり
3. 古じみる
4. After three years (not … for a long time).
5. その発言は子どもじみている。
6. Because 〜ざま carries the speaker's judgment (often negative), whereas 〜ぶり only describes a characteristic style/manner without judgment.

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜ざま = there is judgment (pity/sarcasm); 〜ぶり = neutral description of style; 〜じみる = gentle criticism of "seeming like …".
- 〜ぶり has two meanings: "characteristic way" (仕事ぶり) and "after so long" (三年ぶり) — check the context.
- 〜じみる attaches to na-adjectives or nouns, not to verbs.

## Summary

- 〜ざま: state/appearance + judgment, often negative (無残なざま, 死にざま).
- 〜ぶり: characteristic style/manner, neutral (仕事ぶり); also "after so long" (三年ぶり).
- 〜じみる: giving the impression of "seeming like …", often gentle criticism (古じみる, 子どもじみる).
- All three answer "how does it look?", not "what is the fact?".
- Kanji: 忖 (to sense), 慟 (to lament), 悼 (to mourn), 戚 (relatives), 渾 (blended), 然 (thus), 厖 (large), 汎 (general), 杳 (no news), 冥 (otherworldly), 王 (king).
