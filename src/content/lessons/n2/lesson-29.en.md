## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can write business documents using the 記 / 以上 format, use 丁重語 expressions to soften requests, and use the 前略 / 草々 pair for short letters. You can also tell when to use a full letter (謹啓・敬具) and when a short letter is enough.

## Explanation

**1. 記 / 以上 — document format.** When a document contains bulleted details, the pattern is fixed: an introductory sentence containing 下記のとおり, then 記, then a numbered list, then 以上 as the closer.

- 会議の日程について、下記のとおりお知らせします。記 1. 日時 2. 場所 以上

**2. 丁重語 — softening requests.** Four expressions you must master, each with its own place:

- お手数ですが — "sorry to trouble you", placed before a request: お手数ですが、ご確認ください。
- 恐縮ながら / 恐縮ですが — "with all humility", for heavy requests: 恐縮ながら、再送をお願いします。
- ご検討のほど — "I ask for your consideration"; the word ほど softens the commanding tone: ご検討のほど、よろしくお願いします。
- 〜いただければ幸いです — "I would be glad if …", the gentlest request closer: ご連絡いただければ幸いです。

**3. 前略 / 草々 — short letters.** When the relationship is already close or the matter is urgent, the seasonal pleasantries can be dropped: open with 前略 (forgive me for getting straight to the point), close with 草々. This is still polite, just more concise than 謹啓・敬具.

**4. This lesson's kanji (4).** 謄 (とう = to copy, e.g. 謄本), 憑 (ひょう = to possess, e.g. 憑依), 孜 (し = diligent, e.g. 孜孜), 兢 (きょう = cautious, e.g. 兢兢).

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 会議の日程について、下記のとおりお知らせします。(Regarding the meeting schedule, we inform you as below.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 記 1. 日時:10月20日午後2時 2. 場所:本社会議室 以上(Details — 1. Time: October 20, 2 p.m. 2. Place: head office meeting room — that is all.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 お手数ですが、資料をご確認ください。(Sorry to trouble you, please confirm the documents.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 恐縮ながら、再送をお願い申し上げます。(With all humility, I ask you to resend it.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 ご検討のほど、よろしくお願いします。(I ask for your consideration.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 ご連絡いただければ幸いです。(I would be very glad if you could contact me.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 前略 用件のみにて失礼します。草々(Forgive me for getting straight to business without pleasantries. Yours sincerely.)

## Practice

1. What are the functions of 記 and 以上 in a document? → ______
2. Choose the correct expression before a request: (a) お手数ですが (b) 以上.
3. Translate into Japanese: "Sorry to trouble you, please confirm the documents." → ______
4. When do you use 前略・草々 instead of 謹啓・敬具? → ______
5. Complete: ご検討の___、よろしくお願いします。
6. Correct or wrong: 恐縮ながら is used for light everyday requests. → ______

### Answer key

1. 記 opens bulleted details; 以上 closes them.
2. (a)
3. お手数ですが、資料をご確認ください。
4. For short letters: the relationship is already close or the matter is urgent, without seasonal pleasantries.
5. ほど
6. Wrong — 恐縮ながら is for heavy / awkward requests, not light ones.

## Tips / key patterns

- Fixed order: 下記のとおり → 記 → list → 以上. Don't forget the closer 以上.
- 丁重語 always comes before a request, never after it.
- ご〜のほど + よろしくお願いします is a safe request-closing package.
- 前略・草々 is still polite — don't equate it with casual language.

## Summary

- 記 / 以上: opener and closer of document details (introduced by 下記のとおり).
- 丁重語: お手数ですが, 恐縮ながら, ご検討のほど, 〜いただければ幸いです.
- 前略 / 草々: short letters without seasonal pleasantries.
- Full letters (謹啓・敬具) for formal occasions; short letters for close/urgent ones.
- Kanji: 謄 (to copy), 憑 (to possess), 孜 (diligent), 兢 (cautious).
