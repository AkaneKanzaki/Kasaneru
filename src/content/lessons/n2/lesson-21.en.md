## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the 〜なり〜なり pattern to name two options or examples in turn ("either X or Y"), a formal pattern that often appears in N2 reading passages. You can also distinguish it from 〜なり "the moment… immediately" that you already learned in N4, so you won't be fooled by exam questions.

## Explanation

**1. Form.** This pattern attaches to two parallel elements:

- Nouns: N + なり + N + なり → 電話なりメールなり
- Verbs: dictionary form + なり + dictionary form + なり → 休むなり働くなり
- Na-adjectives: na-adjective + なり → 静かなり賑やかなり

**2. Meaning: "either… or…".** The speaker names two examples representing the options, then hands the decision to the listener. The nuance is somewhat formal and it is often used for instructions, permission, or offers in business contexts.

- 電話なりメールなりで連絡してください。(Contact me by phone or email — whichever you like.)

The two things named are only representative examples; the meaning is "among these, choose whichever you like."

**3. Don't confuse it with 〜なり (N4).** In N4 you learned V-dictionary + なり, which means "the moment… immediately": 家に帰るなり寝た (the moment I got home, I slept). That is a single 〜なり. What we have here is a doubled 〜なり〜なり, meaning "either… or". Different count, different meaning — this is a favorite trap in N2 questions.

**4. This lesson's kanji (11).** 腺 (せん = gland), 腫 (しゅ = swelling, e.g. 腫瘍), 瘍 (よう = ulcer, e.g. 腫瘍), 癌 (がん = cancer), 痺 (しびれる = numb), 眩 (まぶしい = dazzling), 瞼 (まぶた = eyelid), 瞑 (つむる = to close one's eyes), 睫 (まつげ = eyelash), 頤 (おとがい = chin), 齬 (ご = discrepancy, e.g. 齟齬).

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 ご質問は電話なりメールなりでお知らせください。(For questions, please let us know by phone or email.)
2. 〔話し言葉〕 休むなり働くなり、好きにしなさい。(Whether you rest or work, do as you like.)
3. 〔文語・である体〕 来週なり再来週なり、都合のいい日を教えてほしい。(Please tell me a convenient day, either next week or the week after.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 お支払いは現金なり振込なりでお願いします。(For payment, please use cash or bank transfer.)
5. 〔文語・である体〕 彼は医師なり弁護士なり、専門職に就くことを望んでいる。(He wants to pursue a professional career, whether as a doctor or a lawyer.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 泣くなり笑うなり、はっきりしなさい。(Whether you cry or laugh, make it clear!)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 資料は郵送なり持参なり、お好きな方法でお送りください。(Send the documents by post or bring them in person, by whichever method you prefer.)

## Practice

1. Arrange into a correct sentence: で / 連絡する / 電話 / なり / メール / なり → ______
2. Choose the correct one: 来週(なり / やら)再来週(なり / やら)、都合のいい日を教えてください。
3. Translate into Japanese: "Pay by cash or bank transfer." → ______
4. What does 帰るなり寝た mean? (N4 pattern) → ______
5. Fill in with なり: 休む___働く___、好きにしなさい。
6. Correct or wrong: 電話やメールなりで連絡してください。→ ______ (write the correct form)

### Answer key

1. 電話なりメールなりで連絡する
2. なり … なり
3. 現金なり振込なりでお支払いください。
4. The moment I got home, I slept immediately.
5. 休むなり働くなり
6. Wrong → 電話なりメールなりで連絡してください。

## Tips / key patterns

- 〜なり〜なり always comes in pairs; a single なり has a different meaning (the N4 pattern "the moment… immediately").
- The options named are only representative examples — the meaning is "among these, choose as you like."
- It often appears in business instructions: 〜なり〜なりでお知らせください.
- Don't slip や or か in between: ×電話やメールなり.

## Summary

- Form: N + なり + N + なり; V-dictionary + なり + V-dictionary + なり.
- Meaning: "either X or Y"; the decision is handed to the listener.
- Somewhat formal register; typical of instructions, permission, and offers.
- Different from single 〜なり (N4), which means "the moment… immediately".
- Kanji: 腺 (gland), 腫 (swelling), 瘍 (ulcer), 癌 (cancer), 痺 (numb), 眩 (dazzling), 瞼 (eyelid), 瞑 (closing one's eyes), 睫 (eyelash), 頤 (chin), 齬 (discrepancy).
