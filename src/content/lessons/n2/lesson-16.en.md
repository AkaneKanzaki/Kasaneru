## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can express overflowing, irresistible emotions — tears, laughter, anger — with the literary pattern 〜をきんじえない ("cannot hold back"). You also understand the classical origin of this pattern and its variants.

## Explanation

**1. Form and meaning.** The pattern: noun (emotion) + をきんじえない. It means "cannot hold back ...".

- 涙をきんじえない (cannot hold back tears)
- 笑いをきんじえなかった (couldn't hold back laughter)
- 怒りをきんじ得なかった (could not hold back anger)

The noun is almost always an overflowing emotion: 涙, 笑い, 怒り, 驚き, 感動, 喜び, 同情.

**2. Classical origin.** This pattern comes from the verb きんじる ("to restrain, to forbid"), changed into the classical potential form きんじ得る ("can restrain"), then negated into きんじ得ない / きんじえない ("cannot restrain"). Because of its classical tone, this pattern lives in literary written language, speeches, and news — not in casual conversation.

**3. How to write it.** The word きんじる is usually written with the kanji 禁, but that kanji is rarely used in this lesson, so the pattern is always written in kana: きんじえない. A slightly more classical variant: をきんじ得ない. The polite form: をきんじえません. This lesson introduces no new kanji; the kanji in the emotion vocabulary are used naturally.

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・である体〕 卒業式で、ブディさんは涙をきんじえなかった。(At the graduation ceremony, Budi couldn't hold back his tears.)
2. 〔文語・である体〕 シンタさんの冗談に、皆は笑いをきんじえなかった。(At Sinta's joke, everyone couldn't hold back their laughter.)
3. 〔文語・である体〕 友の裏切りに、怒りをきんじ得なかった。(At his friend's betrayal, he could not hold back his anger.)
4. 〔文語・である体〕 十年ぶりの再会に、喜びをきんじえなかった。(At the reunion after ten years, his joy was uncontainable.)
5. 〔話し言葉〕 あの映画、最後は泣かずにいられなかったよ。(That movie — I couldn't watch the ending without crying.)
6. 〔文語・である体〕 故郷を思うと、恋しさをきんじえない。(Whenever I think of my hometown, the longing is unbearable.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 ご支援に、感謝をきんじえません。(For your support, my gratitude is beyond words.)

2. 涙
3. 笑いをきんじえなかった
4. 感謝をきんじえません
5. 彼は怒りをきんじえなかった。

## Tips / key patterns

- The noun is always an emotion or spontaneous reaction (涙, 笑い, 怒り, 驚き). Don't attach it to concrete objects.
- Remember its origin: きんじる → きんじ得る → きんじえない. Knowing this makes the form easy to memorize.
- Different from 〜てたまらない (N3, casual "unbearable"): きんじえない is far more literary and only for overflowing emotions.
- In this lesson the pattern is written in kana (きんじえない) because the kanji 禁 is rarely used in the lesson texts.

## Summary

- N(emotion) + をきんじえない = "cannot hold back ...": 涙をきんじえない.
- Comes from the classical potential form きんじ得る; variants: をきんじ得ない; polite: をきんじえません.
- Literary written language/speeches; the noun is always an overflowing emotion.
