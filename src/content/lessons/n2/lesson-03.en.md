## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use the N2 pattern pair 〜に堪える and 〜に堪えない to express endurance or test-worthiness — for example a work that stands up to criticism, or a sight unbearable to behold.

## Explanation

**1. 〜に堪える (pattern 5).** The formula: ます-stem verb (ます form minus ます) + に堪える, or noun + に堪える. Means "to withstand…", and in judgment contexts "worthy of being tested by…".

- 鑑賞に 堪える 作品 (a work worthy of appreciation)
- 長期の 使用に 堪える 道具 (a tool that withstands long-term use)

**2. 〜に堪えない (pattern 6).** Same formula in negative form. Means "unbearable…" — a strong negative judgment, often for pitiful sights or deeds.

- 見るに 堪えない 光景 (a sight unbearable to behold)

Distinguish from 〜に足る (lesson 2): 〜に足る judges fitness to receive treatment ("worthy of trust"), whereas 〜に堪える judges endurance under treatment ("withstands criticism"). Meanwhile 〜に堪えない is almost always condemnatory in tone, not merely neutral.

The six kanji in this lesson: 贖 (しょく = to atone, e.g. 贖罪), 讒 (ざん = to slander, e.g. 讒言), 言 (げん = words, e.g. 讒言・言論), 尉 (い = officer, e.g. 尉官), 僉 (せん = to reach consensus, e.g. 僉議), 曹 (そう = noncommissioned officer, e.g. 曹長).

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・である体〕 この 映画は 鑑賞に 堪える 作品である。(This film is a work worthy of appreciation.)
2. 〔文語・である体〕 シンタ氏の 論文は 批判に 堪える 内容を 持っている。(Sinta's paper has content that withstands criticism.)
3. 〔文語・である体〕 戦後の 混乱は 見るに 堪えない 光景で あった。(The postwar chaos was a sight unbearable to behold.)
4. 〔文語・である体〕 彼の 讒言は 聞くに 堪えない。(His slander is unbearable to hear.)
5. 〔文語・である体〕 言論の 自由は いかなる 検閲にも 堪える ものでは ない。(Freedom of speech is not something that can withstand any censorship.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 この 試作品は 長期の 使用に 堪えるか、検証が 必要だ。(We need to verify whether this prototype can withstand long-term use.)
7. 〔文語・である体〕 ブディ氏の 演技は 酷評に 堪え、絶賛を 得た。(Budi's acting withstood harsh criticism and earned extraordinary praise.)

## Tips / key patterns

- The formula uses the ます stem (鑑賞し → 鑑賞に堪える), not the dictionary form. This is the opposite of 〜に足る, so memorize the pair well.
- 〜に堪えない is almost always condemnatory or pitiful in tone (見るに堪えない光景). If the context is neutral, the answer is probably not this pattern.

## Summary

- 〜に堪える (ます stem / noun +): "withstands…, worthy of being tested by…" — a positive judgment of endurance.
- 〜に堪えない: "unbearable…, unworthy of being tested…" — a strong, condemnatory negative judgment.
- Different from 〜に足る: 足る judges fitness, 堪える judges endurance.
- Kanji: 贖 (to atone), 讒 (to slander), 言 (words), 尉 (officer), 僉 (to reach consensus), 曹 (noncommissioned officer).
