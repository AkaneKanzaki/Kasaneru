## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use お〜申し上げる and 拝〜 words — level II humble language for very formal writing and speech. You can also distinguish them from お〜する (謙譲語I, N4) and know when to step up to level II.

## Explanation

**1. お〜申し上げる.** Form: お + ます-stem + 申し上げる. The word 申し上げる is itself the humble form of 言う, so the pattern means "I respectfully say / do …".

- お詫び申し上げる (I respectfully apologize)
- お願い申し上げる (I respectfully ask)
- お礼申し上げる (I respectfully give thanks)

**2. 拝〜.** Four set words that cannot be made up freely — memorize them as a package:

- 拝見する = to look at (humble form of 見る): 資料を拝見しました
- 拝読する = to read (humble form of 読む): お手紙を拝読しました
- 拝受する = to receive (humble form of 受け取る): お品物を拝受しました
- 拝借する = to borrow (humble form of 借りる): お知恵を拝借したい

**3. Different from お〜する (謙譲語I, N4).** お〜する, like お持ちします or ご案内します, is standard humble language for everyday business conversation. Level II (お〜申し上げる, 拝〜, ご〜いただく) is used when the situation demands maximum formality: official letters, speeches, ceremonies, and public apologies. Simply put: ordinary meetings → level I; official letters and apologies → level II.

**4. Direction warning.** All these patterns humble the speaker's own actions. Never use them for the other person's actions: ×先生が拝見しました. If the one who looked is your teacher, use ご覧になりました (尊敬語).

**5. This lesson's kanji (4).** 厳 (きびしい = strict; げん = solemn, e.g. 厳粛), 恪 (かく = dutiful and diligent), 恭 (うやうやしい = reverent), 欽 (きん = admiring, e.g. 欽慕).

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 ご迷惑をおかけし、心よりお詫び申し上げます。(We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 資料を拝見しました。(I have looked at the documents.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 お手紙を拝読いたしました。(I have read your letter.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 お品物を拝受いたしました。(I have received the goods.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 何とぞよろしくお願い申し上げます。(I most respectfully ask for your cooperation.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 日頃のご厚情に心よりお礼申し上げます。(From the bottom of my heart, I thank you for your kindness all this time.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 皆様のお知恵を拝借したく存じます。(I wish to borrow the wisdom of everyone present.)

## Practice

1. Change to the お〜申し上げる pattern: お詫びする → ______
2. The correct pair: to look at → ______する; to read a letter → ______する.
3. Choose the correct one for an official letter: (a) お持ちします (b) お詫び申し上げます — for a written apology.
4. Translate into Japanese: "I have looked at the documents." → ______
5. Correct or wrong: 先生が資料を拝見しました。→ ______ (explain)
6. When should you use level II instead of お〜する? → ______

### Answer key

1. お詫び申し上げる
2. 拝見する; 拝読する
3. (b)
4. 資料を拝見しました。
5. Wrong — 拝見 humbles the speaker's own act of looking, so it cannot be used for a teacher; the correct form is 先生が資料をご覧になりました.
6. When maximum formality is required: official letters, speeches, ceremonies, public apologies; ordinary meetings are enough for level I (お〜する).

## Tips / key patterns

- 拝〜 is only four set words (拝見・拝読・拝受・拝借); don't invent your own 拝〜.
- 申し上げる = the humble form of 言う; the お〜申し上げる pattern is always about "I say/do … respectfully".
- The four-piece set for official letters: 拝啓・拝見・拝読・お詫び申し上げる — master it as a package.
- Direction is non-negotiable: humble = for yourself, elevate = for the other person.

## Summary

- お + ます-stem + 申し上げる: level II humble language (お詫び・お願い・お礼申し上げる).
- 拝〜: 拝見 (to look), 拝読 (to read), 拝受 (to receive), 拝借 (to borrow) — memorize as a package.
- Level I (お〜する) for everyday business; level II for official letters and apologies.
- Never for the other person's actions.
- Kanji: 厳 (strict/solemn), 恪 (dutiful), 恭 (reverent), 欽 (admiring).
