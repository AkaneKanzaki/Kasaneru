## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can write a complete Japanese business email following its standard structure: from 宛名 to 署名, including the opening-closing pair 謹啓・敬具. You also know when to use 謹啓 and when 拝啓 is enough.

## Explanation

**1. Formal email structure (7 parts).** The order is fixed and must not be shuffled:

1. 件名 — a clear subject, e.g. 「打ち合わせ日程変更のお願い」
2. 宛名 — company name + person's name + 様
3. 頭語 — opening word: 謹啓 (most formal) or 拝啓
4. 前文 — seasonal greeting and well-wishes: 時下ますますご清栄のこととお慶び申し上げます。
5. 主文 — main content, starting with さて or つきましては
6. 末文 — closing of the content: まずは取り急ぎご連絡まで。
7. 結語 + 署名 — 敬具 (the partner of 謹啓), then date, name, company, contact

**2. 謹啓 / 敬具.** 謹啓 is the most formal 頭語 (opening word); its partner is 敬具 as the 結語 (closing word). 拝啓・敬白 is one level below. The rule is strict: if you open with 謹啓, you must close with 敬具 — don't mix it with 敬白.

**3. Boilerplate sentences.** Japanese business email uses memorized standard sentences, not free composition:

- 前文: 時下ますますご清栄のこととお慶び申し上げます。(I wish you ever greater prosperity.)
- Content opener: さて、〜の件についてご連絡いたします。
- Content closer: まずは取り急ぎご連絡まで。(For now, this is my message.)

**4. This lesson's kanji (4).** 稽 (けい = to practice, e.g. 稽古), 謁 (えつ = to have an audience, e.g. 謁見), 奏 (そう = to play, e.g. 演奏), 綸 (りん = edict, e.g. 綸言).

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 謹啓 時下ますますご清栄のこととお慶び申し上げます。(Respectfully — I wish you ever greater prosperity.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 さて、来週の打ち合わせの日程についてご連絡いたします。(Regarding next week's meeting schedule, I am contacting you.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 ご都合のほど、お知らせいただけますと幸いです。(I would be glad if you could let me know a convenient time.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 まずは取り急ぎご連絡まで。敬具(For now, this is my message. Yours sincerely.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 件名は具体的に書くのが基本です。(Writing the subject specifically is the basic rule.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 宛名には会社名とお名前の後に様を付けます。(For the recipient's name, add 様 after the company name and the person's name.)

## Practice

1. Put in the correct order: 結語・頭語・主文・前文・末文 → ______
2. The correct partner for 謹啓 is ______.
3. What is the function of 前文? → ______
4. Translate into Japanese: "Regarding the meeting schedule, I am contacting you." → ______
5. Correct or wrong: an email opened with 謹啓 and closed with 敬白. → ______
6. Write a good 件名 for an email about changing a meeting schedule. → ______

### Answer key

1. 頭語 → 前文 → 主文 → 末文 → 結語
2. 敬具
3. An opening greeting containing news/seasonal words before moving into the content (e.g. 時下ますますご清栄のこととお慶び申し上げます).
4. さて、打ち合わせの日程についてご連絡いたします。
5. Wrong — the partner of 謹啓 is 敬具; 敬白 pairs with 拝啓.
6. 打ち合わせ日程変更のお願い (example; what matters is that it is specific and starts with the main point).

## Tips / key patterns

- Don't mix the pairs: 謹啓・敬具 or 拝啓・敬白 — never crossed.
- The 件名 decides whether the email gets read; write its main point, not "hello" or "important".
- Boilerplate sentences (前文・末文) are memorized, not composed — interviewers and clients judge you on their accuracy.
- In email, 宛名 uses 様 for outsiders; don't use さん in official letters.

## Summary

- Structure: 件名 → 宛名 → 頭語 → 前文 → 主文 → 末文 → 結語 → 署名.
- 謹啓/敬具 = the most formal pair; 拝啓/敬白 one level below.
- Boilerplate sentences: 時下ますますご清栄のこととお慶び申し上げます / まずは取り急ぎご連絡まで.
- The main content starts with さて or つきましては.
- Kanji: 稽 (to practice), 謁 (to have an audience), 奏 (to play), 綸 (edict).
