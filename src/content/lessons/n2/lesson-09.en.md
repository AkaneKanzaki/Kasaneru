## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can express absolute certainty with the N2 pattern 〜に相違ない ("beyond doubt"). This formal-written-register pattern is used in documents, reports, and official testimony.

## Explanation

**〜に相違ない (pattern 19).** The formula: dictionary-form / negative-form verb + に相違ない; i-adjective + に相違ない; noun + に相違ない. Means "beyond doubt…" — formal certainty, as if already verified.

- 記録に 相違ない。(Beyond doubt, in accordance with the records.)

Note the nuance: 相違 means "difference", so 〜に相違ない literally means "there is no difference (from what is stated)" — "exactly so, no doubt". That is why this pattern is perfect for documents and testimony: asserting that a statement matches the recorded facts.

Distinguish from the two earlier certainty patterns. 〜に決まっている (lesson 5) is grounded in subjective conviction; 〜に違いない is grounded in observed evidence; while 〜に相違ない asserts formal conformity — "already checked, no discrepancy". Its register is also the most formal of the three.

The six kanji in this lesson: 依 (い = to request, e.g. 依頼), 偉 (い = great, e.g. 偉業), 威 (い = dignity, e.g. 威厳), 宛 (あて = addressed to, e.g. 宛名), 傭 (よう = to employ, e.g. 雇用), 俸 (ほう = salary, e.g. 俸給).

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・である体〕 照合の 結果、記録に 相違ない。(The matching results: beyond doubt, in accordance with the records.)
2. 〔文語・である体〕 彼の 言葉に 相違ないことを 保証する。(I guarantee that those are beyond doubt his words.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 ご依頼の 内容に 相違ないことを 確認いたしました。(We have confirmed that the content is beyond doubt as requested.)
4. 〔文語・である体〕 検証の 結果、真相は 報告書の 通りに 相違ない。(The verification results: the truth is beyond doubt as written in the report.)
5. 〔文語・である体〕 宛名に 相違ないことを ご確認 ください。(Please confirm that it is beyond doubt addressed to the right recipient.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 俸給の 支給額に 相違ないことを 保証します。(We guarantee that the salary paid is beyond doubt the correct amount.)
7. 〔文語・である体〕 両者の 証言に 食い違いは あるが、事実に 相違ない。(Though there is a discrepancy between the two testimonies, the facts are beyond doubt.)

## Practice

1. Choose the correct one: 記録 (に相違ない / に相違する)。
2. Fill in with the correct pattern: 彼の 言葉___。
3. Form 〜に相違ない from a noun: 事実 → ______
4. Choose the most formal pattern for documents: (に決まっている / に違いない / に相違ない)。
5. Translate: "Beyond doubt, in accordance with the records." → ______
6. Distinguish: 〜に違いない is grounded in ______, 〜に相違ない asserts ______. (observed evidence / verified formal conformity)
7. True or false: 「彼の言葉に相違する」→ ______ (true/false, explain)

### Answer key

1. に相違ない
2. に相違ない
3. 事実に相違ない
4. に相違ない
5. 記録に 相違ない。
6. 〜に違いない is grounded in observed evidence, 〜に相違ない asserts verified formal conformity
7. false — 相違する means "to differ", not "no doubt"; the standard form is に相違ない

## Tips / key patterns

- Watch out for the trap: 相違 itself means "difference", so ×に相違する actually means "to differ". Certainty only comes in the standard negative form: に相違ない.
- In exam questions, a context of documents, reports, verification, or official testimony is a strong signal for 〜に相違ない.

## Summary

- 〜に相違ない: "beyond doubt…" — formal certainty asserting conformity with recorded facts.
- The certainty family: subjective conviction → に決まっている; evidence-based inference → に違いない; verified conformity → に相違ない.
- Trap: ×に相違する means "to differ" — always use に相違ない.
- Kanji: 依 (to request), 偉 (great), 威 (dignity), 宛 (addressed to), 傭 (to employ), 俸 (salary).
