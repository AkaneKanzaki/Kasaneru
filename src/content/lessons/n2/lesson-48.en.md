## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you will be able to run a full N2 mock (155 minutes) like the real exam: H-1 preparation, answer-sheet discipline, two-session pacing, and the once-played listening rule — without Study mode's safety net.

## Explanation

**Real mode** = no safety net: no pauses between sections (except the official break), no explanations, no notes/phone. Its score is the most honest mirror before the exam.

**H-1 checklist (the day before):**

- [ ] Sleep 7–8 hours; don't stay up "cramming material"
- [ ] Prepare: 2B pencils (2), eraser, sharpener, ID card, wristwatch
- [ ] Phone in airplane mode / off during the mock
- [ ] Distraction-free location + route (for exam day)
- [ ] Eat enough before starting; prepare drinking water

**Answer-sheet discipline.** Blacken the bubbles fully and neatly. Transfer answers PER BLOCK (±10 questions), not per question. Every 10 questions, check: question number = bubble number.

**155-minute pacing.** Session 1: 20'+25'+50'+10' (L45). 15–20' break without notes. Session 2: 50' listening, played once.

**Listening rule.** Play once, no pauses. Missed one question: LET IT GO, focus on the next — chasing it can sink five other questions.

**When panicking:** stop for 10 seconds, breathe, return to the EASIEST question.

## Example sentences

**Example mock-day timeline:**

- 07.00 — wake up, breakfast as usual (don't try new food)
- 08.00 — leave; 08.30 arrive & set up desk
- 09.00–10.45 — SESSION 1 (105 minutes, no pause)
- 10.45–11.05 — break (toilet + snack, no notes)
- 11.05–11.55 — SESSION 2 listening (50 minutes, played once)
- 11.55 — done; rest 1 hour before review (L47).

**Answer-sheet discipline card example** (stick it on your desk):

1. Blacken fully. 2. Transfer answers every 10 questions. 3. Check numbers every 10 questions. 4. Block D: transfer the flagged ones.

## Practice

1. What three things distinguish Real mode from Study mode?
2. Name at least five H-1 checklist items.
3. What is the answer-sheet discipline? Why not per question?
4. What's the listening rule if you miss one question? Why?
5. Write your own mock-day timeline (wake up → done → review).

### Answer key

1. No pauses between sections (besides the official break), no explanations, no notes/phone.
2. 7–8 hours of sleep; stationery + ID + watch; phone off; location ready; eat enough.
3. Blacken fully, transfer per block (±10 questions), check numbers every 10 questions; per-question transfer wastes back-and-forth time.
4. Let it go and focus on the next — chasing it can sink five other questions.
5. Correct example: wake up, set up desk, session 1 (105'), break without notes, session 2 (50'), rest before review.

## Tips / key patterns

- Do it at the same hour as your real exam — a body used to "performing" in the morning beats a brain used to studying at night.
- Don't do two Real modes in one week; ideally 10–14 days between full mocks.
- Record Real and Study scores separately; the gap shows your dependence on the safety net.
- If your Real-mode score is far below Study (>20 points), the problem is usually time management and panic — not knowledge.

## Summary

- Real mode = 155 minutes with no safety net; official break only between sessions.
- H-1: enough sleep, stationery, ID, watch, phone off, location ready.
- Answer sheet: blacken fully, transfer per block, check numbers every 10 questions.
- Listening played once (missed = let go); panic → 10 seconds of breathing → easiest question.
