## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can state that something bad "could happen" with the N2 pattern 〜かねない. You can distinguish it from the N3-level 〜かねる meaning "hard to do", and from 〜おそれがある in the previous lesson — three patterns often confused in exam questions.

## Explanation

**〜かねない (pattern 17).** The formula: ます-stem verb (ます form minus ます) + かねない. Means "could…" — something bad might happen, with a strong negative connotation.

- 誤解を 招きかねない。(It could cause misunderstanding.)

Watch out, don't confuse it with the N3-level 〜かねる meaning "hard to do" (理解しかねる = hard to understand). Remember the pair: かねる = hard, かねない = could (bad).

Then what's the difference from 〜おそれがある (lesson 6)? Both express risk, but 〜おそれがある is objective like a report, while 〜かねない is a subjective warning — "watch out, this could happen".

The five kanji in this lesson: 賠 (ばい = to compensate, e.g. 賠償), 徴 (ちょう = to collect, e.g. 徴収), 譲 (じょう = to transfer, e.g. 譲渡), 兌 (だ = to exchange, e.g. 兌換), 配 (はい = to distribute, e.g. 配当).

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・である体〕 軽い 失言が 信用の 失墜を 招きかねない。(Even a light slip of the tongue could bring down credibility.)
2. 〔文語・である体〕 対応を 誤れば、炎上を 招きかねない。(If mishandled, it could invite mass backlash.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 情報の 漏洩は 損害賠償に 発展しかねません。(An information leak could develop into a compensation claim.)
4. 〔文語・である体〕 無理な 拡張は 経営の 破綻を 招きかねない。(Forced expansion could invite business collapse.)
5. 〔話し言葉〕 そんな 言い方を したら、相手を 怒らせかねないよ。(If you talk like that, you could make the other person angry.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 納期の 遅れは 契約の 解除を 招きかねない。(A delay in delivery could lead to contract cancellation.)
7. 〔文語・である体〕 風評が 広がれば、倒産に 追い込まれかねない。(If rumors spread, the company could be driven into bankruptcy.)

## Practice

1. Choose the correct one: 誤解を (招きかねない / 招きかねる)。
2. Fill in with the correct pattern: 軽い 失言が 炎上を ___。
3. Form 〜かねない from: 失う → ______
4. Distinguish the meanings: 理解しかねる means ______, 誤解を招きかねない means ______. (hard to understand / could cause misunderstanding)
5. Translate into Japanese: "Could lose trust." → ______
6. True or false: 「成功しかねない」→ ______ (true/false, explain)
7. Choose the one with a subjective warning tone: (〜おそれがある / 〜かねない)。

### Answer key

1. 招きかねない
2. 招きかねない
3. 失いかねない
4. 理解しかねる means hard to understand, 誤解を招きかねない means could cause misunderstanding
5. 信用を 失いかねない。
6. false — 〜かねない is almost always for bad things, not good news
7. 〜かねない

## Tips / key patterns

- The formula uses the ます stem: 招き + かねない. Not the dictionary form (×招くかねない).
- Memorize the opposing pair: かねる (N3) = hard to do; かねない (N2) = could happen (bad). Exam questions love to pit them against each other.

## Summary

- 〜かねない (ます stem +): "could happen" — risk with a negative connotation and subjective warning tone.
- Different from 〜かねる (N3): hard to do. Different from 〜おそれがある: more objective and formal.
- Always for bad things; the formula uses the ます stem.
- Kanji: 賠 (to compensate), 徴 (to collect), 譲 (to transfer), 兌 (to exchange), 配 (to distribute).
