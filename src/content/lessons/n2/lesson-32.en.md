## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use two newspaper-style "it is said" expressions: 〜模様だ for news concluded from observation, and 〜とのことだ for news received from a source. You can distinguish their nuances and will master 7 new kanji.

## Explanation

**1. 〜模様だ — news from observation.** Pattern: V-ている + 模様だ, or N + の + 模様だ. The nuance: the speaker CONCLUDES from what is seen or observed — "it seems / reportedly". The source is the speaker's own eyes (or data).

- 検討している模様だ (it seems to be under consideration)
- 霖雨の模様だ (it seems to be prolonged rain)

**2. 〜とのことだ — news from a source.** Pattern: plain-form sentence + とのことだ. The nuance: "according to information received" — there is ANOTHER PARTY giving the news, and the speaker is merely passing it on.

- 延期されるとのことだ (according to reports, it will be postponed)
- けがをしているとのことだ (according to reports, he is injured)

**3. Distinguishing the three.** 〜模様だ = your own observation-based conclusion; 〜とのことだ = relaying someone else's information; 〜そうだ (N4) = an immediate impression on the spot. Don't use 模様だ for your own condition: わたしは 病気の模様だ is a wrong sentence.

The seven kanji of this lesson: 霖 (りん = long rain, e.g. 霖雨), 凪 (なぎ = calm), 汐 (しお = tide), 浪 (なみ = wave, e.g. 波浪), 箏 (こと = koto), 雅 (みやび = elegant, e.g. 雅楽), 様 (よう = appearance, e.g. 模様).

## Example sentences

1. 〔新聞〕 政府は 新制度を 検討している模様だ。(The government appears to be considering a new system.)
2. 〔新聞〕 会議は 延期されるとのことだ。(According to reports, the meeting will be postponed.)
3. 〔新聞〕 台風は あす 上陸する模様だ。(The typhoon appears to make landfall tomorrow.)
4. 〔新聞〕 被害者は けがをしているとのことだ。(According to reports, the victim is injured.)
5. 〔新聞〕 霖雨が 週末まで 続く模様だ。(The prolonged rain appears to continue until the weekend.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 部長は あす 出張するとのことだ。(According to reports, the manager will be on a business trip tomorrow.)
7. 〔新聞〕 凪の 海で 祭りが 開かれる模様だ。(The festival appears to be held on a calm sea.)

## Practice

1. Choose the correct one (the journalist is looking at the study documents): 新制度を 検討している (模様だ / とのことだ)。
2. Choose the correct one (info from PR): 会議は 延期される (模様だ / とのことだ)。
3. Turn into a headline: 台風が あす 近づいている → ______
4. Translate: "According to reports, the festival is postponed." → ______
5. Explain the difference: when to use 模様だ and when to use とのことだ?
6. Fill in the blank: 霖雨が 続く______。

### Answer key

1. 模様だ
2. とのことだ
3. 台風、あす近づいている模様だ
4. 祭りは 延期されるとのことだ。
5. 模様だ = a conclusion from one's own observation; とのことだ = relaying information from another party
6. 模様だ

## Tips / key patterns

- 模様だ attaches to ている (a process OBSERVED in progress); とのことだ attaches to a complete plain-form sentence.
- Negative forms: 〜していない模様だ (it seems not to be...), 〜ないとのことだ.
- JLPT trap: 〜そうだ (an immediate impression, e.g. 雨が 降りそうだ) vs 〜模様だ (a conclusion from data/observation).
- In TV news, とのことだ is often read quickly as とのことです — same meaning.

## Summary

- 〜模様だ: "it seems/reportedly" from one's own observation (V-ている + 模様だ).
- 〜とのことだ: "according to reports" from another source (plain sentence + とのことだ).
- Don't use 模様だ for your own condition.
- Kanji: 霖 (long rain), 凪 (calm), 汐 (tide), 浪 (wave), 箏 (koto), 雅 (elegant), 様 (appearance).
