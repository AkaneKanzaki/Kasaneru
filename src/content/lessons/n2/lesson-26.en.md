## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use ご〜いただく — level II humble language — to express "receiving a favor from the other person" in formal business Japanese. You also understand the direction of respect: who humbles themselves and who is elevated, so you don't get it backwards.

## Explanation

**1. Form.** ご + ます form without ます of a kango verb + いただく.

- 指導する → ご指導いただく, 連絡する → ご連絡いただく, 理解する → ご理解いただく
- Conjugation: ご指導いただきます・いただきました・いただけます

**2. Meaning and direction of respect.** This pattern is used when the speaker receives a favor from the other person. The speaker humbles themselves (the act of "receiving" is made humble through いただく), and the other person is elevated (their act is wrapped in ご).

- 先生にご指導いただきました。(I received guidance from my teacher — I humble myself, the teacher is elevated.)

Don't get it backwards: this pattern is never used for the other person's actions. ×先生がご指導いただきました is a fatal direction error.

**3. Different from 〜ていただく (N4).** In N4 you learned 教えていただく — that is for wago verbs (native Japanese). ご〜いただく is its more formal kango version, specifically for Sino-Japanese verbs like 指導・連絡・理解・確認. The rule: kango words → ご〜いただく; wago words → 〜ていただく.

**4. This lesson's kanji (4).** 啓 (けい = to open, e.g. 謹啓・拝啓), 謹 (きん = reverent, e.g. 謹啓), 粛 (しゅく = solemn quiet, e.g. 自粛・厳粛), 凛 (りん = sternly dignified, e.g. 凛々しい).

## Example sentences

1. 〔ビジネス〕 先生にご指導いただきました。(I received guidance from my teacher.)
2. 〔ビジネス〕 ご連絡いただき、ありがとうございます。(Thank you for the news you gave me.)
3. 〔ビジネス〕 ご理解いただきたく存じます。(I humbly ask for your understanding.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 資料をご確認いただけますようお願いします。(Please confirm the documents.)
5. 〔ビジネス〕 ご返信をお待ちしております。(I am awaiting your reply.)
6. 〔ビジネス〕 社長にご説明いただきました。(I received an explanation from the company president.)
7. 〔ビジネス〕 ご利用いただき、誠にありがとうございます。(Thank you very much for using our services.)

## Practice

1. Change to the ご〜いただく pattern: 指導する → ______
2. Who humbles themselves in 先生にご指導いただきました? → ______
3. Choose the correct one: (a) 先生がご指導いただきました (b) 先生にご指導いただきました.
4. Translate into Japanese: "Thank you for the news." → ______
5. The correct pair: kango word 確認 → ______いただく; wago word 教える → ______いただく.
6. Correct or wrong: ご〜いただく can be used for a superior's actions. → ______

### Answer key

1. ご指導いただく
2. The speaker (I) humbles themselves; the teacher is elevated.
3. (b)
4. ご連絡いただき、ありがとうございます。
5. ご確認いただく; 教えていただく
6. Wrong — ご〜いただく is for the speaker's own act of "receiving", not for a superior's actions.

## Tips / key patterns

- The direction of respect is the soul of this pattern: I receive (humble) ← you give (elevated). Get it backwards once and the meaning breaks.
- Only for kango verbs (指導・連絡・理解・確認・返信・説明); wago words stay with 〜ていただく.
- In business email, ご〜いただき、ありがとうございます is the standard thank-you sentence.
- Don't double the humility: ×ご指導していただく.

## Summary

- Form: ご + ます-stem of a kango verb + いただく (謙譲語II).
- Meaning: "receiving a favor from you" — the speaker humbles themselves, the other person is elevated.
- Specifically for kango words; the wago equivalent is 〜ていただく (N4).
- Business email key: ご連絡いただき、ありがとうございます.
- Kanji: 啓 (to open), 謹 (reverent), 粛 (solemn), 凛 (dignified).
