## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you can use 〜かたわら to express two activities running side by side ("alongside … also …"), including its contrastive nuance that can be translated as "although". You can also distinguish it from 〜ながら (N4) so you don't mix up the register.

## Explanation

**1. Form.** N + の + かたわら + clause. Example: 研究のかたわら、アルバイトをする. The noun before かたわら is the main activity, while the clause after it is the additional activity.

**2. Meaning 1 — parallel: "alongside".** Two activities run side by side over a long period; the subject of both clauses is usually the same.

- 彼は研究のかたわら、大学で教えている。(Alongside his research, he teaches at a university.)

**3. Meaning 2 — contrast: "although".** When the paired activities are opposites, a contrastive nuance appears that can be translated as "although" or "while on the other hand".

- 成功を収めたかたわら、健康を損なった。(Although he achieved success, his health was ruined.)

Literally the meaning is still "alongside / together with", but the contrastive context makes "although" the most fitting translation.

**4. Different from 〜ながら (N4).** 〜ながら is used for two momentary actions done simultaneously: 音楽を聞きながら歩く (to walk while listening to music). 〜かたわら is used for long-term parallel activities, and its register is formal written — in casual conversation it sounds stiff.

**5. This lesson's kanji (11).** 髄 (ずい = marrow, e.g. 骨髄), 腱 (けん = tendon), 腑 (ふ = innards, e.g. 腑に落ちる), 腔 (くう = cavity, e.g. 口腔), 膏 (こう = ointment, e.g. 膏薬), 齦 (ぎん = gums, e.g. 歯齦), 齲 (う = cavity, e.g. 齲歯), 歯 (は = tooth), 髭 (ひげ = mustache), 鬚 (ひげ = beard), 狡 (ずるい = cunning).

## Example sentences

1. 〔文語・である体〕 ブディ氏は研究のかたわら、ジャカルタの大学で教えている。(Alongside his research, Budi teaches at a university in Jakarta.)
2. 〔話し言葉〕 シンタさんは仕事のかたわら、夜は日本語を勉強している。(Alongside her work, Sinta studies Japanese at night.)
3. 〔文語・である体〕 彼は事業で成功を収めたかたわら、健康を大きく損なった。(Although he succeeded in business, his health was severely damaged.)
4. 〔ビジネス〕 営業のかたわら、新規事業の企画も担当しております。(Alongside sales, I also handle planning for new businesses.)
5. 〔文語・である体〕 母は家事のかたわら、地域のボランティアを続けている。(Alongside household chores, my mother continues volunteering in her neighborhood.)
6. 〔話し言葉〕 彼は医者のかたわら、小説も書いているんだ。(He, alongside being a doctor, also writes novels.)

## Tips / key patterns

- Always N + の + かたわら; it cannot attach directly to a verb (×勉強するかたわら).
- The contrastive meaning ("although") is born from the opposed pair, not from a separate word.
- Formal written register — good for essays, profiles, and reports; avoid it in casual chat.
- The subject of both clauses is usually the same person.

## Summary

- Form: N + の + かたわら.
- Meaning 1: long-term parallel ("alongside … also …").
- Meaning 2: contrastive nuance ("although …").
- Different from 〜ながら, which is for momentary simultaneous actions.
- Kanji: 髄 (marrow), 腱 (tendon), 腑 (innards), 腔 (cavity), 膏 (ointment), 齦 (gums), 齲 (cavity), 歯 (tooth), 髭 (mustache), 鬚 (beard), 狡 (cunning).
