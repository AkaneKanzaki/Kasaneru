## Lesson goals

After this lesson, you will be able to use Study mode for your first full N2 simulation (155 minutes): time allocation, strategy per section, how to use the answer explanations, and recording error patterns. The goal is an honest ability map for L47 — not a high score.

## Explanation

**Study mode** = full simulation in learning conditions: pausing BETWEEN sections is allowed, viewing explanations after answering is allowed, timer as a reference. The goal is to MAP your ability, not to prove yourself.

**Official N2 time (155 minutes):** session 1 (Language Knowledge + Reading) 105', session 2 (Listening) 50'. Split session 1: vocab 20' + grammar 25' + reading 50' + buffer 10'. Record the ACTUAL time per section.

**Score target (ESTIMATE).** N2 pass benchmark: total ≥90/180, each section above the threshold — all ESTIMATES, not official JLPT scores. Realistic first-mock target: estimated total ≥70/180, no empty sections. The score may be low; the data must be complete.

**Using the explanations.** Read them for WRONG and CORRECT-BUT-UNSURE questions. Each problem question → one log line: section | question type | cause (target ≥15 lines). Example: "Reading | 主張理解 | choosing a detail as the main idea".

## Example sentences

Strategy per section:

**Session 1 (105').** Vocabulary (20'): start with what you know best; ≤60 seconds/question; stuck → flag, move on. Grammar (25'): read the sentence to the END before looking at options. Reading (50'): short texts first (L41–L43); long texts last.

**Session 2 (50').** L44 protocol: play once, 4K notes, read options before the audio. A 10-minute break before session 2 to calm down — not to open notes.

### Answer key

1. Session 1 (Language Knowledge + Reading) 105', session 2 (Listening) 50'; total 155'. Session 1 split: vocab 20' + grammar 25' + reading 50' + buffer 10'.
2. Study mode allows pauses between sections and viewing explanations; Real mode allows neither.
3. Correct-but-unsure questions — "correct by guessing" can fail on the real exam.
4. Estimated total ≥70/180, no empty sections. App scores are ESTIMATES (statistical scaling), not official JLPT scores.
5. Format: section | question type | cause. Example: "Grammar | 〜ざるを得ない | confused with 〜ないではいられない".

## Tips / key patterns

- A good first mock produces a list of fixes, not a number. Score 65 + 20 log lines > score 85 with no notes.
- Don't take the same mock twice — memorized questions ruin the measurement.
- Breaks between sections are allowed in Study mode, but record their length so the comparison with Real mode is honest.
- Reading explanations without writing actions = entertainment, not studying.

## Summary

- Study mode = full simulation to map ability; pauses between sections and viewing explanations are allowed.
- N2 time: session 1 = 105' (20+25+50+10), session 2 = 50'; total 155 minutes.
- First-mock target: estimated total ≥70/180, ≥15 log lines (including correct-but-unsure).
- App scores are always ESTIMATES, not official JLPT scores.
