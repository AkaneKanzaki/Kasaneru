# Privacy Policy — Kasaneru

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Kasaneru is an offline-first, open-source Japanese learning application.
This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## The short version

**We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**
Everything you do in Kasaneru stays on your device.

## Data we do NOT collect

- No account is required. We do not ask for or collect your name, email
  address, or any contact details.
- No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or tracking
  technologies of any kind.
- No cookies.

## Data stored on your device

To function, Kasaneru stores the following **only on your device**
(in the app's local storage):

- Learning progress: completed lessons, kana practice, flashcard reviews,
  exam history, XP, and streaks.
- Profile: language, theme, display name, and target level.

This data never leaves your device unless **you** choose to export a backup
file (Profile → Export backup). The backup file goes wherever you decide
to save or send it — we never receive a copy.

## Your rights

Because your data stays with you, you are always in control:

- **Access and portability:** view your progress directly in the app, or
  export it at any time via Profile → Export backup.
- **Deletion:** Profile → Reset all data permanently deletes all local
  data on the device.

These controls are available to every user, consistent with data-subject
rights under the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Brazil's LGPD, and
Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 of 2022).

## Children

Kasaneru does not collect personal data from anyone, including children
under 13. The app is a general-audience educational tool.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be
noted in the app's release notes.
